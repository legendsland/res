In his early philosophy, the traktatus, he had a view very oriented towards formalism and logic, limited to considerations about language, and a later philosophy that turned much more towards humanity, specifically everyday humanity as a whole. In doing so, he also dismantled his earlier perspective piece by piece, going into detail. This is rare among philosophers, rare among thinkers in general. In his work traktatus logiko philosophikus, Wittgenstein definitely wants to clarify how thinking relates to the world.

他相信，通过分析语言可以找到通往现实的钥匙。哲学家探索了两千五百年之后，世界与思想在语言中相遇。词语如何表达现实？我们如何借助词语表象并理解世界？对维特根斯坦而言，每一个命题都是现实的模型，就像我们可以用模型直观地重现一场交通事故。

He believes he has found the key to reality in the analysis of language. After philosophers have been searching in vain for 2500 years, the world and thought meet in language. How can words express reality? How can we represent and understand the world with words? For Wittgenstein, every sentence is a model of reality. With a model, we represent reality vividly, for example, a traffic accident.

玩偶和玩具汽车代表现实中的对象，人们可以借它们重建事故：一辆红车从这里驶向某一点，一辆绿色车辆从右侧接近；红车继续前进，绿车也继续前进，最终发生碰撞。一则关于法庭重建交通事故的报道给维特根斯坦带来了关键启发。

Dolls and toy cars stand for reality; with them, one can reconstruct the accident. A red car drives from down here towards this point. 语言的界限

Natural sciences and technology give Wittgenstein examples of how to describe the world rationally and unambiguously without the ambiguity of everyday language.

初等数论中的一个不可解问题1

AN UNSOLVABLE PROBLEM OF ELEMENTARY NUMBER THEORY.1

阿隆佐·丘奇

By Alonzo Church.

1. 引言

1. Introduction.

初等数论中有这样一类问题：要求找到一个以 n 个正整数为自变量的有效可计算函数 f，使得 f(x1,x2,…,xn)=2 是某个以 x1,x2,…,xn 为自由变量的初等数论命题为真的充要条件。2

There is a class of problems of elementary number theory which can be stated in the form that it is required to find an effectively calculable function f of n positive integers, such that f(x1,x2,…,xn)=2 is a necessary and sufficient condition for the truth of a certain proposition of elementary number theory involving x1,x2,…,xn as free variables.2

这类问题的一个例子，是寻找一种方法，判定对于任意给定的正整数 n，是否存在正整数 x、y、z，使 xn+yn=zn。因为这可以解释为：要求找到一个有效可计算函数 f，使得当且仅当存在满足 xn+yn=zn 的正整数 x、y、z 时，f(n) 等于 2。显然，函数 f 必须有效可计算，是这个问题的实质性要求；没有这一要求，问题就变得平凡了。

An example of such a problem is the problem to find a means of determining of any given positive integer n whether or not there exist positive integers x, y, z, such that xn+yn=zn. For this may be interpreted, required to find an effectively calculable function f, such that f(n) is equal to 2 if and only if there exist positive integers x, y, z, such that xn+yn=zn. Clearly the condition that the function f be effectively calculable is an essential part of the problem, since without it the problem becomes trivial.

这类问题的另一个例子，是拓扑学中寻找闭三维单纯流形在同胚下的一组完备的、有效可计算的不变量的问题。由于拓扑复形可以用关联矩阵表示，这个问题可以解释为初等数论问题。事实上，众所周知，一组关联矩阵表示闭三维流形这一性质，以及两组关联矩阵表示同胚复形这一性质，都可以用纯粹的数论术语描述。如果用一种直接的方式枚举表示闭三维流形的各组关联矩阵，就能立即证明，所讨论的问题，即寻找闭三维流形的一组完备的、有效可计算的不变量，等价于寻找一个以正整数为自变量的有效可计算函数 f，使得当且仅当枚举中的第 m 组和第 n 组关联矩阵表示同胚复形时，f(m,n) 等于 2。

Another example of a problem of this class is, for instance, the problem of topology, to find a complete set of effectively calculable invariants of closed three-dimensional simplicial manifolds under homeomorphisms. This problem can be interpreted as a problem of elementary number theory in view of the fact that topological complexes are representable by matrices of incidence. In fact, as is well known, the property of a set of incidence matrices that it represent a closed three-dimensional manifold, and the property of two sets of incidence matrices that they represent homeomorphic complexes, can both be described in purely number-theoretic terms. If we enumerate, in a straightforward way, the sets of incidence matrices which represent closed three-dimensional manifolds, it will then be immediately provable that the problem under consideration (to find a complete set of effectively calculable invariants of closed three-dimensional manifolds) is equivalent to the problem, to find an effectively calculable function f of positive integers, such that f(m,n) is equal to 2 if and only if the m-th set of incidence matrices and the n-th set of incidence matrices in the enumeration represent homeomorphic complexes.

读者很容易想到其他例子。

Other examples will readily occur to the reader.

本文的目的是提出一个有效可计算性的定义。我们认为，这个定义能令人满意地对应于人们在陈述这类问题时常用的、略显模糊的直观概念；同时，我们将通过一个例子说明，这一类问题并非全都可解。3

The purpose of the present paper is to propose a definition of effective calculability3 which is thought to correspond satisfactorily to the somewhat vague intuitive notion in terms of which problems of this class are often stated, and to show, by means of an example, that not every problem of this class is solvable.

2. 转换与 λ 可定义性

2. Conversion and λ-definability.

我们选定一个符号表，其中包含符号 {、}、(、)、λ、[、]，以及一个可数无穷的符号集 a、b、c、……，这些符号称为变量。我们把由这个符号表中的符号组成的任意有限序列称为公式。然后用归纳法定义合式公式、自由变量和约束变量：单独一个变量 x 是合式公式，其中 x 的这次出现是自由出现；若公式 F 和 X 合式，则 {F}(X) 合式，而且 x 在 F 或 X 中的自由出现或约束出现，在 {F}(X) 中仍分别是自由出现或约束出现；若公式 M 合式，而且 M 中有 x 的自由出现，则 λx[M] 合式，x 在其中的每次出现都是约束出现，而异于 x 的变量 y 在 M 中的自由出现或约束出现，在 λx[M] 中仍分别是自由出现或约束出现。

We select a particular list of symbols, consisting of the symbols {, }, (, ), λ, [, ], and an enumerably infinite set of symbols a, b, c, … to be called variables. And we define the word formula to mean any finite sequence of symbols out of this list. The terms well-formed formula, free variable, and bound variable are then defined by induction as follows. A variable x standing alone is a well-formed formula and the occurrence of x in it is an occurrence of x as a free variable in it; if the formulas F and X are well-formed, {F}(X) is well-formed, and an occurrence of x as a free (bound) variable in F or X is an occurrence of x as a free (bound) variable in {F}(X); if the formula M is well-formed and contains an occurrence of x as a free variable in M, then λx[M] is well-formed, any occurrence of x in λx[M] is an occurrence of x as a bound variable in λx[M], and an occurrence of a variable y, other than x, as a free (bound) variable in M is an occurrence of y as a free (bound) variable in λx[M].

我们用粗体字母表示可变的或尚未确定的公式。并约定：除非另有说明，每个粗体字母都代表一个合式公式；每个独立写出、含有粗体字母的符号组，也都代表一个合式公式。

We shall use heavy type letters to stand for variable or undetermined formulas. And we adopt the convention that, unless otherwise stated, each heavy type letter shall represent a well-formed formula and each set of symbols standing apart which contains a heavy type letter shall represent a well-formed formula.

书写具体的合式公式时，我们采用下列缩写。如果 F 是一个符号，或用一个符号表示，则公式 {F}(X) 可以缩写为 F(X)。公式 {{F}(X)}(Y) 可以缩写为 {F}(X,Y)；若 F 是一个符号，或用一个符号表示，也可缩写为 F(X,Y)。类似地，{{{F}(X)}(Y)}(Z) 可以缩写为 {F}(X,Y,Z) 或 F(X,Y,Z)，依此类推。公式 λx1[λx2[⋯λxn[M]⋯]] 可以缩写为 λx1x2⋯xn·M，或 λx1x2⋯xnM。

When writing particular well-formed formulas, we adopt the following abbreviations. A formula {F}(X) may be abbreviated as F(X) in any case where F is or is represented by a single symbol. A formula {{F}(X)}(Y) may be abbreviated as {F}(X,Y), or, if F is or is represented by a single symbol, as F(X,Y). And {{{F}(X)}(Y)}(Z) may be abbreviated as {F}(X,Y,Z), or as F(X,Y,Z), and so on. A formula λx1[λx2[⋯λxn[M]⋯]] may be abbreviated as λx1x2⋯xn·M or as λx1x2⋯xnM.

我们还允许随时引入这样的缩写：用一个特定符号 α 代表一个特定符号序列 A。引入这种缩写时，记作 α→A，读作“α 代表 A”。

We also allow ourselves at any time to introduce abbreviations of the form that a particular symbol α shall stand for a particular sequence of symbols A, and indicate the introduction of such an abbreviation by the notation α→A, to be read, “α stands for A.”

我们立即引入下列无穷多个缩写：

We introduce at once the following infinite list of abbreviations,

1→λab·a(b),2→λab·a(a(b)),3→λab·a(a(a(b))),

依此类推，每个用阿拉伯数字写出的正整数，都代表一个形如 λab·a(a(⋯a(b)⋯)) 的公式。

and so on, each positive integer in Arabic notation standing for a formula of the form λab·a(a(⋯a(b)⋯)).

表达式 SNxM| 用来表示把 M 中所有 x 都替换为 N 所得的结果。

The expression SNxM| is used to stand for the result of substituting N for x throughout M.

我们考虑对合式公式的以下三种操作：

We consider the three following operations on well-formed formulas:

  1. 把公式中任意一部分 λx[M] 替换为 λy[SyxM|]，其中 y 是不在 M 中出现的变量。

    To replace any part λx[M] of a formula by λy[SyxM|], where y is a variable which does not occur in M.

  2. 把公式中任意一部分 {λx[M]}(N) 替换为 SNxM|，条件是 M 中的约束变量既不同于 x，也不同于 N 中的自由变量。

    To replace any part {λx[M]}(N) of a formula by SNxM|, provided that the bound variables in M are distinct both from x and from the free variables in N.

  3. 把公式中任意一部分 SNxM|，只要它不是紧跟在 λ 后面，替换为 {λx[M]}(N)，条件是 M 中的约束变量既不同于 x，也不同于 N 中的自由变量。

    To replace any part SNxM| (not immediately following λ) of a formula by {λx[M]}(N), provided that the bound variables in M are distinct both from x and from the free variables in N.

这些操作的任意有限序列称为一次转换。如果 B 能由 A 经过一次转换得到，就说 A 可转换为 B，或记作“A conv B”。如果 B 与 A 相同，或者能由 A 通过操作 I、II、III 中的某一个操作应用一次得到，就说 A 可直接转换为 B。

Any finite sequence of these operations is called a conversion, and if B is obtainable from A by a conversion we say that A is convertible into B, or, “A conv B.” If B is identical with A or is obtainable from A by a single application of one of the operations I, II, III, we say that A is immediately convertible into B.

恰好包含一次操作 II、而不包含操作 III 的转换，称为一次归约。

A conversion which contains exactly one application of Operation II, and no application of Operation III, is called a reduction.

如果一个公式合式，而且不含形如 {λx[M]}(N) 的部分，就说它处于正规形。如果 B 处于正规形，而且 A conv B，就说 B 是 A 的一个正规形。

A formula is said to be in normal form, if it is well-formed and contains no part of the form {λx[M]}(N). And B is said to be a normal form of A if B is in normal form and A conv B.

变量原先给定的顺序 a、b、c、……称为它们的自然顺序。如果一个公式处于正规形，其中没有变量同时作为自由变量和约束变量出现，而且按公式中的出现顺序，紧跟在 λ 后面的各变量从 a 开始、无重复地按自然顺序排列，只跳过那些在公式中作为自由变量出现的变量，就说该公式处于主正规形。4 如果 B 处于主正规形，而且 A conv B，就说 B 是 A 的主正规形。

The originally given order a, b, c, … of the variables is called their natural order. And a formula is said to be in principal normal form if it is in normal form, and no variable occurs in it both as a free variable and as a bound variable, and the variables which occur in it immediately following the symbol λ are, when taken in the order in which they occur in the formula, in natural order without repetitions, beginning with a and omitting only such variables as occur in the formula as free variables.4 The formula B is said to be the principal normal form of A if B is in principal normal form and A conv B.

下面三个定理中，第一个的证明是直接的，第二个和第三个已经由作者与 J. B. Rosser 证明。5

Of the three following theorems, proof of the first is immediate, and the second and third have been proved by the present author and J. B. Rosser:5

定理 I。如果一个公式处于正规形，就不能再对它进行归约。

THEOREM I. If a formula is in normal form, no reduction of it is possible.

定理 II。如果一个公式有正规形，则这个正规形在允许应用操作 I 的意义下是唯一的，而且该公式的任何归约序列，只要继续进行，都必定终止于该正规形。

THEOREM II. If a formula has a normal form, this normal form is unique to within applications of Operation I, and any sequence of reductions of the formula must (if continued) terminate in the normal form.

定理 III。如果一个公式有正规形，则它的每个合式部分都有正规形。

THEOREM III. If a formula has a normal form, every well-formed part of it has a normal form.

如果一个函数的每个自变量的取值范围都是正整数类，而且因变量的取值范围包含于正整数类，就把它称为正整数函数。需要指明自变量个数时，我们就说一元正整数函数、二元正整数函数，依此类推。因此，如果 F 是 n 元正整数函数，而 a1,a2,…,an 是正整数，则 F(a1,a2,…,an) 必须是正整数。

We shall call a function a function of positive integers if the range of each independent variable is the class of positive integers and the range of the dependent variable is contained in the class of positive integers. And when it is desired to indicate the number of independent variables we shall speak of a function of one positive integer, a function of two positive integers, and so on. Thus if F is a function of n positive integers, and a1,a2,…,an are positive integers, then F(a1,a2,…,an) must be a positive integer.

如果能找到一个公式 F，使得只要 F(m)=r，就有 {F}(m)convr，便称一元正整数函数 F 是 λ 可定义的。这里粗体 m 和 r，是按前面引入的缩写，由用阿拉伯数字写出的正整数 m 和 r 所代表的公式。

A function F of one positive integer is said to be λ-definable if it is possible to find a formula F such that, if F(m)=r and m and r are the formulas for which the positive integers m and r (written in Arabic notation) stand according to our abbreviations introduced above, then {F}(m)convr.

类似地，如果能找到一个公式 F，使得只要 F(m,n)=r，公式 {F}(m,n) 就可转换为粗体 r，便称二元正整数函数 F 是 λ 可定义的。这里 m、n、r 是正整数，而粗体 m、n、r 是相应的公式。三元或更多元的正整数函数，也作同样定义。6

Similarly, a function F of two positive integers is said to be λ-definable if it is possible to find a formula F such that, whenever F(m,n)=r, the formula {F}(m,n) is convertible into r (m, n, r being positive integers and m, n, r the corresponding formulas). And so on for functions of three or more positive integers.6

显然，对于任何 λ 可定义的正整数函数，把公式归约到正规形的过程，都给出了有效计算该函数各个具体值的算法。

It is clear that, in the case of any λ-definable function of positive integers, the process of reduction of formulas to normal form provides an algorithm for the effective calculation of particular values of the function.

3. 公式的哥德尔表示

3. The Gödel representation of a formula.

我们把哥德尔提出的一种方法应用于刚才描述的形式记法，7 按如下方式给每个公式关联一个代表它的正整数：让符号 {、(、[ 各自对应数 11，让符号 }、)、] 各自对应数 13，让符号 λ 对应数 1，并让变量 a、b、c、……依次对应素数 17、19、23、……。对于依次由 n 个符号 τ1,τ2,…,τn 组成的公式，关联数 2t13t2⋯pntn，其中 ti 是符号 τi 对应的数，而 pn 表示第 n 个素数。

Adapting to the formal notation just described a device which is due to Gödel,7 we associate with every formula a positive integer to represent it, as follows. To each of the symbols {, (, [ we let correspond the number 11, to each of the symbols }, ), ] the number 13, to the symbol λ the number 1, and to the variables a, b, c, … the prime numbers 17, 19, 23, … respectively. And with a formula which is composed of the n symbols τ1,τ2,…,τn in order we associate the number 2t13t2⋯pntn, where ti is the number corresponding to the symbol τi, and where pn stands for the n-th prime number.

数 2t13t2⋯pntn 称为公式 τ1τ2⋯τn 的哥德尔表示。

This number 2t13t2⋯pntn will be called the Gödel representation of the formula τ1τ2⋯τn.

两个不同的公式有时可能具有相同的哥德尔表示，因为 11 和 13 各自都对应三种不同符号；但很容易证明，两个不同的合式公式不可能具有相同的哥德尔表示。而且显然，给定任意公式，可以用一种有效方法计算其哥德尔表示；同样，给定任意正整数，也可以用一种有效方法判定它是否是某个合式公式的哥德尔表示，如果是，还可以得到该公式。

Two distinct formulas may sometimes have the same Gödel representation, because the numbers 11 and 13 each correspond to three different symbols, but it is readily proved that no two distinct well-formed formulas can have the same Gödel representation. It is clear, moreover, that there is an effective method by which, given any formula, its Gödel representation can be calculated; and likewise that there is an effective method by which, given any positive integer, it is possible to determine whether it is the Gödel representation of a well-formed formula and, if it is, to obtain that formula.

在这里，哥德尔表示所起的作用，类似于组合拓扑中的关联矩阵，参见上文第 1 节。因为在合式公式理论中，有一类重要问题，其中每个问题都等价于一个可借助哥德尔表示得到的初等数论问题。8

In this connection the Gödel representation plays a rôle similar to that of the matrix of incidence in combinatorial topology (cf. § 1 above). For there is, in the theory of well-formed formulas, an important class of problems, each of which is equivalent to a problem of elementary number theory obtainable by means of the Gödel representation.8

4. 递归函数

4. Recursive functions.

我们定义一类称为初等表达式的表达式。除圆括号和逗号外，它们还涉及符号 1、S，一个无穷的数值变量集 x、y、z、……，以及对于每个正整数 n，一个带下标 n 的无穷函数变量集 fn,gn,hn,…。定义按如下归纳方式给出：单独的符号 1 或任意数值变量是初等表达式；若 A 是初等表达式，则 S(A) 是初等表达式；若 A1,A2,…,An 是初等表达式，而 fn 是任意带下标 n 的函数变量，则 fn(A1,A2,…,An) 是初等表达式。

We define a class of expressions, which we shall call elementary expressions, and which involve, besides parentheses and commas, the symbols 1, S, an infinite set of numerical variables x, y, z, … and, for each positive integer n, an infinite set fn,gn,hn,… of functional variables with subscript n. This definition is by induction as follows. The symbol 1 or any numerical variable, standing alone, is an elementary expression. If A is an elementary expression, then S(A) is an elementary expression. If A1,A2,…,An are elementary expressions and fn is any functional variable with subscript n, then fn(A1,A2,…,An) is an elementary expression.

特定的初等表达式 1、S(1)、S(S(1))、……称为数码。正整数 1、2、3、……分别对应于数码 1、S(1)、S(S(1))、……。

The particular elementary expressions 1, S(1), S(S(1)), … are called numerals. And the positive integers 1, 2, 3, … are said to correspond to the numerals 1, S(1), S(S(1)), … .

形如 A=B 的表达式，其中 A 和 B 是初等表达式，称为初等方程。

An expression of the form A =B, where A and B are elementary expressions, is called an elementary equation.

一组初等方程 E 的导出方程按如下归纳方式定义：E 本身的方程都是导出方程；如果 A=B 是一个含有数值变量 x 的导出方程，则把 A=B 中所有 x 都替换为某个特定数码，所得结果也是导出方程；如果 A=B 是一个含有初等表达式 C 的导出方程，C 可以是 A 或 B 的一部分，而且 C=D 或 D=C 是导出方程，则把 A=B 中某一次出现的 C 替换为 D，所得结果也是导出方程。

The derived equations of a set E of elementary equations are defined by induction as follows. The equations of E themselves are derived equations. If A =B is a derived equation containing a numerical variable x, then the result of substituting a particular numeral for all the occurrences of x in A =B is a derived equation. If A =B is a derived equation containing an elementary expression C (as part of either A or B), and if either C =D or D =C is a derived equation, then the result of substituting D for a particular occurrence of C in A =B is a derived equation.

假设某个有限初等方程组 E 的导出方程中，不存在形如 k=l、其中 k 与 l 是不同数码的方程；E 中出现的函数变量为 fn11,fn22,…,fnrr，下标分别为 n1,n2,…,nr；而且对于从 1 到 r 的每个 i，以及每组数码 k1i,k2i,…,knii，都存在唯一数码 ki，使 fnii(k1i,k2i,…,knii)=ki 是 E 的导出方程。再令 F1,F2,…,Fr 为这样定义的正整数函数：在所有情况下，Fi(m1i,m2i,…,mnii)=mi，其中这些正整数分别对应于上述数码。那么，就说方程组 E 定义了任意一个函数 Fi，或是它的一组递归方程；并说函数变量 fnii 表示函数 Fi。

Suppose that no derived equation of a certain finite set E of elementary equations has the form k=l where k and l are different numerals, that the functional variables which occur in E are fn11,fn22,…,fnrr with subscripts n1,n2,…,nr respectively, and that, for every value of i from 1 to r inclusive, and for every set of numerals k1i,k2i,…,knii, there exists a unique numeral ki such that fnii(k1i,k2i,…,knii)=ki is a derived equation of E. And let F1,F2,…,Fr be the functions of positive integers defined by the condition that, in all cases, Fi(m1i,m2i,…,mnii) shall be equal to mi, where m1i,m2i,…,mnii, and mi are the positive integers which correspond to the numerals k1i,k2i,…,knii, and ki respectively. Then the set of equations E is said to define, or to be a set of recursion equations for, any one of the functions Fi, and the functional variable fnii is said to denote the function Fi.

能够给出一组递归方程的正整数函数，称为递归函数。9

A function of positive integers for which a set of recursion equations can be given is said to be recursive.9

显然，对于任意递归正整数函数，都存在一个算法，能够有效计算所需的任意具体函数值。因为递归方程组 E 的导出方程可以有效枚举；而计算由函数变量 fnii 表示的函数 Fi 的具体值的算法，就是枚举 E 的导出方程，直到找到所需的特定方程 fnii(k1i,k2i,…,knii)=ki。10

It is clear that for any recursive function of positive integers there exists an algorithm using which any required particular value of the function can be effectively calculated. For the derived equations of the set of recursion equations E are effectively enumerable, and the algorithm for the calculation of particular values of a function Fi, denoted by a functional variable fnii, consists in carrying out the enumeration of the derived equations of E until the required particular equation of the form fnii(k1i,k2i,…,knii)=ki is found.10

如果满足 F(n) 为某个正整数无穷序列第 n 项的函数 F 是递归的，就称该序列是递归的。

We call an infinite sequence of positive integers recursive if the function F such that F ( n ) is the n-th term of the sequence is recursive.

如果一个函数根据某个正整数命题函数为真或为假而分别取值 2 或 1，而且这个函数是递归的，就称该命题函数是递归的。正整数的递归性质，是指一元正整数的递归命题函数；正整数之间的递归关系，是指二元或更多元正整数的递归命题函数。

We call a propositional function of positive integers recursive if the function whose value is 2 or 1, according to whether the propositional function is true or false, is recursive. By a recursive property of positive integers we shall mean a recursive propositional function of one positive integer, and by a recursive relation between positive integers we shall mean a recursive propositional function of two or more positive integers.

设函数 F 的因变量取值范围包含于正整数类，而自变量或每个自变量的取值范围是正整数类的某个子集，不必是全体正整数。如果能找到一个正整数递归函数 F′，其每个自变量的取值范围都是全体正整数，使得凡 F 有定义之处，F′ 的值都与 F 的值一致，就称 F 是潜在递归的。

A function F, for which the range of the dependent variable is contained in the class of positive integers and the range of the independent variable, or of each independent variable, is a subset (not necessarily the whole) of the class of positive integers, will be called potentially recursive, if it is possible to find a recursive function F′ of positive integers (for which the range of the independent variable, or of each independent variable, is the whole of the class of positive integers), such that the value of F′ agrees with the value of F in all cases where the latter is defined.

合式公式上的一个运算，是指因变量取值范围包含于合式公式类，而自变量或每个自变量的取值范围都是全体合式公式的函数。如果把所有公式替换为它们的哥德尔表示后得到的相应函数是潜在递归的，就称这个运算是递归的。

By an operation on well-formed formulas we shall mean a function for which the range of the dependent variable is contained in the class of well-formed formulas and the range of the independent variable, or of each independent variable, is the whole class of well-formed formulas. And we call such an operation recursive if the corresponding function obtained by replacing all formulas by their Cb;del representatioiis is potentially recursive.

类似地，若一个函数的因变量取值范围包含于正整数类或合式公式类，而每个自变量的取值范围等于全体正整数或全体合式公式，允许有些取值范围属于前一类、另一些属于后一类，那么，只要把所有公式替换为其哥德尔表示后得到的函数是潜在递归的，就称该函数是递归的。如果合式公式的某个无穷序列所对应的哥德尔表示序列是递归的，就称该公式序列是递归的。如果合式公式的某种性质或关系所对应的哥德尔表示的性质或关系是潜在递归的，就称该性质或关系是递归的。如果存在一个递归无穷序列，其中所有项都属于某个合式公式集合，而且该集合的每个公式都至少出现一次，就称这个集合是递归可枚举的。11

Similarly any function for which the range of the dependent variable is contained either in the class of positive integers or in the class of well-formed formulas, and for which the range of each independent variable is identical either with the class of positive integers or with the class of well-formed formulas (allowing the case that some of the ranges are identical with one class and some with the other), will be said to be recursive if the corresponding function obtained by replacing all formulas by their Gödel representations is potentially recursive. We call an infinite sequence of well-formed formulas recursive if the corresponding infinite sequence of Gödel representations is recursive. And we call a property of, or relation between, well-formed formulas recursive if the corresponding property of, or relation between, their Gödel representations is potentially recursive. A set of well-formed formulas is said to be recursively enumerable if there exists a recursive infinite sequence which consists entirely of formulas of the set and contains every formula of the set at least once.11

利用递归性概念，我们还可以按如下归纳方式定义初等数论命题：如果 φ 是 n 元正整数的递归命题函数，由相应的取值为 2 和 1 的函数的一组特定递归方程给出，而且 x1,x2,…,xn 是取正整数值的变量，则 φ(x1,x2,…,xn) 是初等数论命题。如果 P 是一个含有自由变量 x 的初等数论命题，则把 P 中所有作为自由变量出现的 x 替换为某个特定正整数，所得结果也是初等数论命题；(x)P 和 (∃x)P 也是初等数论命题，其中 (x) 和 (∃x) 分别是 x 在正整数类上的全称量词和存在量词。

In terms of the notion of recursiveness we may also define a proposition of elementary number theory, by induction as follows. If φ is a recursive propositional function of n positive integers (defined by giving a particular set of recursion equations for the corresponding function whose values are 2 and 1) and if x1,x2,…,xn are variables which take on positive integers as values, then φ(x1,x2,…,xn) is a proposition of elementary number theory. If P is a proposition of elementary number theory involving x as a free variable, then the result of substituting a particular positive integer for all occurrences of x as a free variable in P is a proposition of elementary number theory, and (x)P and (∃x)P are propositions of elementary number theory, where (x) and (∃x) are respectively the universal and existential quantifiers of x over the class of positive integers.

于是很容易看出，一个初等数论命题的否定，或两个初等数论命题的逻辑积或逻辑和，都可以用简单方式等价地表示为另一个初等数论命题。

It is then readily seen that the negation of a proposition of elementary number theory or the logical product or the logical sum of two propositions of elementary number theory is equivalent, in a simple way, to another proposition of elementary number theory.

7. 有效可计算性的概念

7. The notion of effective calculability.

我们现在把前面已经讨论过的、正整数函数有效可计算这一概念，定义为与正整数递归函数的概念相同，18 或等价地，与 λ 可定义的正整数函数的概念相同。我们认为，下面的讨论能够为这个定义提供正当理由，其程度限于：在选择一个与直观概念相对应的形式定义时，究竟能够得到多少肯定性的支持。

We now define the notion, already discussed, of an effectively calculable function of positive integers by identifying it with the notion of a recursive function of positive integers18 (or of a λ-definable function of positive integers). This definition is thought to be justified by the considerations which follow, so far as positive justification can ever be obtained for the selection of a formal definition to correspond to an intuitive notion.

前面已经指出，对于按刚才的定义有效可计算的每个正整数函数，都存在一个计算其值的算法。

It has already been pointed out that, for every function of positive integers which is effectively calculable in the sense just defined, there exists an algorithm for the calculation of its values.

反过来，在同一个有效可计算性定义下，凡存在计算其值的算法的函数，都是有效可计算的。例如，对一元正整数函数 F，算法是一种方法：给定任意正整数 n，能够得到用某种记法写成的表达式序列 En1,En2,…,Enrn；给定 n，可以有效计算 En1；给定 n 和所有 j<i 的表达式 Enj，可以有效计算 Eni；给定 n 以及直到 Enrn 为止的所有这些表达式，可以有效知道算法已经终止，并能有效计算 F(n)。假设为这些表达式所用的记法建立一套哥德尔表示，再采用哥德尔的方法，用单个正整数 2en13en2⋯pieni 表示有限表达式序列 En1,En2,…,Eni，其中各个 e 分别是相应 E 的哥德尔表示；特别地，用正整数 1 表示空表达式序列。于是可以定义一个二元正整数函数 G：若 x 表示有限序列 En1,En2,…,Enk，则当 i=k+1 时，G(n,x) 等于 Eni 的哥德尔表示；如果 k=rn，即算法已经在 Enk 处终止，则 G(n,x)=10；在其他情况下，G(n,x)=1。还可以定义一个二元正整数函数 H，它的值与 G(n,x) 相同，唯一的例外是 G(n,x)=10 时，H(n,x)=F(n)。如果允许把算法描述中出现的有效可计算性要求，解释为函数 G 和 H 的有效可计算性，19 并把 G 和 H 的有效可计算性理解为递归性或 λ 可定义性，那么通过直接论证，就能推出 F 的递归性或 λ 可定义性。

Conversely it is true, under the same definition of effective calculability, that every function, an algorithm for the calculation of the values of which exists, is effectively calculable. For example, in the case of a function F of one positive integer, an algorithm consists in a method by which, given any positive integer n, a sequence of expressions (in some notation) En1,En2,…,Enrn can be obtained; where En1 is effectively calculable when n is given; where Eni is effectively calculable when n and the expressions Enj,j<i, are given; and where, when n and all the expressions Eni up to and including Enrn are given, the fact that the algorithm has terminated becomes effectively known and the value of F(n) is effectively calculable. Suppose that we set up a system of Gödel representations for the notation employed in the expressions Eni, and that we then further adopt the method of Gödel of representing a finite sequence of expressions En1,En2,…,Eni by the single positive integer 2en13en2⋯pieni where en1,en2,…,eni are respectively the Gödel representations of En1,En2,…,Eni (in particular representing a vacuous sequence of expressions by the positive integer 1). Then we may define a function G of two positive integers such that, if x represents the finite sequence En1,En2,…,Enk, then G(n,x) is equal to the Gödel representation of Eni, where i=k+1, or is equal to 10 if k=rn (that is if the algorithm has terminated with Enk), and in any other case G(n,x) is equal to 1. And we may define a function H of two positive integers, such that the value of H(n,x) is the same as that of G(n,x), except in the case that G(n,x)=10, in which case H(n,x)=F(n). If the interpretation is allowed that the requirement of effective calculability which appears in our description of an algorithm means the effective calculability of the functions G and H,19 and if we take the effective calculability of G and H to mean recursiveness (λ-definability), then the recursiveness (λ-definability) of F follows by a straightforward argument.

假设我们处理某个特定的符号逻辑系统，其中包含表示正整数相等的符号 =，表示一元正整数函数作用于其实参的符号 {}()，以及表示正整数的表达式 1、2、3、……。系统的定理由一个有限或可数无穷的表达式表，即形式公理，加上从它们出发，有限次应用给定的有限或可数无穷操作表中的操作，即推演规则，所得到的全部表达式组成。如果系统要满足通常赋予符号逻辑系统的目的，就必须要求：每条推演规则都是有效可计算的运算；全部推演规则若为无穷多个，则可有效枚举；全部形式公理若为无穷多个，则可有效枚举；而且正整数与表示它的表达式之间的关系可有效判定。假设我们把这解释为：对于逻辑表达式的一套哥德尔表示，每条推演规则都必须是递归运算，20 全部推演规则必须递归可枚举，具体说，存在一个递归函数 Φ，使 Φ(n,x) 是把第 n 条推演规则应用于 x 所表示的有序有限公式集后所得结果的表示；全部形式公理必须递归可枚举；正整数与表示它的表达式之间的关系必须递归。21 如果逻辑中存在一个表达式 f，使 {f}(μ)=ν 为定理，当且仅当 F(m)=n 为真，其中 μ 和 ν 是表示正整数 m 和 n 的表达式，就称一元正整数函数 F 在该逻辑内可计算。22 由于该逻辑的全部定理递归可枚举，根据上面的定理 IV，就能推出：每个在该逻辑内可计算的一元正整数函数，也都是按我们的定义有效可计算的。

Suppose that we are dealing with some particular system of symbolic logic, which contains a symbol, =, for equality of positive integers, a symbol { }( ) for the application of a function of one positive integer to its argument, and expressions 1,2,3,… to stand for the positive integers. The theorems of the system consist of a finite, or enumerably infinite, list of expressions, the formal axioms, together with all the expressions obtainable from them by a finite succession of applications of operations chosen out of a given finite, or enumerably infinite, list of operations, the rules of procedure. If the system is to serve at all the purposes for which a system of symbolic logic is usually intended, it is necessary that each rule of procedure be an effectively calculable operation, that the complete set of rules of procedure (if infinite) be effectively enumerable, that the complete set of formal axioms (if infinite) be effectively enumerable, and that the relation between a positive integer and the expression which stands for it be effectively determinable. Suppose that we interpret this to mean that, in terms of a system of Gödel representations for the expressions of the logic, each rule of procedure must be a recursive operation,20 the complete set of rules of procedure must be recursively enumerable (in the sense that there exists a recursive function Φ such that Φ(n,x) is the representation of the result of applying the n-th rule of procedure to the ordered finite set of formulas represented by x), the complete set of formal axioms must be recursively enumerable, and the relation between a positive integer and the expression which stands for it must be recursive.21 And let us call a function F of one positive integer22 calculable within the logic if there exists an expression f in the logic such that {f}(μ)=ν is a theorem when and only when F(m)=n is true, μ and ν being the expressions which stand for the positive integers m and n. Then, since the complete set of theorems of the logic is recursively enumerable, it follows by Theorem IV above that every function of one positive integer which is calculable within the logic is also effectively calculable (in the sense of our definition).

这样就说明，采用两种自然想到的方法中的任何一种，都不能得到比上面提出的定义更一般的有效可计算性定义。这两种方法是：（1）若存在计算函数值的算法，就把函数定义为有效可计算；（2）若对于每个正整数 m，都存在正整数 n，使 F(m)=n 是可证明的定理，就把一元正整数函数 F 定义为有效可计算。

Thus it is shown that no more general definition of effective calculability than that proposed above can be obtained by either of two methods which naturally suggest themselves (1) by defining a function to be effectively calculable if there exists an algorithm for the calculation of its values (2) by defining a function F (of one positive integer) to be effectively calculable if, for every positive integer m, there exists a positive integer n such that F(m)=n is a provable theorem.

8. 转换的不变量

8. Invariants of conversion.

一个自然产生的问题，是寻找我们称为转换的公式变换的不变量。目前已知的有效可计算不变量，只有那些一眼可见的，例如公式中所含自由变量的集合。其他重要的不变量很可能存在。但我们将证明，按第 7 节提出的有效可计算性定义，不存在转换的一组完备的、有效可计算的不变量，见定理 XIX，另参见第 1 节。

The problem naturally suggests itself to find invariants of that transformation of formulas which we have called conversion. The only effectively calculable invariants at present known are the immediately obvious ones (e.g. the set of free variables contained in a formula). Others of importance very probably exist. Hence, utilizing the fact that a conversion is a finite sequence of immediate conversions, the proposition Ψ(a,b) will be expressible that a and b are Gödel representations of formulas A and B such that A conv B. Moreover if A conv B, and a and b are the Gödel representations of A and B respectively, the proposition Ψ(a,b) will be provable in the system, by a proof which amounts to exhibiting, in terms of Gödel representations, a particular finite sequence of immediate conversions, leading from A to B; and if A is not convertible into B, the ω-consistency of the system means that Ψ(a,b) will not be provable. If the Entscheidungsproblem for the system were solved, there would be a means of determining effectively of every proposition Ψ(a,b) whether it was provable, and hence a means of determining effectively of every pair of formulas A and B whether A conv B, contrary to Theorem XIX.

特别地，如果《数学原理》的系统是 ω 一致的，那么它的判定问题不可解。

In particular, if the system of Principia Mathematica be ω-consistent, its Entscheidungsproblem is unsolvable.

Chapter 1. Frege and “Currying”

1.1. 戈特洛布·弗雷格

1.1. Gottlob Frege

遵循罗瑟 [Ros82] 的思路，我们从戈特洛布·弗雷格开始这段旅程；罗瑟本人在我们的故事中也扮演着重要角色。弗雷格是一位德国数学家和逻辑学家，生于 1848 年 11 月 8 日。他主要关注哲学与数学的交汇处，即数学哲学，同时也研究数理逻辑。弗雷格“独自发现了使现代逻辑的整个发展成为可能的基本思想，并由此开创了一整门学科。”[Dum18]

In the spirit of Rosser [Ros82] who himself plays an important role in our story, we begin our journey with Gottlob Frege, a German mathematician and logician born on November 8, 1848. Focusing primarily on the intersection of philosophy and mathematics (i.e., the philosophy of mathematics) alongside mathematical logic, Frege “discovered, on his own, the fundamental ideas that have made possible the whole modern development of logic and thereby invented an entire discipline.” [Dum18]

1891 年，弗雷格在耶拿医学与自然科学学会作了一次演讲 [Fre91]，讨论函数的概念及其与他先前提出的概念观念之间的关系，并开始为函数的运作规定具体的逻辑规则。他指出，函数概念最初用于分析等领域，但当时对它的理解大致可以概括为：“所谓 x 的函数，被看作一个包含 x 的数学表达式，一个含有字母 x 的公式。”直到后来，才逐渐形成更严格的定义。弗雷格这次演讲及其后发表的文章，目的就是明确有关规律，从而更有意义地界定函数究竟是什么。

In 1891, Frege gave a lecture [Fre91] to the Fenaischen Gesellschaft fur Medizin und Naturwissenschaften in which he discussed the notion of functions and their relation to his former notion of concepts, and in which he began to assign specific logical rules for how functions operate. He put forth that the concept of a function had been used in areas such as analysis originally, but that the original understanding of them might have been summarized by saying something such as “A function of x was taken to be a mathematical expression containing x, a formula containing the letter x.” It was only later that a more strict definition came to be down, and the purpose of Frege's lecture and eventual article was to narrow down laws which provide a more meaningful definition of what a function is.

两年后的 1893 年，弗雷格出版了《算术基本规律》（Grundgesetze der Arithmetik）系列的第一部分 [Fre93]，使用他自己的函数表述来陈述和证明有关算术的定理。其中有一节运用他的记号¹，实际上说明了如何把一个接受两个参数的函数看成两个各接受一个参数的函数。下一节将进一步阐述这一思想。莫塞斯·申芬克尔 [Sch20] 和后来的哈斯凯尔·柯里 [CF58] 对它作了进一步研究，如今这一技巧便以柯里的名字命名。

Two years later in 1893, Frege published [Fre93] the first part of a series called Grundgesetze der Arithmetik (Basic Laws of Arithmetic), which used his own formulation of functions to state and proove theorems regarding arithmetic. Within this piece is a section which, using his notation¹, effectively shows how functions which take two arguments can be thought of as two functions which each take one argument. This idea, elaborated upon in the subsequent section, was explored further by Moses Schon nkel [Sch20] and later by Haskell Curry [CF58], after whom the technique is now named.

1.2. Curry：食物？数学家？还是处理函数的一种实用技巧？

1.2. Curry: Food? Mathematician? Or A Useful Technique For Working With Functions?

正如后文将看到的，λ 演算最初是为了帮助研究函数而创立的。在最初的 λ 演算中，每个函数都恰好接受一个参数。乍看之下，这似乎是一种限制；本节将说明为什么事实并非如此。

As we will see later, Lambda Calculus was originally created to aid in the study of functions. In the original lambda calculus, every function took exactly one argument. While this might seem like a limitation at first glance, in this section, we examine why that is not the case.

虽然在较初级的数学课程中，我们很容易忘记函数本身也是一等数学对象，但这里请记住这一点。事实上，让我们从形式化的集合论基础出发，回顾一下什么是（全）函数。

Although in lower-level mathematics courses, it can be easy to forget that functions themselves are first-class mathematical objects, let us remember this fact. In fact, let us recall what a (total) function is from a formal, set-theoretic foundation.

注 1：这是一种与今天所用记号大不相同的函数记法，但本文读者很可能会觉得它颇有趣味。

a functional notation which is vastly different from what we use today, but likely to be found interesting by a reader of the present paper.

定义 1.1. 函数 f:X→Y 是由二元组 (x,y) 构成的集合，其中 x∈X 且 y∈Y，并且 X 的每个元素都恰好是该集合中一个二元组的第一分量。

Definition 1.1. A function f:X→Y is a set of 2-tuples (x,y), so that x∈X and y∈Y, such that every element of X is the first component of exactly one of the tuples in the set.

现在考虑接受两个参数的函数，例如 f:(X,Y)→Z，或者用稍常见一些的记号写成 f:X×Y→Z。此时，上述定义所描述的集合，其元素必定具有 ((x,y),z) 的形式，其中 x∈X、y∈Y、z∈Z。在这种情况下，可以把函数 f 应用于两个实参 x 和 y，写成 f(x,y)=z∈Z。

Let us consider for a moment functions which take two parameters, such as f:(X,Y)→Z (or to use a slightly more conventional notation, f:X×Y→Z). Then elements of the set described in the definition above must have the form ((x,y),z), where x∈X, y∈Y, and z∈Z. In this case, we can apply the function f to the two arguments x and y, as in f(x,y)=z∈Z.

柯里化技巧指出，对于每个函数 f，都可以把 f:X×Y→Z 看成与函数 fcurried:X→(Y→Z) 等价，反之亦然。

What the technique of currying says is that for every function f, we can think of f:X×Y→Z as being equivalent to a function fcurried:X→(Y→Z), and vice versa.

让我们看一个例子。

Let us look at an example.

例 1.2. 设 f:ℤ×ℤ→ℤ 由通常的加法运算定义，即 f(n,k)=n+k。

Example 1.2. Let f:ℤ×ℤ→ℤ be defined by the usual addition operation, f(n,k)=n+k.

经过柯里化，它等同于函数 fcurried:ℤ→(ℤ→ℤ)，定义如下：

By currying, this is the same as a function fcurried:ℤ→(ℤ→ℤ), defined as follows:

fcurried(x) 是一个函数 g:ℤ→ℤ，其中 g 定义为 g(y)=x+y，这里的 x 是最初应用 fcurried 时传入的实参。

fcurried(x) = a function g:ℤ→ℤ, where g is then defined as g(y)=x+y, where x is the original argument to which fcurried was applied.

因此，我们有 f(2,3)=2+3=5，并且：

So we have: f(2,3)=2+3=5, and:

fcurried(2) 是一个函数，把它应用于某个数时，就会给这个数加上 2。因此可以写成 fcurried(2)(3)=2+3=5。

fcurried(2) = a function which when applied to some number adds 2 to it. So we can do: fcurried(2)(3)=2+3=5.

用现代记号，可以表示为 ZX×Y≅(ZY)X。左边表示从 X×Y 到 Z 的所有函数构成的集合；右边表示从 X 到“从 Y 到 Z 的函数集合”的所有函数构成的集合。用现代术语来说，我们还可以借助范畴论的发展，把这一概念推广到集合与函数之外的其他范畴²，但这超出了本文的范围。函数空间、拓扑学、同调代数、域论、逻辑和类型论等数学分支中，也可以定义这种对应关系。

In modern notation, we might express this by: ZX×Y≅(ZY)X, where the left hand side represents the set of all functions X×Y to Z and the right hand side represents the set of all functions from X to the set of functions from Y to Z. In modern terminology, we might fall back on advancements in category theory beyond the scope of the present paper, where the concept can be generalized further to categories other than sets and functions². The correspondence can also be defined by other branches of mathematics such as function spaces, topology, homological algebra, domain theory, logic, and type theory.

为求完整，下面证明这一关系对集合之间的函数成立。

For the sake of completeness, let us prove it true for set functions.

引理 1.3. ZX×Y 与 (ZY)X 之间存在双射。

Lemma 1.3. There exists a bijection between ZX×Y and (ZY)X.

证明。给定 g:X×Y→Z。再给定 x∈X，令函数 fx 由 fx(y)=g(x,y) 定义。令函数 f 由 f(x)=fx 定义。我们必须证明这一对应既是单射，也是满射。

Proof. Let g:X×Y→Z be given. Also let x∈X be given, and let the function fx be given by fx(y)=g(x,y). Let the function f be given by f(x)=fx. We must show this is one-to-one and onto.

设给定 g1 和 g2，且 f1(x)(y)=g1(x,y)、f2(x)(y)=g2(x,y)。如果 f1=f2，那么 ∀x:f1(x)=f2(x)。于是 ∀x,y:f1(x)(y)=f2(x)(y)，从而 ∀x,y:g1(x,y)=g2(x,y)。因此 g1=g2，这就证明了单射性。

Suppose g1 and g2 are given with f1(x)(y)=g1(x,y) and f2(x)(y)=g2(x,y). If f1=f2, then ∀x:f1(x)=f2(x). Then ∀x,y:f1(x)(y)=f2(x)(y). So ∀x,y:g1(x,y)=g2(x,y). So g1=g2 which shows one-to-one.

现在给定 f′。以 g(x,y)=f′(x)(y) 定义 g。然后由 fx(y)=g(x,y) 从 g 得到 fx，再由 f(x)=fx 得到 f。于是 ∀x,y:f(x)(y)=g(x,y)=f′(x)(y)，所以可以断言 f=f′。这就证明了满射性，证明完毕。³ □

Now let f′ be given. Define g by g(x,y)=f′(x)(y). Then we get fx from g by fx(y)=g(x,y) and f by f(x)=fx. So ∀x,y:f(x)(y)=g(x,y)=f′(x)(y) and we can claim f=f′. This shows onto, completing the proof.³ □

In category-theoretic terms, this relationship is a universal property of exponential objects and forms an adjunction in a cartesian closed category. This generalization has many uses, but category theory is a complex topic and is probably not something which should be explained to a sophomore who has yet to discover it.

Chapter 2. Combinatory Logic

2.1. λ 演算问世之前

2.1. Lambda Calculus Before Its Time

现在，我们把注意力转向一种称为组合逻辑或组合子演算的逻辑系统。莫塞斯·申芬克尔 [Sch20] 于 1920 年提出了这一逻辑系统及其记号，相关论文于 1924 年发表；1927 年末，哈斯凯尔·柯里 [Cur30] 又重新发现了它。此时距离 λ 演算登场还不到十年。

We now turn our attention to the advent of a logical system known as combinatory logic or combinator calculus, a logic system and notation developed by Moses Schon nkel [Sch20] in 1920 (published in 1924) and rediscovered by Haskell Curry [Cur30] in late 1927, less than a decade before Lambda Calculus entered the scene.

开发这一系统的目的是：在处理高阶逻辑（HOL）中的语句和表达式时，消除对变量绑定的顾虑，也就是对由“∀”“∃”等量词引入的变量的顾虑。

The system was developed for the purpose of eliminating concerns of variable bindings (i.e. variables introduced by quantifiers such as “∀” and “∃”) when working with statements and expressions in higher-order logic (HOL).

组合逻辑中有一组原始组合子。下面来定义它的含义。

In combinatory logic, there is a set of primitive combinators. Let us define what this means.

定义 2.1. 高阶函数是一种仅使用函数应用和先前定义的组合子来生成结果的函数。

Definition 2.1. A higher-order function is a function which only uses function application and previously defined combinators to generate its result.

定义 2.2. 类似地，组合子是一种其参数本身也是函数的函数。

Definition 2.2. Similarly, a combinator is a function whose argument is also a function.

用 λ 演算的术语来说，我们可以给组合子一个稍有不同的定义，下一章将会看到。

In the parlance of Lambda Calculus, we can ascribe to combinator a slightly different definition, as we will see in the subsequent chapter.

由于每个组合子都接受一个参数，本章假定所有组合子都已按上一章所述进行柯里化。因此，如果说某个函数接受“多个参数”，这只是若干各接受一个参数的柯里化函数的简写。

As combinators each take one argument, let us assume in this chapter that all combinators are curried as discussed in the foregoing chapter. Thus, if we speak of a function taking “multiple parameters”, we imply shorthand notation for curried functions which take one parameter each.

2.2. 组合项

2.2. Combinatory Terms

在组合逻辑¹中，项可以有两种形式：P，即某个原始组合子；或者 (E1E2)，即把组合项 E1 应用于 E2。

In combinatory logic¹, terms can take one of two forms: P (which is one of the primitive combinators), or (E1E2) which is the application of the combinatory term E1 to E2.

虽然组合逻辑存在多种表述方式，可以通过改变系统包含哪些原始组合子来得到，但最常见的表述包含三个原始组合子，称为 S、K 和 I。值得注意的是，申芬克尔最初的系统还包含另外几个原始组合子，但他证明了其他所有组合子，事实上也包括 I，都可以仅由 S 和 K 导出 [Sch20]。

Though various formulations of cominatory logic exist (by modifying which primitive combinators are included in the system), the most common formulation includes three primitive combinators, known as S, K, and I. It is worth noting that Schönfinkel’s original system had several other primitive combinators as well, but he proved that all of the others (and in fact I) can be derived from only S and K [Sch20].

注 1：凡是对数学与计算机科学这一分支有所学习的人，都会熟悉本节内容。因此，本节不逐一引用具体来源，任何一本组合逻辑入门书都足够；但对这一主题的历史感兴趣的读者，应参阅 [Sch20]。

The information contained in this section is well-known to anyone who has studied this branch of math and computer science to any extent. As such, we don't cite a specific source throughout this section (any introductory combinatory logic book suces), but the reader interested in the history of the subject should consult [Sch20].

考察 S、K 和 I，我们把 I 组合子定义为恒等函数。传给它什么，它就原封不动地返回什么，即 (Ix)=x。²

Looking at S, K, and I, we define the I combinator to be simply the identity function. Whatever is passed to it is simply returned unchanged, as in (Ix)=x.²

K 用来创建常值函数。也就是说，给定一个实参 x，(Kx) 是一个组合子，它接受参数 y，但总是返回 x，忽略新传入的 y。因此 (Kxy)=x。这里必须注意，按照约定，项向左结合，所以 (Kxy)=((Kx)y)。

The K creates constant functions. That is, given an argument x, (Kx) is a combinator which takes a parameter y and always returns x, ignoring the newly passed y. Thus, (Kxy)=x. Here it is important to note that by convention terms are grouped to the left, so (Kxy)=((Kx)y).

S 组合子是代入函数的一种推广，定义如下：(Sxyz)=(xz(yz))。也就是说，它分别把 z 作为实参传给 x 和 y，然后把后一次应用的结果作为实参传给前一次应用的结果。

The S combinator is a generalized version of the substitution function, and is defined as follows: (Sxyz)=(xz(yz)). That is, it applies the argument z to the arguments x and y, and then applies the result of the latter application to the result of the former.

细心的读者也许会发现，仅用 S 和 K 就可以构造一个与 I 等价的函数。

The astute reader might recognize that a function equivalent to I can be constructed purely out of S and K.

(SKKx)=(Kx(Kx))=x

2.3. 与 λ 演算的联系

2.3. Connection to Lambda Calculus

读者可能会问：“那么，这和 λ 演算有什么关系？”正如我们将看到的，两者的关系非常密切。事实上，这种组合子演算在计算能力上与 λ 演算等价，而 λ 演算本身又与图灵机以及哥德尔的一般递归函数记法在计算能力上等价。不过，我们先不要操之过急。

“So,” the reader might ask, “what has this got to do with Lambda Calculus?” It turns out, as we will see, that it has quite a lot to do with Lambda Calculus. In fact, this combinator calculus is computationally equivalent to the Lambda Calculus (which itself is computationally equivalent to Turing machines and to Godel's notation of generalized recursive functions). But let us not get ahead of ourselves.

上述组合子演算以及由它导出的其他演算，可以看作某种特殊的 λ 演算。不过，此时它之所以与我们的故事有关，是因为它在 λ 演算的发展中发挥了关键作用。

The combinator calculus described above (and others derived from it) can be seen as a particular kind of Lambda Calculus, but it is of interest to our story at this point because it plays a critical role in the development of Lambda Calculus.

虽然申芬克尔创立了组合逻辑理论，但他只发表了 [Sch20] 这一篇有关该主题的论文。除此之外，只有另一篇论文署有他的名字 [CH06]。事实上，[CH06] 告诉我们：“到 1927 年，据说他已患上精神疾病，住进了疗养院”，而他的晚年是在“困苦和贫穷”中度过的。他于 1942 年去世。令人遗憾的是，由于当时的战争环境，他最后那些已知的手稿被当作取暖的燃料烧掉了 [CH06]。

While Schon nkel founded the theory of combinatory logic, he only published [Sch20] and no other paper on the topic. Only one other paper bears his name [CH06]. In fact, [CH06] tells use that “By 1927, he was said to be mentally ill and in a sanatorium,” due to his later years being spent in “hardship and poverty.” He died in 1942. Sadly, the last known of his manuscripts were burned for warmth due to the wartime conditions of the era [CH06].

2.3.1. Enter: Haskell Curry

哈斯凯尔·柯里生于 1900 年。后来，他独立于申芬克尔重新发现了组合子演算，也独立于冯·诺伊曼；后者在 1925 年的工作中使用了组合逻辑，但尚不清楚他的工作是源自申芬克尔，还是独立发现的 [CH06]。罗素与怀特海的《数学原理》中没有给出命题逻辑代入规则的陈述，罗素后来也承认了这一点。下面让 [CH06] 进一步介绍柯里：

In 1900, Haskell Curry was born and would come to rediscover combinatory calculus independently of Schönfinkel (and of von Neumann who made use of combinatory logic in his work in 1925, though it is not known whether or not his work was derived from Schönfinkel’s work, or discovered independently) [CH06]. The statement of the substitution rule for propositional logic was missing from Russell and Whitehead’s Principia Mathematica, which was admitted by Russell later. We let [CH06] talk more about Curry:

大约在 1926—1927 年间，柯里开始寻找一种把代入过程分解为更简单步骤的方法。为此，他引入了一个组合子概念，与申芬克尔的概念基本相同。

Around 1926-27, Curry began to look for a way to break the substitution process down into simpler steps, and to do this, he introduced a concept of combinator essentially the same as Schon nkel's.

Note that depending on who is asked, the study of combinatory logic doesn’t itself include variables, and it is purely the combinators themselves which are studied. Here and in the following examples, we use variables x,y,… for demonstrating how the combinators work when applied to something.

1927 年稍晚时，柯里读到了 [Sch20]。此后，他创建了一个基于组合子的形式系统，并给出了完备性证明，其中使用了首创的抽象算法。后来，丘奇 [Chu35]、罗瑟 [Ros35] 和罗森布卢姆 [Ros50] 提出了与之竞争的“更简单”的算法，但这些算法往往产生更晦涩的结果，尽管结果等价，而且效率更低 [CH06]。到了 20 世纪 70 年代，人们对把这类系统用于实际编程的兴趣日益增长，与柯里最初算法相似的算法再次受到关注 [CH06]。

Later in 1927, Curry would discover [Sch20]. He would go on to create a formal system based on combinators along with a completeness proof, which used an abstraction algorithm that was the first of its kind. Later, Church [Chu35], Rosser [Ros35] and Rosenbloom [Ros50], would develop competing “simpiler” algorithms, but these would often produce more esoteric (though equivalent) results, and were more inecient [CH06]. When the 1970s came around and interest rose in the use of such systems for practical programming purposes, algorithms similar to Curry's original were once again of interest [CH06].

第 3 章　λ 演算

Chapter 3. Lambda Calculus

3.1. 阿隆佐·丘奇登场

3.1. Enter: Alonzo Church

我们的旅程继续，来到阿隆佐·丘奇。他于 1903 年出生在华盛顿特区 [CH06]。丘奇成为普林斯顿大学的学生，并在那里从事学习研究直到 1967 年 [CH06]。

We move ahead in our journey to Alonzo Church, born in 1903 in Washington D.C.[CH06]. Church became a student of Princeton, studying there until 1967 [CH06].

大约在 1928 年，丘奇开始构造一个研究逻辑基础的形式系统，希望它比罗素在《数学原理》附录中提出的类型论以及策梅洛的集合论都“更加自然”[CH06]。他还坚持要求系统中不包含自由变量。

Circa 1928, Church began to construct a formal system with the purpose of studying logical foundations while being “more natural” than both the type theory put forth by Russell in an appendix of Principia Mathematica and Zermelo's set theory [CH06]. He also insisted that the system not contain free variables.

丘奇决定以函数而不是集合为系统的基础，并把抽象与应用的概念作为原始概念引入。今天使用的记号，例如 λx:M 表示抽象，(FX) 表示应用 [CH06]。值得再次指出的是，据 [CH06] 所述，丘奇并不是第一个为函数抽象使用显式记号的人；但他是第一个同时为这种记号配备一组转换规则，并详细研究由此产生的理论的人。这一理论后来被称为 λ 演算。

Church decided to base the system on functions instead of sets, and included primitively a notion of abstraction and application. Today the notation used for these are, for example, λx:M (for abstraction), and (FX) (for application) [CH06]. It is worth noting (again per [CH06]) that Church was not the first to use an explicit notation for function abstraction, but he was the first to do so alongside a set of conversion rules for the notation, and to study the resulting theory in detail, which would become known as Lambda Calculus.

后来人们发现，早在几十年前的 1903—1905 年间，罗素在急切寻求罗素悖论的解决方案时，就已经“预见”了 λ 演算 [Kle03]。他的记号不同，但许多概念十分相似。然而，由于他把精力集中在解决悖论上，而这些工作又没有带来令人满意的结果，罗素从未发表过这套先于 λ 演算的演算。事实上，人们认为，丘奇虽然总体上受到了罗素工作的影响，但不可能知道罗素这套先行的演算。

It was later discovered that Russell had “anticipated” the Lambda Calculus several decades earlier between 1903 and 1905, while trying frantically to develop a solution to Russell's Paradox [Kle03]. His notation was different, but many of the concepts were very similar. However beacuse his e orts were so focused on solving the paradox, and this work not leading to a satisfactory result, Russell never published his pre-Lambda-Calculus. In fact, it's believed that Church, though influenced by Russell's work in general, could not have known about Russell's anticipatory calculus.

丘奇在 [Chu32] 中给出的系统是无类型的，“具有不受限制的量化，但没有排中律。”[CH06]

Church's system given in [Chu32] was a type-free one, “with unrestricted quantification but without the law of excluded middle.” [CH06]

该系统包含代入规则，允许把所有自由变量替换为某个项；还包含 α 等价，即只要改变绑定变量的名字，项仍然等价，例如 λx:x 与 λy:y 是同一个表达式，也就是恒等函数；以及 β 归约，它是项重写的主要规则 [ST18]。此外，还有 η 等价的概念，它允许在 λ 项不起作用时将其去掉。例如，λx:Mx 可以通过 η 归约重写为简单的 M。

The system included rules for substution (allowing for the replacement of all free variables with some term), α-equivalence (stating equivalence of terms up to a change of bound variables: for example, λx:x is the same expression (the identity function) as λy:y), and β-reduction, which is the primary rule for term-rewriting [ST18]. There is also a notion of η-equivalence, which allows for the dropping of lambda terms in cases where they do nothing. For example, λx:Mx can be rewritten by η-reduction to simply M.

在 1933 年同名论文的一个版本中，丘奇给出了用 λ 演算项表示正整数的方法，后来称为丘奇数 [Chu33]。这些数不是作为原始对象给出的，而是完全用 λ 抽象表示，如下所示 [CH06]：

In a 1933 version of his paper with the same name, Church gave a representation of positive integers using Lambda Calculus terms, which became known as church numerals [Chu33]. The numbers were represented not primitively, but purely in terms of lambda abstractions as follows[CH06]:

  • 1=λxy:xy
  • succ=λxyz:y(xyz)

不过，需要注意的是，这个最初的系统中没有 0。在 [Kle36] 中，斯蒂芬·克林研究出了一种略有不同的整数定义方式，为更一般的递归函数铺平了道路 [Ros82]。在 [CR36] 中，人们已经知道：每个能用 λ 演算定义的正整数到正整数的函数，都是有效可计算的 [Ros82]。随后，丘奇提出了一个如今称为丘奇论题的结果，给出了反方向的断言：从正整数到正整数的有效可计算函数，恰好就是那些能在 λ 演算中定义的函数。这一结果给出了“一种很强、而且相当出乎意料的完备性形式。”[Ros82]

We note, however, that there is no 0 this original system. In [Kle36], Stephen Kleene worked out a slightly different way to define integers, which paved the way for more general recursive functions [Ros82]. In [CR36] it was known that every function from positive integers to positive integers that is definable in terms of the Lambda calculus is effectively calculable [Ros82]. Church then presented a result now known as Church's Thesis which showed the converse: E ectively calculable functions from positive integers to positive integers are exactly those definable in the Lambda Calculus. This result provided a “strong, and quite unexpected, version of completeness.” [Ros82]

同样在 [CR36] 中，作为一个著名定理证明的一部分，作者展示了 λ 演算的一种相当强的一致性形式。这个定理称为丘奇—罗瑟定理，下面只陈述而不证明。¹

Also in [CR36], a fairly strong form of consistency is shown for the Lambda Calculus, as part of a proof of a well-known theorem called the Church-Rosser Theorem, which we now state but do not prove.¹

定理 3.1（丘奇—罗瑟）。对于任意表达式 x 和 y，如果 x 归约到 y，那么存在一个 z，使得 x 和 y 都归约到 z。

Theorem 3.1 (Church-Rosser). For every expression x and y so that x reduces to y, there exists a z so that both x reduces to z and y reduces to z.

诚然，关于 λ 演算自身的发展，以及 20 世纪 30 年代产生的各种变体，还有很多内容可讲。例如，[KR35] 发现丘奇 1933 年版本的演算受到理查德悖论的困扰。不过，旅程进行到这里，我们把有兴趣的读者引向 [CH06] 及其中的参考文献，接下来讨论 λ 演算在可计算性问题以及著名的判定问题中的应用。

There is, admittedly, much more to say about the development itself of Lambda Calculus and the variations that resulted in the 1930s. For example, the 1933 version of Church's Calculus was found to be plagued with the Richard paradox in [KR35]. However, at this point in our journey, we refer the interested reader to [CH06] and the references therein, and move on to the application of the Lambda Calculus to the problem of computability and the well-known Entscheidungsproblem.

3.2. 判定问题

3.2. Entscheidungsproblem

著名的判定问题可以追溯到 1928 年的希尔伯特。其想法是寻找一种算法，以一个一阶逻辑语句为输入，并以“是”或“否”回答该语句是否普遍有效，即在每个满足公理的结构中都有效 [HA28]。换句话说，就是寻找一种算法，判定给定语句是否能够仅使用一阶逻辑的规则，从公理中证明出来。

The famous Entscheidungsproblem dates back to Hilbert in 1928. The idea is to nd an algorithm which takes a first-order logic statement as input and produces a yes-or-no answer as to whether or not the statement is universally valid (valid in every structure satisfying the axioms) [HA28]. Put another way, the idea is to nd an algorithm which decides whether or not a given statement is provable from the axioms, using only the rules of first-order logic.

在 [Kle36] 中，克林给出了一般递归函数的定义，并把这一定义归于哥德尔 [Ros82]。哥德尔认为，这一概念应当“被当作有效可计算性的判据”。不过，在同一篇文章中，克林证明了一般递归性与可在 λ 演算中定义完全相同，从而为丘奇论题提供了有力支持 [Ros82]。

In [Kle36], a definition by Kleene (attributed to Godel) is given for that of a general recursive function [Ros82]. Godel thought that this concept should be “taken as the criterion of effectively calculable.” In the same article, however, Kleene showed that general recursiveness is exactly the same as being definable in the Lambda Calculus, lending strong support to Church's Thesis [Ros82].

在这些研究进行的同时，艾伦·图灵一直专注于发展一种抽象的计算机概念，如今称为图灵机 [Ros82] [Tur36]。他认为，有效可计算的概念应当等同于可由图灵机计算的那些函数。然而，一年后，图灵证明了这一概念与可在 λ 演算中定义相同 [Ros82]。这就是 λ 演算和组合子演算在计算机科学各领域中发挥如此重要作用的原因 [Ros82]。这一结果称为丘奇—图灵论题。因此，今天 λ 演算被用作许多不同函数式编程语言的基础，也受到证明论、类型论以及其他处于数学与计算机科学交汇处的分支研究者的关注。这里值得注意的是，λ 演算给出了判定问题不可解的第一个证明 [CH06]。不过，图灵通过他的机器给出的证明更加易懂、更加清晰 [CH06]。

As this research was happening, Alan Turing had been focusing on developing an abstraction notion of a computer, now known as a Turing machine [Ros82] [Tur36]. He thought that the notion of effectively calculable should be the same as those functions computable on a Turing machine. However a year later, Turing proved that this concept is the same as being definable in the Lambda Calculus [Ros82]. This is why the Lambda Calculus and combinator calculus play such a large role within various fields of computer science [Ros82]. This result is known as the Church-Turing thesis. As a result, today, Lambda Calculus is used as the foundation for many different functional programming languages and is of interest to those doing work in proof theory and type theory, among other branches that lie in the intersection of mathematics and computer science. It is worth noting here that the Lambda Calculus provided the first proof of the unsolvability of the Entscheidungsproblem [CH06]. However, Turing's proof via his machines was much more accessible and transparent [CH06].

注 1：所引论文给出了已知最早的丘奇—罗瑟定理证明，但此后人们又提出并研究了许多证明。这个结果也称为 λ 演算的合流性，事实证明，它对 λ 演算的研究极其重要。

The paper cited provides the first known proof of the Church-Rosser Theorem, but many proofs have been created and studied since. The result, also known as confluence of the Lambda Calculus, has proven extremely important to its study.

随着时间推移，人们又提出了其他“有效可计算”的定义，后来发现它们也都彼此等价。有关这些定义的信息，请参阅 [Ros82] 第 16 页及其中引用的文献。

As time went on, other later-found-equivalent definitions of “effectively calculable” were proposed. For information on these, we refer the reader to page 16 of [Ros82] and the sources cited therein.

3.3. 类型论

3.3. Type Theory

如果不提及类型论这一领域，本文就不算完整。如上所述，丘奇最初的 λ 演算是完全无类型的。

The present paper would not be complete without a mention of the field of type theory. Originally, as noted above, Church's Lambda Calculus was purely untyped.

定义 3.2. 类型论是数学与计算机科学的一个分支，最初由伯特兰·罗素作为解决罗素悖论的一种手段，在《数学原理》的附录 B 中提出。此后，它发展成为一个独立的研究领域，并推动了计算机科学中程序设计语言理论这一子领域的大量研究。

Definition 3.2. Type theory is a branch of mathematics and computer science originally created by Bertrand Russell as an appendix (appendix B) to Principia Mathematica as a means of solving Russell's Paradox. It has since become a field of study in its own right and drives much research in the computer science subfield of programming language theory.

类型论使罗素能够在他的集合论表述中有效地排除 x∈x 这一公式。简而言之²，罗素的类型论给指定论域中的每个对象标上一个类型，在他的体系中，这个类型是一个自然数。然后，他要求：要使 x∈y 成为一个合法语句，y 的类型必须比 x 的类型大 1。

What type theory allowed Russell to do was effectively reject x∈x as a formula in his formulation of set theory. In brief², Russell’s type theory indexed every inhabitant of the given universe with a type (in his case, a natural number). He then required that for x∈y to be a valid statement, the type of y must be one greater than the type of x.

[ST18] 告诉我们：

We are told by [ST18] that:

1940 年，丘奇给出了一种“包含 λ 转换某些特征的简单类型论表述”[Chu40]。虽然简单类型的历史超出了本文的范围，而且丘奇最初的表述也超出了无类型 λ 演算的语法范围，其方式与最初那个不一致的表述有些相似，但我们可以直观地把类型看成一种语法修饰：它限制项的构成，使函数只能应用于合适的实参。

In 1940, Church gave a “formulation of the simple theory of types which incorporates certain features of λ-conversion” [Chu40]. Though the history of simple types extends beyond the scope of this paper and Church’s original formulation extended beyond the syntax of the untyped λ-calculus (in a way somewhat similar to the original, inconsistent formulation), one can intuitively think of types as syntactic decorations that restrict the formation of terms so that functions may only be applied to appropriate arguments.

从这个意义上说，类型使我们能够限制哪些项可以存在，以及它们在什么条件下存在；用计算机科学的说法，这些项就是函数或程序。正因如此，λ 演算在 Coq、Agda 和 Isabelle/HOL 等自动定理证明器这类编程系统中发挥着关键作用。丘奇最初将这一概念应用于 λ 演算的表述，称为“简单类型 λ 演算”。

In this sense, types allow us to place restrictions on which terms (functions or programs, in the vernacular of computer science) can be inhabited and under which conditions. It is for this reason that the Lambda Calculus plays such a critical role in programming systems such as automated theorem provers like Coq, Agda, and Isabelle/HOL. Church's original formulation of the concept as applied to Lambda Calculus is known as the “Simply Typed Lambda Calculus.”

多年来，类型论经历了多次扩展，如今仍是计算机科学和数学现代分支中的活跃研究领域。例如，同伦类型论位于拓扑学、同伦论、类型论与数学基础的交汇处，尤其是在直觉主义背景下，并采用一条称为“单价性”的公理。³

Type theory has been extended many times over the years, and is still an active area of research in modern branches of computer science and mathematics, such as Homotopy Type Theory, which lives in the intersection of topology, homotopy theory, type theory, and mathematical foundations, particularly in an intuitionistic setting, with an axiom known as “Univalence.”³

The original appendix where type theory first appears describes it better than we can in this brief paper. The interested reader is suggested to refer to that appendix for a historic view of type theory. For modern approaches and uses for it, particularly in computer science, Benjamin Pierce's “Types and Programming Languages” or CMU's Robert Harper's “Practical Foundations for Programming Languages” are excellent resources.

注 3：这个主题虽然极其有趣，但远远超出了本文的范围。有兴趣的读者应参阅《同伦类型论》教材，可从 Homotopy Type Theory 免费下载。

We note that there is much more to be said about every concept and section listed in this paper, and that an extensive survey of the topic would likely be at least double or triple the length of this paper.

有兴趣的读者可以进一步研究的主题包括：简单类型 λ 演算的更多内容；讨论 λ 演算其他变体及其与类型论关系的“λ 立方体”；不同版本的类型论，包括马丁-勒夫的依赖类型论以及线性类型系统；以及人们如何发现图灵机、哥德尔的一般递归函数与丘奇的 λ 演算在计算能力上等价。特别关注 λ 演算数学应用的读者，会希望了解它如何在范畴论中充当笛卡尔闭范畴的内部语言；而对类型论感兴趣的读者也会注意到，我们没有讨论柯里—霍华德对应。这是一种重要的对应关系，把类型与命题紧密联系起来，也把程序，即函数，与这些命题的证明紧密联系起来。

Topics that the interested reader might consider researching more about include more information about Simply Typed Lambda Calculus, the “Lambda Cube” which discusses other variants of the Lambda Calculus and their relation to type thoery, different versions of type theory (including Martin-Lof's theory of dependent types, as well as linear type systems), and more about how the connection between Turing's machines, Godel's general recursive functions, and Church's Lambda Calculus were found to be equivalent in computability power. Those interested particulary in the mathematic applications of Lambda Calculus will be keen to learn about its use as an internal language for Cartesian-closed categories in category theory; and those interested in type theory will note that we did not discuss the CurryHoward Correspondence, an important correspondence which intimately links types with propositions and programs (functions) with proofs of those propositions.

这些主题对于全面了解 λ 演算都是必要的；不过，考虑到时间与纸张的节省，我们只好道一声晚安，待到明朝再会。

These topics are necessary for a complete overview of the Lambda Calculus, but in the interest of time and conservation of paper, we shall have to say good night, till it be morrow.

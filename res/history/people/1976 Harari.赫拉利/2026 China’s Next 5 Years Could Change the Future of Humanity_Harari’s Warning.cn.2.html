未来五年可能改变一切The Next Five Years Could Change Everything

如果中国接下来的五年，关乎的不只是中国呢？如果它们成为整个人类物种的转折点呢？因为历史上最大的变化，很少伴随着某一个戏剧性的事件到来。它们悄然发生：一项新技术变得寻常，一种新经济体系变得无法回避，一种新型监控变得方便，一种人与机器的新关系变得普通。What if the next 5 years in China are not simply about China? What if they become a turning point for the entire human species? Because the biggest changes in history rarely arrive with a single dramatic event. They arrive quietly. A new technology becomes normal. A new economic system becomes unavoidable. A new form of surveillance becomes convenient. A new relationship between humans and machines becomes ordinary.

等到世界理解究竟发生了什么变化，变化早已固化。中国正在进入这样一个时期：人工智能、机器人、生物技术、数字基础设施、经济实力与国家能力，可能以非同寻常的速度汇聚。问题并不只是中国会不会变得更强。And by the time the world understands what has changed, the change has already become permanent. China is entering a period where artificial intelligence, robotics, biotechnology, digital infrastructure, economic power, and state capacity may converge at an extraordinary speed. The question is not simply whether China will become more powerful.

更深的问题是：当世界上最大的文明之一，开始以一种人类制度从未被设计用来应对的技术速度运行时，人类会怎样？想象五年后醒来，发现你曾经视为正常的世界，已经悄然消失。工厂变了，工作变了，城市变了，政府的决策方式变了，企业的运作方式也变了。The deeper question is this, what happens to humanity when one of the world's largest civilizations begins operating at a technological speed that human institutions were never designed to handle. Imagine waking up 5 years from now and discovering that the world you considered normal has quietly disappeared. The factories are different. The jobs are different. The cities are different. The way governments make decisions is different. The way businesses operate is different.

也许最重要的是，人与智能机器的关系完全不同了。你可能仍然认得那些街道，仍然认得自己的家，仍然认得身边的人。但表象之下，文明可能已经按照一种完全不同的逻辑运行。这就是未来五年如此重要的原因。中国不只是又一个正在经历技术变革的国家。And perhaps most importantly, the relationship between human beings and intelligent machines is completely different. You might still recognize the streets. You might still recognize your home. You might still recognize the people around you. But underneath the surface, civilization could be operating according to a completely different logic. This is why the next 5 years matter. China is not simply another country experiencing technological change.

它拥有世界上最大规模的人口之一，是最强大的制造业国家之一，是重要的科学中心，也是一个能够大规模部署技术的文明体。这些因素结合起来，形成了历史上并不常见的局面：只有当发明与规模相遇，技术进步才会真正带来转型。实验室里一台出色的机器，改变不了太多；在整个经济体中运转的数百万台智能机器，却可能改变几乎一切。未来也因此变得难以预测。It is one of the world's largest populations, one of its greatest manufacturing powers, a major scientific center, and a civilization with the ability to deploy technologies at enormous scale. That combination creates something historically unusual because technological progress becomes truly transformative when invention meets scale. A brilliant machine in a laboratory changes very little. Millions of intelligent machines operating across an economy can change almost everything. And this is where the future becomes difficult to predict.

在人类历史的大部分时间里，技术延伸的是我们的身体力量。车轮让移动更高效，机器让生产更高效，发动机让出行更快。但人工智能带来了根本不同的东西：它开始延伸人类心智的某些方面。当机器不再只是等待指令的工具，而开始成为能够分析、预测、生成、协调并作出决策的系统时，社会就进入了陌生的领域。For most of human history, technology extended the strength of our bodies. The wheel allowed us to move more efficiently. Machines allowed us to produce more efficiently. Engines allowed us to travel faster. But artificial intelligence introduces something fundamentally different. It can begin extending aspects of the human mind. And when machines stop being merely tools that wait for instructions and start becoming systems that analyze, predict, generate, coordinate, and make decisions, society enters unfamiliar territory.

问题于是变成：当智能本身可以规模化扩展，会发生什么？一个工人需要多年的教育，一台机器却可能被复制。一位人类专家能够教导的人数有限，一个数字系统却可能把同样的能力分发到数百万台机器上。这改变了智能的经济逻辑。而每当某种根本事物的经济逻辑发生变化，社会也会随之改变。The question becomes, what happens when intelligence itself becomes scalable? A human worker needs years of education. A machine can potentially be copied. A human expert can teach a limited number of people. A digital system can potentially distribute the same capability across millions of machines. That changes the economics of intelligence. And whenever the economics of something fundamental changes, society changes with it.

为什么中国的未来关乎全人类Why China’s Future Matters to Humanity

这就是为什么，中国未来的意义远远超出中国自身。This is why the future of China matters far beyond China.

想象一个经济体，工厂越来越少依赖人类工人；仓库里的机器人彼此协调；交通系统不断自我优化；人工智能帮助设计产品、管理供应链、分析科学数据，并协调庞大的工业体系。单独看，没有哪一项发展听起来像是世界末日。但历史很少被单一发明改变，而是被各种组合改变。印刷术与大众识字能力结合，改变了政治。Imagine an economy where factories increasingly operate with fewer human workers. Imagine warehouses where robots coordinate with one another. Imagine transportation systems that continuously optimize themselves. Imagine artificial intelligence helping design products, manage supply chains, analyze scientific data, and coordinate enormous industrial systems. Individually, none of these developments sounds like the end of the world. But history is rarely transformed by one invention. It is transformed by combinations. The printing press combined with mass literacy changed politics.

工业机械与化石燃料结合，改变了生产；计算机与电信结合，创造了数字世界。现在，想象人工智能与机器人、生物技术、先进制造和庞大数字基础设施结合。这种组合可能开启文明的新阶段。这里，我们遇到了一个人类反复低估的问题：我们很擅长发明新工具，却不太擅长预测，这些工具将怎样改变我们自己。Industrial machinery combined fossil fuels transformed production.computers combined with telecommunications created the digital world. Now imagine artificial intelligence combining with robotics, biotechnology, advanced manufacturing, and enormous digital infrastructure. That combination could create a new phase of civilization. And here we encounter a problem that humans repeatedly underestimate. We are very good at inventing new tools. We are much less good at predicting how those tools will change us.

汽车普及时，它不只是让马不再那么重要，还改变了城市、建筑、就业，以及日常生活的空间格局。智能手机出现时，它不只是给人们一部更好的电话，还改变了人们交流、购物、导航、社交、工作和获取信息的方式。技术不会一直停留在人类生活之外。最终，它会进入我们的习惯、制度、期待，最后进入我们对“正常”的定义。When automobiles became widespread, they did not simply make horses less important. They transformed cities. They transformed architecture. They transformed employment. They transformed the geography of daily life. When smartphones appeared, they did not merely give people better telephones. They changed how people communicate, shop, navigate, socialize, work, and consume information. Technology does not remain outside humanity. Eventually, it enters our habits, then our institutions, then our expectations, and finally our definition of what is normal.

这就是未来五年可能如此重要的原因。如果人工智能与自动化深度融入世界上最大的经济体之一，后果就不会只停留在工厂内部。它们会扩散到教育、就业、全球贸易、地缘政治、科学竞争，最终进入普通人的日常生活。但还有一个更深的问题：当经济价值越来越不依赖人类劳动，人类会怎样？This is why the next 5 years could be so important. Because if artificial intelligence and automation become deeply integrated into one of the world's largest economies, the consequences will not remain inside factories. They will spread into education, into employment, into global trade, into geopolitics, into scientific competition, and eventually into the daily lives of ordinary people. But there is an even deeper question. What happens to human beings when economic value becomes less dependent on human labor?

数千年来，一个人的生存能力，与工作能力紧密相连。你狩猎、耕种、建造、制造、计算、组织，为别人提供他们需要的东西。但如果机器越来越能同时完成体力和智力任务，旧有关系就开始松动。社会于是面对一个很少真正面对过的问题：当生产率不再能证明一个人有用时，一个人究竟有什么价值？For thousands of years, your ability to survive was closely connected to your ability to work. You hunted, you farmed, you built, you manufactured, you calculated, you organized, you provided something that other humans needed. But if machines can increasingly perform both physical and intellectual tasks, that old relationship begins to weaken. And then society faces a question it has rarely confronted. What is a human being worth when productivity is no longer proof of usefulness?

这不只是经济问题，也是心理问题。人们想要的不只是钱，还有意义、认可、被需要的感觉，以及相信自己的存在很重要。如果技术逐渐让数百万人不再被传统经济活动所必需，那么社会需要的，就不只是新的工作岗位。This is not merely an economic question. It is a psychological question. People do not only want money, they want significance, they want recognition, they want to feel necessary, they want to believe their existence matters. If technology gradually removes millions of people from traditional forms of economic necessity, societies will need something more than new jobs.

人工智能与智能机器的崛起The Rise of AI and Intelligent Machines

它们还需要关于意义的新观念。They will need new ideas about meaning.

未来也正是在这里变得危险，因为最大的威胁未必是技术性失业，而可能是心理上的失位感。失去工作的人可以再找一份工作。但当一个人开始相信，自己的能力永远不如机器时，会怎样？当智能不再是人类独有的优势时，会怎样？当机器写得更快、算得更快、能分析更多信息，还能作出人类不容易理解的预测时，会怎样？And this is where the future becomes dangerous, because technological unemployment is not necessarily the great threat may be psychological displacement. A person who loses a job can search for another job. But what happens when someone begins to believe that their abilities are permanently inferior to machines? What happens when intelligence is no longer an exclusively human advantage? What happens when machines can write faster, calculate faster, analyze more information, and make predictions humans cannot easily understand?

危险不在于机器突然变邪恶，而在于人类因为机器看起来更能干，开始交出决策。起初，我们向机器征求建议；然后，请它们预测；再然后，请它们直接决策。最后，我们可能不再追问，它们究竟该不该作这些决定。这就引出了未来十年最重要的问题之一：那些越来越深刻影响人类生活的系统，究竟由谁控制？The danger is not that machines suddenly become evil. The danger is that humans begin surrendering decisions because machines appear more competent. First, we ask machines for recommendations. Then we ask them for predictions. Then we ask them to make decisions. Eventually, we may stop asking whether they should be making those decisions at all. And this brings us to one of the most important questions of the coming decade. Who controls the systems that increasingly influence human life?

因为智能会创造权力。如果一个算法比数百万人更理解市场，它就拥有经济权力；如果它能预测行为，就拥有社会权力；如果它能优化工业生产，就拥有经济权力；如果它能处理关于人口的大量信息，就拥有政治权力。因此，决定未来的，不只是“谁拥有最先进的技术”，还包括“谁控制它”。Because intelligence creates power. If an algorithm can understand markets better than millions of individuals, it has economic power. If an algorithm can predict behavior, it has social power. If an algorithm can optimize industrial production, it has economic power. If an algorithm can process enormous amounts of information about populations, it has political power. Future will therefore not be determined only by who possesses the most advanced technology. It will also be determined by who controls it.

也许更重要的是，谁理解它。在这里，个人与机构之间的差异变得至关重要。一个人可能不理解复杂人工智能系统怎样得出某个结论，但政府可能依赖它，企业可能依赖它，金融机构可能依赖它。数百万普通人，可能依赖着自己看不见、也无法理解的系统。这创造了一种奇特的新型依赖。And perhaps even more importantly, who understands it. This is where the difference between individuals and institutions becomes critical. A human being may not understand how a complex artificial intelligence system reaches a particular conclusion. A government may depend upon it. A corporation may depend upon it. A financial institution may depend upon it. Millions of ordinary people may depend upon systems they cannot see and cannot understand. That creates a strange new form of dependency.

人类可能被比任何单个人都更聪明的系统包围，却仍然无法充分解释它们的行为。这并不意味着未来注定失败，而意味着，未来要求人类拿出一种在历史上始终难以形成的东西：与力量相匹配的智慧。中国未来五年，不会单独决定人类的命运，没有哪个国家能完全控制历史。Humanity could become surrounded by systems more intelligent than any individual human while remaining unable to fully explain their behavior. This does not mean the future is doomed. It means the future demands something humanity has historically struggled to produce, wisdom equal to our power. China's next 5 years will not determine the destiny of humanity by themselves. No single nation can control history completely.

但中国可能成为最重要的观察场之一，让我们看到：当庞大的技术能力，与庞大人口、工业规模、科学雄心和强大制度相遇，会发生什么。世界其他地方都会注视着。有的国家会竞争，有的会模仿，有的会抵制，有的会监管。但最终，几乎所有人都得作出回应，因为技术竞争不会被限制在地理边界之内。But China can become one of the most important laboratories for observing what happens when enormous technological capacity meets enormous population, industrial scale, scientific ambition, and powerful institutions. And the rest of the world will be watching. Some countries will compete, some will imitate, some will resist, some will regulate. But eventually, almost everyone will have to respond because technological competition does not remain geographically contained.

如果一个社会发现了效率高得多的制造、沟通、自动化或信息组织方式，其他社会就无法简单地忽视。它们必须适应，于是形成了一场竞赛。这不仅是争取更好机器的竞赛，更是人类适应能力与技术加速之间的竞赛。If one society discovers a dramatically more efficient way to manufacture, communicate, automate, or organize information, other societies cannot simply ignore it. They must adapt, and this creates a race, not merely a race for better machines, a race between human adaptation and technological acceleration.

当人类劳动失去优势When Human Labor Loses Its Advantage

这可能才是未来五年的真正挑战。教育能不能足够快地改变？政府能不能理解那些演进速度超过立法速度的技术？劳动者能不能在职业消失以前完成适应？That may be the real challenge of the next 5 years. Can education change quickly enough? Can governments understand technologies that evolve faster than legislation? Can workers adapt before their professions disappear?

当自动化系统越来越便利，社会还能保有人类的判断力吗？在构建能够预测人类行为的系统时，人类还能守住自由吗？这些问题超越中国，超越美国，也超越欧洲，属于每一个将亲历这场转型的人。也许，最让人不安的可能性是：未来不会以一场戏剧性的革命到来，而会以便利的面貌出现，机器替你做一件事。Can societies maintain human judgment when automated systems become increasingly convenient? Can humanity preserve freedom while building systems capable of predicting human behavior? These questions are bigger than China, they are bigger than America, they are bigger than Europe, they belong to every human being who will live through the transition. And perhaps the most uncomfortable possibility is this, the future may not arrive as a dramatic revolution. It may arrive as convenience, a machine makes one do.

算法推荐一样东西，机器人完成一项任务，数字系统管理一道流程。因为每个单独的变化都让生活更轻松，我们便接受了。接着，另一个系统接管另一项决策，再接管一项。直到有一天，我们环顾四周，发现世界已经高效得不可思议，却不再确定，自己究竟还控制着多少。这就是为什么，我们不应该带着恐惧观察未来几年。An algorithm recommends one thing, a robot performs one task, a digital system manages one process, and because each individual change makes life easier, we accept it. Then another system takes over another decision, and another, until one day we look around and realize that the world has become incredibly efficient, but we are no longer certain how much of it we control. That is why the coming years should not be watched with fear.

我们应该带着专注去观察。因为人类最大的优势，从来不是拥有最强壮的身体、最快的大脑，而是合作、想象、学习和改变的能力。但如今，我们创造出了变化速度超过自己的技术。因此，挑战不是阻止未来，而是让自己足够明智，能够塑造未来。最重要的问题不是“技术会不会改变人类”。They should be watched with attention, because humanity's greatest advantage has never been that we possess the strongest bodies or the fastest minds. Our greatest advantage has been our ability to cooperate, imagine, learn, and change. But now we have created technologies that can change faster than we can. The challenge is therefore not to stop the future. The challenge is to become intelligent enough to shape it. Because the most important question is not will technology change humanity?

它已经在改变了。真正的问题是：人类能不能足够明智地改变自己，继续掌握方向？未来五年，也许会给出一部分答案。历史回望这段时期时，可能不会只把它记作机器变得更强大的时刻，而会记作人类被迫决定，自己究竟想成为什么样文明的时刻。It already is. The real question is, will humanity change wisely enough to remain in control of the direction? The next 5 years may give us part of the answer. And when history looks back at this period, it may not remember it simply as the moment when machines became more powerful. It may remember it as the moment when humanity was forced to decide what kind of civilization it wanted to become.

在人类历史的大部分时间里，文明变化得足够缓慢，一代人能够理解自己继承的世界。农民可以把基本的生存规则教给孩子。生活虽然艰难，但社会的基本结构仍然熟悉可辨。工具可能改进，王国可能兴衰，但人与工作的关系相对稳定。今天，这个假设正变得越来越危险。For most of human history, civilizations changed slowly enough that one generation could understand the world it had inherited. A farmer could teach his children the basic rules of survival. And although life was difficult, the fundamental structure of society remained recognizable. The tools might improve, kingdoms might rise and fall, but the relationship between human beings and their work remained relatively stable. Today, that assumption is becoming increasingly dangerous.

当人工智能、机器人、先进制造、数字基础设施和科学研究开始相互强化，中国未来五年可能成为一个重要测试：整个文明究竟能够多快地转变。关键不是中国会突然发明某一种改变一切的神奇技术，历史很少这样运作。更深层的转型，来自多种技术开始连接成一个系统。孤立的机器人，只是一台机器。China's next 5 years could become an important test of how rapidly an entire civilization can transform itself when artificial intelligence, robotics, advanced manufacturing, digital infrastructure, and scientific research begin reinforcing one another. The important point is not that China will suddenly invent one magical technology that changes everything. History rarely works that way. The deeper transformation comes when many technologies begin connecting into a single system. A robot in isolation is simply a machine.

孤立的人工智能，只是一套软件；高度自动化的工厂，也只是一家高效工厂。但如果把智能软件、能够在现实中行动的机器、可以大规模制造这些机器的工厂、协调它们的数字系统，以及能够快速部署它们的制度结合起来，某种根本不同的东西就开始出现。Artificial intelligence in isolation is simply software. A highly automated factory is simply an efficient factory. But combine intelligent software with machines that can physically act, factories that can produce those machines at enormous scale, digital systems that can coordinate them, and institutions capable of deploying them rapidly, and something fundamentally different begins to emerge.

中国与自动化的未来China and the Future of Automation

这就是发明与转型的区别。人类一直在创造强大的工具，但过去的工具，主要放大的是肌肉的力量。This is the difference between invention and transformation. Human beings have always created powerful tools, but our previous tools mostly amplified muscles.

机器能举起比人更多的重量，发动机能跑得比人更快，工厂能比单个工匠制造更多物品。在某种意义上，机器仍然从属于人类智能。人决定要做什么，机器帮助人完成。人工智能挑战了这种安排，因为它开始进入曾被视为人类独有的领域：识别模式、生成语言、分析信息、预测结果、设计系统，以及提出建议。A machine could lift more than a human. An engine could move faster than a human. A factory could produce more objects than individual craftsmen. The machine remained, in a sense, subordinate to human intelligence. Humans decided what had to be done, while machines helped them do it. Artificial intelligence challenges this arrangement because it begins to enter the territory once considered uniquely human, recognizing patterns, generating language, analyzing information, predicting outcomes, designing systems, and making recommendations.

一旦智能本身开始部分自动化，人类决策与机器辅助之间的旧边界，就越来越难维持。中国在这个故事中重要，是因为规模会改变一切。一项只有几千人使用的技术，很有意思；部署到数亿人之间，它就会变成一种社会力量。Once intelligence itself becomes partially automated, the old boundaries between human decision making and machine assistance become increasingly difficult to maintain. And China matters in this story because scale changes everything. A technology used by a few thousand people is interesting. A technology deployed across hundreds of millions of people becomes a social force.

当一个国家拥有庞大的制造能力，又能把新技术整合进交通、物流、能源、通信、工厂和城市，技术变革就可以从实验走向基础设施。这带来了一种引人深思的历史可能：技术发展的下一阶段，也许不是创造一台台令人惊叹的机器，而是创造整个环境，让机器在其中持续协同。When a country possesses enormous manufacturing capacity and can integrate new technologies across transportation, logistics, energy, communications, factories, and cities, technological change can move from experiment to infrastructure. This creates a fascinating historical possibility. The next phase of technological development may not be about creating individual machines that are impressive. It may be about creating entire environments in which machines cooperate continuously.

想象这样的工厂：机器人不再只是重复固定动作，而是与智能系统沟通，由后者监控生产、发现故障、重新设计流程、协调供应链。想象仓库在消费者下单以前就预测需求，交通系统根据数百万条信息不断调整，科学实验室由人工智能搜索那些人类研究团队根本不可能手动逐一检验的可能性。单独看，每项发展似乎都很平常。Imagine factories where robots do simply repeat fixed movements, but communicate with intelligent systems that monitor production, detect failures, redesign processes, and coordinate supply chains. Imagine warehouses that can predict demand before consumers even place orders. Imagine transportation systems constantly adjusting themselves according to millions of pieces of information. Imagine scientific laboratories where artificial intelligence searches through possibilities that no human research team could examine manually. Each development may appear ordinary when viewed separately.

但结合起来，它们可能重新定义工业文明。因此，未来五年不应该只被理解成“谁的人工智能模型最好”的竞争。更深的竞争，可能在于谁能最有效地把智能连接到物理现实。能够将软件、机器、能源、制造、数据、教育和基础设施整合为一个运转良好的生态系统的国家，可能拥有一种别人很难迅速复制的优势。Together, they could redefine the meaning of industrial civilization. This is why the next 5 years should not be understood simply as a competition over who has the best artificial intelligence model. The deeper competition may be over who can intelligence to physical reality most effectively. The country that can combine software, machines, energy, manufacturing, data, education, and infrastructure into one functioning ecosystem may possess an advantage that is difficult for others to reproduce quickly.

但这种转型还有另一面。文明变得更高效，并不意味着自动变得更明智。效率回答的是：“怎样更快、更便宜、或以更大规模完成这件事？”智慧问的却是：“我们到底该不该做？”But, there is another side to this transformation. When civilizations become more efficient, they do not automatically become wiser. Efficiency answers the question, "How can we do this faster, cheaper, or at greater scale?" Wisdom asks, "Should we do it at all?"

AI 的力量与信息控制AI Power and the Control of Information

这不是同一个问题。一个文明可能变得极其高效，生产的却是后来会制造巨大问题的东西。These are not the same question. A civilization can become extraordinarily efficient at producing something that later creates enormous problems.

历史上有很多例子：社会越来越擅长做某些事，却没有越来越理解它们的后果。随着中国和其他大国加速技术发展，这一区别会越来越重要。能够构建某种东西，不一定意味着能够控制它的后果。这又引向第二场重大转型：人类工作的意义。数千年来，经济生活围绕人类劳动组织起来。History contains many examples of societies becoming better at doing things without becoming better at understanding their consequences. This distinction will become increasingly important as China and other major powers accelerate technological development. The ability to build something does not necessarily mean the ability to control its consequences. And this leads to the second great transformation, the meaning of human work. For thousands of years, economic life was organized around human labor.

无论一个人是农民、士兵、商人、工匠、教师、行政人员还是建造者，社会通常都依赖某种形式的人类贡献。即使机器进入工业世界，它们也主要替代特定的体力任务，同时创造新的人类工作类别。人工智能带来了更复杂的可能性：当自动化开始超越体力劳动，进入认知劳动，会发生什么？Whether a person was a farmer, soldier, merchant, craftsman, teacher, administrator, or builder, society generally depended upon some form of human contribution. Even when machines entered the industrial world, they mostly replaced particular physical tasks while creating new categories of human work. Artificial intelligence introduces a more complicated possibility. What happens when automation begins moving beyond physical labor and into cognitive labor?

工厂机器人能够替代一项重复性的体力任务，智能系统则可能分析文档、翻译语言、编写软件、识别模式、生成设计、总结信息并辅助决策。因此，问题不再只是机器能不能替代工人，而是：机器能否越来越多地完成那些现代社会一直用来定义人类经济价值的活动？几代人以来，教育都被视为应对技术变革的答案。A factory robot can replace a repetitive physical task, but an intelligent system can potentially analyze documents, translate languages, write software, detect patterns, generate designs, summarize information, and assist with decisions. This means the question is no longer simply whether machines can replace workers. The question becomes whether machines can increasingly perform the very activities through which modern societies have defined human economic value. For generations, education was presented as the answer to technological change.

一种职业消失，就学另一种技能；机器替代体力劳动，就成为知识工作者；常规工作被自动化，就培养创造力和批判性思维。但如果人工智能也开始进入这些领域，会怎样？人类于是面对一个更深的挑战。我们可能发现，问题从来不只是技能不足，而在于我们一直假设：总有某种具有经济价值的事情，人类能比机器做得更好。If one profession disappeared, learn another skill. If machines replace physical labor, become a knowledge worker. If routine work became automated, develop creativity and critical thinking. But what happens when artificial intelligence begins entering those areas as well? Then humanity faces a much deeper challenge. We may discover that the problem was never simply a shortage of skills. The problem was our assumption that there would always be something economically valuable that humans could do better than machines.

这个假设已经不再有保障。如果它开始动摇，社会就必须重新思考一种最古老的信念：一个人在经济上的有用程度，能够可靠地衡量他的价值。一个人不只是一名劳动者，也是一份意识、一种关系、一个故事、一段记忆，是共同体的参与者。然而，现代经济体常常通过生产率衡量人：你能生产多少？能赚多少？That assumption is no longer guaranteed. And if it weakens, society will have to reconsider one of its oldest beliefs, that a person's economic usefulness is a reliable measure of their value. A human being is not merely a worker. A human being is a consciousness, a relationship, a story, a memory, a participant in a community. Yet modern economies often measure people through productivity. How much can you produce? How much can you earn?

你做事有多高效？你的劳动能创造多少价值？几个世纪以来，这些衡量标准与生存紧密相连。但如果智能机器不需要多少人类参与，就能创造巨大的经济价值，这种旧联系就开始断裂。这可能带来未来时代最深刻的社会问题之一：如果机器越来越高产，人类要怎样使用自己的时间？如果就业变得不再那么必要，人们又将到哪里寻找人生目的？How efficiently can you perform? How much value can your labor generate? For centuries, these measurements were closely connected to survival. But if intelligent machines can produce enormous amounts of economic value with little human involvement, the old connection begins to break. And that could create one of the most profound social questions of the coming era. If machines become increasingly productive, what will humans do with their time? If employment becomes less necessary, where will people find purpose?

生物技术与人类生命的未来Biotechnology and the Future of Human Life

如果智能变得充裕，什么还能让一个人觉得自己独一无二？这些问题最终可能比哪个国家造出了最先进的机器更重要。技术革命不只改变经济，也改变人的身份认同。越早理解这一点的社会，可能越能为接下来的世界做好准备。更深层的转型，也许并不发生在机器内部，而是发生在围绕机器的系统之中。If intelligence becomes abundant, what will make an individual feel unique? These questions may eventually become more important than the question of which country produces the most advanced machines. Technological revolutions do not merely change economies, they change human identities. And the societies that understand this early may be better prepared for the world that follows. The deeper transformation may not happen inside the machines themselves. It may happen inside the systems that surround them.

数千年来，人类建立制度时都基于一个假设：决定最终由其他人来作。农民决定种什么，商人决定卖什么，经理决定雇谁，医生决定怎样治疗病人，政府官员决定怎样分配资源。即使这些决定受到信息和工具影响，最终权威通常仍然是另一个人。For thousands of years, human beings have built institutions around the assumption that decisions are ultimately made by other human beings. A farmer decides what to plant. A merchant decides what to sell. A manager decides whom to hire. A doctor decides how to treat a patient. A government official decides how resources should be distributed. Even when these decisions were influenced by information and tools, the final authority was usually another human being.

人工智能开始挑战这一假设。想象一家工厂，智能系统不只是操纵机器，还不断决定应该生产什么、何时生产、原料来自哪里、需要多少库存，以及整个业务应怎样应对需求变化。系统内部某处，也许仍然有人类工人，但连接成千上万项决策的逻辑，可能已经不再来自人类。Artificial intelligence begins to challenge that assumption. Imagine a factory in which an intelligent system does not merely operate machines, but continuously decides what should be produced, when it should be produced, where materials should come from, how much inventory is necessary, and how the entire operation should respond to changing demand. There may still be human workers somewhere inside the system, but the logic connecting thousands of decisions may no longer be human.

从工厂转向其他领域：交通系统可以预测拥堵，能源系统可以预测需求，金融系统可以发现模式，通信平台可以预测人们可能消费什么信息，医疗系统可以分析海量医学数据，政府系统可以处理数百万公民的信息。每一个系统单独看都很有用，但当它们开始互相通信，某种根本不同的东西就出现了。你拥有的不再只是一组工具。Move from the factory to traffic systems can predict congestion. Energy systems can predict demand. Financial systems can detect patterns.communication platforms can predict what information people are likely to consume. Healthcare systems can analyze enormous quantities of medical data. Government systems can process information about millions of citizens. Each individual system may appear useful, but when they begin communicating with one another, something fundamentally different emerges. You no longer have a collection of tools.

你拥有的是一种环境，而环境无需征得许可，就能够影响人的行为。这是历史最重要的教训之一：权力并不总是通过直接下命令运作。有时，权力通过控制那些看起来可供选择的选项来运作。一个人可能相信，自己在几种可能性之间自由选择，但周围的系统早已决定了，他最有可能看见哪些可能性。You have an environment, and an environment can influence human behavior without asking for permission. This is one of the most important lessons of history. Power does not always operate by giving direct orders. Sometimes power operates by controlling the choices that appear available. A person may believe that they are freely choosing between several possibilities, while the surrounding system has already determined which possibilities they are most likely to see.

在算法时代，这一点尤其重要。假设一个算法，比你自己更了解你的习惯。它知道你通常买什么、看什么，什么使你愤怒、什么使你害怕，什么时候你最容易改变主意，以及什么信息最容易抓住你的注意力。一开始，这似乎无害。它可以推荐电影，推荐商品。This becomes particularly important in the age of algorithms. Suppose an algorithm understands your habits better than you understand them yourself. It knows what you usually buy, what you usually watch, what makes you angry, what makes you afraid, when you are most likely to change your mind, and which messages are most likely to hold your attention. At first, this may seem harmless. It can recommend a movie. It can recommend a product.

它可以推荐上班路线。但同样的原理，最终也可能用于后果重得多的决定：你应该学什么？应该接受哪份工作？应该追逐哪些金融机会？应该相信哪种政治信息？什么信息值得你关注？到那时，问题不再是算法能不能帮助人类决策，而是人类是否仍然有能力，在没有算法的情况下作决定。It can recommend a route to work. But, the same principle can eventually be applied to much more consequential decisions. What should you study? Which job should you accept? Which financial opportunities should you pursue? Which political message should you believe? Which information deserves your attention? At that point, the question is no longer whether algorithms can help humans make decisions. The question becomes whether humans remain capable of making decisions without them.

当机器开始作出决策When Machines Begin Making Decisions

这一区别很重要，因为便利一直是人类历史上最强大的力量之一。人们很少因为有人宣布要夺走控制权，就主动交出控制。他们会因为生活因此更轻松，一点一点地交出去。机器替你记住密码，算法替你选择音乐，导航系统替你选择路线，推荐系统替你选择接下来观看的内容。最终，人工智能系统替你决定，哪些信息值得关注。This distinction matters because convenience has always been one of the most powerful forces in human history. People rarely surrender control because someone announces that control is being taken away. They surrender small pieces of control because doing so makes life easier. The machine remembers your passwords. The algorithm chooses your music. The navigation system chooses your route. The recommendation system chooses what you watch next. The artificial intelligence system eventually chooses what information deserves your attention.

每一个决定看似微不足道，但数百万个微不足道的决定，能够创造一种完全不同的文明。因此，中国以及其他地方正在发生的技术转型，不应该只被理解为打造更快机器的竞争。它也是设计人类将生活于其中的系统的竞争。这就引出了更大的地缘政治问题：几个世纪以来，政治体系围绕领土、资源、军队和贸易路线展开竞争。Each decision seems insignificant, but millions of insignificant decisions can create a completely different civilization. This is why technological transformation taking place in China and elsewhere should not be understood simply as a competition to build faster machines. It is also a competition to design the systems through which human beings will live. And this brings us to the larger geopolitical question. For centuries, political systems competed over territory, resources, armies, and trade routes.

在工业时代，它们也竞争工厂、能源、交通网络和科学知识。未来时代，竞争可能越来越涉及一种更不显眼、却可能更强大的东西：组织信息与智能的能力。不同文明，可能对同一个问题给出不同答案。强大技术应该由谁控制？市场决定吗？政府决定吗？个人公民决定吗？大型企业决定吗？或者，最终应该允许智能系统自己作出某些决定吗？In the industrial age, they also competed over factories, energy, transportation networks, and scientific knowledge. In the coming era, competition may increasingly involve something less visible, but potentially more powerful. The ability to organize information and intelligence. Different civilizations may develop different answers to the same question. Who should control powerful technologies? Should the market decide? Should governments decide? Should individual citizens decide? Should large corporations decide? Or should intelligence systems themselves eventually be allowed to make certain decisions?

这些问题没有技术上的答案。机器可以计算哪个选项效率更高，却不能自动告诉人类，效率究竟为了什么。效率不是道德原则。一个社会可以极其高效地生产某种可怕的东西，人类历史已经反复证明了这一点。二十世纪表明，工业组织、科学知识和行政效率能够创造非凡成就，但当它们指向错误目标，也会放大人类造成的毁灭。There is no technological answer to these questions. A machine can calculate which option is more efficient. It cannot automatically tell humanity what efficiency is for. Efficiency is not a moral principle. A society can become extremely efficient at producing something terrible. Human history has demonstrated this many times. The 20th century showed that industrial organization, scientific knowledge, and administrative efficiency can produce extraordinary achievements, but they can also magnify human destruction when directed toward the wrong goals.

技术不会自动让文明更明智，只会让文明更强大。而力量会放大那些已经存在的价值观。因此，未来的技术竞争，最终可能变成不同文明模式之间的竞争。想象两个社会拥有同样先进的人工智能，但根据技术由谁控制、哪些信息可被获取、决策怎样作出，以及个人拥有哪些权利，它们仍然可能变成截然不同的社会。Technology does not automatically make civilization wiser. It makes civilization more powerful. And power amplifies whatever values are already present. This is why the coming technological competition may eventually become a competition between different models of civilization. Imagine two societies possessing equally advanced artificial intelligence. They could still become radically different societies depending on who controls the technology, what information is available, how decisions are made, and what rights individuals possess.

一个社会可能主要用智能系统最大化经济生产率，另一个可能最大化社会稳定，另一个可能优先保障个人自主，还有一个可能优先推进科学。也有社会会结合几个不断相互竞争的目标。机器可能相似，文明不会相同。这意味着，要理解未来，不能只问哪个国家开发出了最好的人工智能。更深的问题是，那个国家拿它来做什么。One society might use intelligent systems primarily to maximize economic productivity. Another might use them to maximize social stability. Another might prioritize individual autonomy. Another might prioritize scientific advancement. Another might combine several goals that constantly compete with one another. The machines may be similar. The civilizations will not be. This means that the future cannot be understood simply by asking which country develops the best artificial intel Deeper question is what that country does with it.

技术依赖的隐性风险The Hidden Risk of Technological Dependence

一项技术获得历史重要性，不是在它出现的时候，而是在它嵌入制度的时候。蒸汽机改变文明，是因为工厂和交通系统围绕它重新组织；电力改变文明，是因为整座城市、整个行业围绕它重新设计；互联网改变文明，是因为通信、商业、娱乐、教育和政治围绕数字网络重新组织。人工智能可能遵循同样的模式。第一阶段是发明，第二阶段是采用。A technology becomes historically important not when it exists, but when it becomes embedded in institutions. The steam engine transformed civilization because factories and transportation systems were reorganized around it. Electricity transformed civilization because entire cities and industries were redesigned around it. The internet transformed civilization because communication, commerce, entertainment, education, and politics were reorganized around digital networks. Artificial intelligence could follow the same pattern. The first stage is invention. The second stage is adoption.

第三阶段是依赖。而到了依赖阶段，转型就变得难以逆转。一旦数百万个组织依靠智能系统，放弃它们就会极其昂贵。一旦劳动者围绕它们受训，企业围绕它们组织，政府依靠它们运作，这些系统看起来就不再是可选技术，而是基础设施。基础设施会悄然支配文明。大多数人开灯时，不会想到电网。The third stage is dependence. And dependence is where the transformation becomes difficult to reverse. Once millions of organizations depend on intelligent systems, abandoning them becomes extremely expensive. Once workers are trained around them, businesses are organized around them, and governments rely upon them, these systems stop looking like optional technologies. They become infrastructure. And infrastructure quietly governs civilization. Most people do not think about the electrical grid when they turn on a light.

发消息时，不会想到全球服务器网络；看见桌上一件普通物品时，不会想到背后的供应链。现代文明之所以能够运转，正是因为庞大的系统在幕后无形地工作。人工智能可能成为这种无形基础设施的又一层。这带来一个奇特的悖论：智能系统越成功，就可能越不显眼。They do not think about the global network of servers when they send a message. They do not think about the supply behind an ordinary object sitting on a table. Modern civilization works precisely because enormous systems operate invisibly in the background. Artificial intelligence could become another layer of this invisible infrastructure. And that creates a strange paradox. The more successful intelligent systems become, the less visible they may become.

人们可能不会在某天早上醒来，突然发现 AI 改变了文明。他们只会年复一年地发现，越来越少的重要决定，是在没有 AI 的情况下作出的。所以，真正的问题不是机器会不会变聪明，它们会。真正的问题是，人类的制度能否保持足够的智慧来治理它们。因为一个文明完全可能拥有非凡的技术力量，却缺乏运用它所必需的智慧。People may not wake up one morning and discover that AI has transformed civilization. They may simply discover year by year that fewer and fewer important decisions are made without it. That is why the real question is not whether machines will become intelligent. They will. The real question is whether human institutions will remain intelligent enough to govern them. Because a civilization can possess extraordinary technological power and still lack the wisdom necessary to use it.

这也许正是每一个进入下一个技术时代的社会所面对的核心挑战：不是怎样构建能作出更多决策的系统，而是怎样确保人类仍然理解，哪些决定绝不应该交出去。生物技术又带来了另一层不确定性：人工智能改变我们处理信息的方式，生物技术则改变生命本身的可能性。在人类历史的大部分时期，生物性被当作某种既定条件。And perhaps this is the central challenge facing every society entering the next technological era. Not how to build systems that can make more decisions, but how to ensure that humans still understand which decisions should never be surrendered. Biotechnology introduces another layer of uncertainty because artificial intelligence changes the way we process information while biotechnology changes the possibilities of life itself. For most of human history, biology was treated as something given to us.

我们可以影响环境、建设城市、栽培作物、研发药物，但人体的基本构造，大体上不受我们控制。现在，这条边界正变得模糊。基因技术、生物工程、医学研究和计算生物学的进步，正逐渐把生物学从人类主要观察的对象，变成越来越能够干预的对象。当这种发展与人工智能结合，后果会远远超过任一技术单独能够带来的影响。We could influence our environment, we could build cities, cultivate crops, and develop medicines, but the basic architecture of the human body remained largely outside our control. Now that boundary is becoming less clear. Advances in genetic technologies, biological engineering, medical research, and computational biology are gradually transforming biology from something humans primarily observe into something humans can increasingly manipulate. When this development combines with artificial intelligence, the consequences become much larger than either technology alone.

人工智能检查海量生物信息的速度，远超过任何一位科学家。它可以识别模式、比较基因序列、模拟分子结构，帮助研究者搜索那些几乎不可能手动检验的可能性。Artificial intelligence can examine enormous quantities of biological information far faster than any individual scientist. It can identify patterns, compare genetic sequences, model molecular structures, and help researchers search through possibilities that would have been almost impossible to examine manually.

中国与全球技术竞赛China and the Global Technology Race

随后，生物技术能够把其中一些发现，转化为实际干预。这就形成了一个强大的反馈循环：机器越来越擅长理解生物系统，人类也越来越擅长改造这些系统。Biotechnology can then turn some of those discoveries into physical interventions. This creates a powerful feedback loop. Machines become better at understanding biological systems and humans become better at modifying those systems.

因此，重要问题不再只是技术会不会让人类更健康。更深的问题是：人类是否正在接近这样一个时期，生命本身的特征，越来越成为技术设计的对象？这种可能性改变了权力的含义。工业时代的权力，很大程度上与土地、能源、工厂、交通和物质生产的控制相连。信息时代的权力，则越来越依赖数据和计算的控制。The important question is therefore no longer simply whether technology will make humans healthier. The deeper question is whether humanity is approaching a period in which the characteristics of life itself become increasingly subject to technological design. That possibility changes the meaning of power. In the industrial age, power was largely connected to control over land, energy, factories, transportation, and physical production. In the information age, power increasingly depends on control over data and computation.

在一个由生物技术与人工智能塑造的未来，权力可能与更根本的东西相连：理解和影响生物过程的能力。谁最有能力解释生物信息，谁就可能在医学、农业、科学研究，乃至最终的人体增强方面获得优势。所以，不能只把技术竞争理解为制造更快计算机或更精巧机器人的竞赛。不同技术正在越来越多地相互强化。In a future shaped by biotechnology and artificial intelligence, power could become connected to something even more fundamental, the ability to understand and influence biological processes. Whoever possesses the greatest capacity to interpret biological information may gain advantages in medicine, agriculture, scientific research, and perhaps eventually in human enhancement. This is why technological competition cannot be understood simply as a race to produce faster computers or more sophisticated robots. Different technologies increasingly reinforce one another.

人工智能可以加速科学发现，先进制造可以生产新机器，机器人可以让实验室和工厂自动化，生物技术可以改变生物系统，庞大数字网络可以把这些过程全部连接起来。结果不再是一组彼此独立的技术，而是一个越来越一体化的技术生态。生态系统的行为，与单一发明并不相同。当几种强大技术相互作用，综合影响就可能变得难以预测。Artificial intelligence can accelerate scientific discovery. Advanced manufacturing can produce new machines. Robotics can automate laboratories and factories. Biotechnology can alter biological systems. Massive digital networks can connect all of these processes. The result is not a collection of separate technologies and increasingly integrated technological ecosystem. And ecosystems behave differently from individual inventions. When several powerful technologies interact, their combined effect can become difficult to predict.

一项单独看似乎无害的发展，连接到另一个系统之后，可能产生巨大的后果。更强的 AI 可以加速生物技术研究，更好的生物技术又可能创造对计算系统的新需求；自动化实验室可以加快实验，先进制造则让新技术更便宜、更容易部署。因此，每一次改进，都可能让下一次改进来得更快。这创造了一种奇特的历史局面。A development that appears harmless in isolation may become enormously consequential when connected to another system. A more capable AI may accelerate biotechnology research. Better biotechnology may create new demands for computational systems. Automated laboratories may accelerate experimentation. Advanced manufacturing may make new technologies cheaper and easier to deploy. Each improvement can therefore increase the speed of the next improvement. This creates a strange historical situation.

人类文明一直在变化，但历史上大多数时候，变化足够缓慢，几代人可以逐渐适应。一个农民可以预期，自己一生中许多基本生活条件，都会保持熟悉可辨。Human civilization has always changed, but for much of history change was slow enough that generations could adapt gradually. A farmer could expect many of the basic conditions of life to remain recognizable throughout his lifetime.

人类能够控制自己的造物吗？Can Humanity Control Its Own Creations?

工业劳动者经历了巨大变化，但即使工业化，也是以数十年为跨度展开的。二十一世纪的技术环境则不同，因为从发明到大规模采用的间隔，可能大幅缩短。危险未必是技术一夜之间失控。An industrial worker experienced enormous changes, but even industrialization unfolded over decades. The technological environment of the 21st century is different because the interval between invention and mass adoption can be dramatically shorter. The danger is not necessarily that technology becomes uncontrollable overnight.

危险在于，社会逐渐依赖那些没有人完全理解的系统。想想，当一项重要决策被交给算法，会发生什么。最初，算法可能只提供建议，人仍然负责最终决策。但如果算法始终表现得比人好，人们自然会越来越信任它。下一步，就是允许系统自动作出决定。The danger is that society gradually becomes dependent upon systems that nobody fully understands. Consider what happens when an important decision is delegated to an algorithm. At first, the algorithm may simply provide advice. Humans remain responsible for the final decision. But if the algorithm consistently appears to perform better than humans, people naturally begin trusting it more. The next step is to allow the system to make the decision automatically.

最终，质疑系统甚至可能显得不理性，因为机器掌握的信息，比任何单个人都多。这个过程不需要有人有意识地决定交出控制权，它可以通过便利悄然发生。导航系统告诉我们走哪条路，推荐系统告诉我们看什么，自动化金融系统判断哪些交易可疑，算法帮助企业判断哪些求职者值得关注。Eventually, questioning the system may seem irrational because the machine has access to more information than any individual human. This process can happen without anyone consciously deciding to surrender control. It can happen through convenience. A navigation system tells us which road to take. A recommendation system tells us what to watch. An automated financial system determines which transactions appear suspicious. An algorithm helps a company decide which applicants deserve attention.

预测系统协助医生识别可能的疾病。这些发展，没有哪一项必然剥夺人的自由。但结合起来，它们创造了一个世界：越来越多的决策，受那些超出普通人理解范围的系统影响。所以，核心问题不只是人工智能，而是人类判断正逐渐从必需品变成可选功能。当人必须作决定，就必须培养判断力。A predictive system assists doctors in identifying possible diseases. None of these developments necessarily removes human freedom. But together, they create a world in which more and more decisions are influenced by systems operating beyond ordinary human understanding. The central problem is therefore not simply artificial intelligence. It is a gradual transformation of human judgment from a necessity into an optional feature. When humans are required to make decisions, they must develop judgment.

当机器替人作决定，人可能逐渐失去运用判断力的能力。在高度自动化系统中长大的一代，可能极其高效，却越来越无法理解那些组织着自己生活的系统。最大的危险，也许就在这里出现。人类常常假设，更多信息会自动带来更多智慧，但历史并没有多少证据支持这一假设。信息可以增加知识，也可以增加操纵。When machines make the decisions, humans may gradually lose the ability to exercise it. A generation raised inside highly automated systems could become extraordinarily efficient while becoming less capable of understanding the systems that organize its own existence. This is where the greatest danger may emerge. Humanity has often assumed that having more information automatically makes people wiser. History provides little evidence for that assumption. Information can increase knowledge, but it can also increase manipulation.

如果人们无法区分可靠信息和精心设计的叙事，那么被无穷信息包围的人群，反而可能更容易受影响。让机构理解人群的技术，也能让机构影响人群。如果一个系统知道数百万人害怕什么、渴望什么、购买什么、搜索什么、讨论什么，就可能以非同寻常的准确度预测行为。A population surrounded by endless information may become easier to influence if it cannot distinguish reliable information from carefully engineered narratives. The same technologies that allow institutions to understand populations can also allow institutions to influence them. If a system knows what millions of people fear, desire, purchase, search for, and discuss, it may become possible to predict behavior with extraordinary accuracy.

文明的未来The Future of Civilization

当行为模式能够被持续分析，传统的隐私观念就更难维持，由此产生新的政治问题：数据归谁所有？算法由谁控制？谁决定算法获准做什么？那些操纵系统、监视所有人的人，又由谁来监督？当政府和企业建设越来越复杂的数字基础设施，这些问题尤其重要。The traditional idea of privacy becomes much harder to maintain when behavioral patterns can be analyzed continuously, this creates a new political question. Who owns the data? Who controls the algorithms? Who decides what the algorithms are allowed to do? And who watches the people operating the systems that watch everyone else? These questions become particularly important as governments and corporations build increasingly sophisticated digital infrastructures.

技术可以让社会更高效，但效率与自由并不总是同一回事。一个完全优化的系统，仍然可能产生人们并不想要的结果。想象一个社会，每个动作都能被测量，每次购买都能被分析，每次交流都能被分类，每种偏好都能被预测。这样的社会，可能极其擅长在问题出现以前识别问题。但同一种能力，也可能创造前所未有的社会控制形式。Technology can make societies more efficient, but efficiency and freedom are not always the same thing. A perfectly optimized system may still produce outcomes that people do not consider desirable. Imagine a society where every movement can be measured, every purchase analyzed, every communication categorized, and every preference predicted. Such a society could potentially become extremely efficient at identifying problems before they occur. But the same capability could also create unprecedented forms of social control.

这不意味着每个技术社会都会走向威权，而意味着，扩大控制的技术可能性，会改变政治格局。当一个社会获得大规模监测行为的能力，控制这种能力的机构，就变得至关重要。这引出了第六个，也许是最深的问题：人类适应自身造物的速度，可能太慢了。我们的制度，是为变化相对缓慢的世界建立的。制定法律可能需要数年。This does not mean every technological society will become authoritarian. It means the technical possibility of greater control changes the political landscape. When a society gains the ability to monitor behavior at enormous scale, the institutions controlling that ability become extraordinarily important. And this leads to the sixth and perhaps deepest problem. Humanity may adapt too slowly to its own creations. Our institutions were built for a world where change was comparatively slow. Laws can take years to develop.

教育体系改革可能需要数十年，政治辩论推进缓慢，人类心理变化得更慢。但技术未必遵守这些时间表。一个新算法可以在几周内改进，一套新软件可以在几个月内触达数百万用户，一种制造工艺可以跨行业传播。Educational systems can take decades to reform. Political debates move slowly. Human psychology changes even more slowly. Technology does not necessarily respect these timelines. A new algorithm can be improved in weeks. A new software system can reach millions of users in months. A manufacturing process can spread across industries.

未来五年可能揭示什么What the Next Five Years Could Reveal

甚至几乎瞬间传播到全球。这会形成一种不对称：人类制度以协商的速度前进，技术则可以以复制的速度前进。A globally almost instantly. This could an asymmetry. Human institutions move at the speed of negotiation. Technology can move at the speed of replication.

当这两种速度变得相差悬殊，制度就开始疲于应对那些已经发生的变化。等政府理解一项技术的后果，企业可能已经依赖它；等学校重新设计课程，孩子们可能早已向自动化系统学习；等社会开始讨论一项新技术该不该存在，数百万人可能已经在使用它。And when those speeds become dramatically different, institutions begin reacting to changes that have already happened. By the time a government understands the consequences of a technology, companies may already depend upon it. By the time schools redesign their curriculum, children may already be learning from automated systems. By the time society debates whether a new technology should exist, millions of people may already be using it.

因此，未来几年的核心挑战，不只是技术创新，而是技术治理。人类必须学会，不仅问什么能够被构建，还要问什么应该被构建、应该由谁……如果它取得了超出预期的成功，又会怎样？最危险的问题，往往不是一台机器能不能完成任务，而是社会会不会在理解依赖的后果以前，就已经依赖上了这台机器。This is why the central challenge of the coming years will not simply be technological innovation. It will be technological governance. Humanity must learn to ask not only what can be built, but what should be built, who should What happens if it succeeds beyond expectations? The most dangerous question is often not whether a machine can perform a task. It is whether society becomes dependent on that machine before understanding the consequences of dependence.

最后的思考Final Reflection

每一种强大技术都会创造新的可能性，也会创造新的脆弱性。一个文明联系越紧密，信息和资源流动就越高效，扰动扩散也越迅速。高度自动化的社会能够创造非凡财富，但也可能高度依赖复杂系统。当这些系统完美运转，生活更轻松；当它们失败，后果就可能沿着整个网络蔓延。Every powerful technology creates new possibilities, but it also creates new forms of vulnerability. The more interconnected a civilization becomes, the more efficiently information and resources can move through it, and the more rapidly disruptions can spread. A highly automated society can produce extraordinary wealth, but it may also become highly dependent upon complex systems. When those systems function perfectly, life becomes easier. When they fail, the consequences can spread across entire networks.

因此，目标不应该是拒绝技术。人类无法简单地回到过去。真正的挑战，是在技术力量仍在发展时，就建立起能够理解它的制度。教育必须从记忆信息，转向学习怎样质疑信息；领导者必须提高技术素养；公民必须足够理解算法，能够识别便利何时正在变成依赖；社会必须保留人类可以作出决定的空间，即使机器看起来更快。因为未来不只取决于机器的智能，还取决于人类的智慧——由人来决定，这种智能应当被……Therefore, the goal should not be to reject technology. Humanity cannot simply return to an earlier age. The challenge is to build institutions capable of understanding technological power while that power is still developing, education must change from memorizing information to learning how to question information. Leadership must become more technologically literate. Citizens must understand enough about algorithms to recognize when convenience is becoming dependency. And societies must preserve spaces where humans can make decisions even machines appear faster. Because the future will not be determined only by the intelligence of machines. It will be determined by the wisdom of the humans who decide where that intelligence be

摘要Abstract

创建一种编程语言，需要有指导原则为开发者指明目标。本文阐述了 Racket 二十年发展背后的三项基本原则。第一，编程就是陈述问题并求解，而这项活动通常发生在拥有自身话语体系的语境中；优秀的程序员应当把这种话语体系表达成编程语言。因此，Racket 是一种用于创建新编程语言的编程语言。第二，遵循这种面向语言的编程方式，系统就会成为由相互连接的组件构成的多语言集合。每种语言、每个组件，都必须能够保护各自的不变量。为此，Racket 提供保护机制，支持从 C 级位操作到具有健全类型系统的扩展这一完整的语言谱系。第三，既然 Racket 把编程视为使用恰当语言解决问题，它也会将语言之外的机制转变为语言构造，尤其是管理资源和项目的机制。本文解释这些原则及 Racket 如何践行它们，介绍设计过程背后的评估框架，最后概述 Racket 的不足以及未来改进的机会。The creation of a programming language calls for guiding principles that point the developers to goals. This article spells out the three basic principles behind the 20-year development of Racket. First, programming is about stating and solving problems, and this activity normally takes place in a context with its own language of discourse; good programmers ought to formulate this language as a programming language. Hence, Racket is a programming language for creating new programming languages. Second, by following this language-oriented approach to programming, systems become multi-lingual collections of interconnected components. Each language and component must be able to protect its specific invariants. In support, Racket offers protection mechanisms to implement a full language spectrum, from C-level bit manipulation to soundly typed extensions. Third, because Racket considers programming as problem solving in the correct language, Racket also turns extra-linguistic mechanisms into linguistic constructs, especially mechanisms for managing resources and projects. The paper explains these principles and how Racket lives up to them, presents the evaluation framework behind the design process, and concludes with a sketch of Racket’s imperfections and opportunities for future improvements.

1 从历史看 Racket1 Racket, Historically Speaking

1995 年，我们开始创建一个面向编程初学者的推广项目。经过一整年在实验室里观察学生，我们认识到：不可能用一小时教会 Scheme，然后就专注于计算和编程的本质。这与教师中一种广为流传的看法相反；那种看法源于许多基于 MIT《计算机程序的构造和解释》的课程（Abelson 等，1985）。真正需要的，是适合教授初学者的教学语言。此外，虽然 emacs、vi 等编辑器提供了专门用于编辑 Scheme 和与之交互的模式，但它们仍令从未编过程序的学生不知所措。因此，我们还希望为初学者提供一个教学编程环境，而不只是把专业人员使用的强大工具改作教学之用。In 1995, we set out to create an outreach project for novice programmers. After observing students in labs over the course of a year, we understood that nobody could teach Scheme in an hour and then focus on the essence of computing and programming—contrary to an opinion widely held by instructors due to the numerous courses based on MIT’s Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs (Abelson et al. 1985). What was needed, instead, was a teaching language suitable for instructing beginners. Furthermore, emacs, vi, and similar editors were overwhelming students who had never programmed before, despite their special modes for editing and interacting with Scheme. We therefore also wanted a pedagogical programming environment for our beginners, not just a re-appropriated power tool for professionals.

决定实现自己的教学语言及其环境后，我们仍希望证明，Scheme 是实现这些项目的优秀工具。我们认为，Scheme 的宏系统有助于试验语言设计。它看起来也非常适合构建一个简单的交互式开发环境（IDE）；毕竟，许多 Lisp 课程都讲授如何创建“读取—求值—打印”循环，而 emacs 的很大一部分也是用 Lisp 编写的。After we decided to implement our own teaching language and its environment, we still wanted to demonstrate that Scheme was an excellent implementation vehicle for these projects. We thought Scheme’s macro system would help us experiment with language designs. The language also appeared to be a perfect match for constructing a simple interactive development environment (IDE); after all, many Lisp courses taught how to create a read-eval-print loop, and a lot of emacs was written in Lisp.

事实证明，以 Scheme 为起点是可以接受的选择，但我们很快发现，所需的东西远不止这些。仅仅提供一种卫生的、面向模式的 Lisp 传统宏变体，并不足够。同样，开发大型系统让我们看清了“真正具有安全的抽象”与“用 lambda 模仿安全抽象”之间的区别。最后，实现 IDE 还要求在我们自己的程序控制下执行任意程序；这个目标使我们认识到，Scheme 并未提供管理资源、确保宿主程序安全所需的手段。Using Scheme as a starting point turned out to be an acceptable choice, but we soon found we needed a lot more. We discovered that more was needed than a hygienic and pattern-oriented variant of Lisp’s old macros. In the same vein, the development of a large system exposed the difference between having safe abstractions and mimicking them via lambda. Finally, implementing an IDE also called for executing arbitrary programs under the control of our own program—and this goal clarified that Scheme did not come with the means for managing resources and for ensuring the security of the hosting program.

随着时间推移，我们不断改造 Scheme，使之满足自身需要。我们在 Scheme 宏之上构建语法扩展系统，加入创建安全抽象的机制，并把外围操作系统的功能转化为语言构造，从而能够通过编程实现资源管理器和安全容器。渐渐地，这门语言成为软件工程师可用于实际工作的完备工具。到 2010 年，我们的 Scheme 方言已经演变得如此不同，于是将它改名为 Racket（Flatt 与 PLT，2010），向外界表明：我们提供的已经是不同的东西了。Over time, we adapted Scheme to serve our needs. We built a syntax extension system on top of Scheme’s macros, added mechanisms for the creation of safe abstractions, and turned features of the surrounding operating system into linguistic constructs so that we could program resource administrators and security containers. In time, our language became a full-fledged tool for the working software engineer. By 2010, our dialect of Scheme had evolved so much that we renamed it to Racket (Flatt and PLT 2010) to let the world know that we had something different.

2 Racket 的原则2 The Principles of Racket

我们已经在其他文献中报告过这个项目的教学方面（Felleisen 等，2004）。本文则介绍 Racket 背后的设计原则，并通过具体例子说明，这些原则如何影响其实际实现。我们把这些原则概括为三句口号：While we have reported on the pedagogic aspect of the project elsewhere (Felleisen et al. 2004), this paper presents the design principles behind Racket and illustrates with concrete examples how they affect the reality of its implementation. It groups these principles under three slogans:

  1. Racket 致力于快速创建新的编程语言。Racket is about creating new programming languages quickly.

    编程是一种解决问题的活动。恰当的做法，是使用问题领域的语言来陈述问题，并表达求解过程。为支持这种编程方式，Racket 帮助程序员创建并迅速部署新语言。尤其重要的是，创建和部署语言的机制，必须包含在语言自身之内。安装 Racket 后，使用任何一种基于它的新语言，都不应再需要走到语言之外。这与许多用于创建嵌入式领域专用语言的外部工具和命令行预处理器，形成鲜明对比。Programming is a form of problem solving. A proper approach uses the language of the domain to state the problem and to articulate solution processes. In support of this mode of programming, Racket helps programmers create and quickly deploy new languages. In particular, the mechanisms for creating and deploying languages must be contained within the language itself. Once Racket is installed, there must be no need to step outside to use one of its new languages. This principle is in stark contrast to the numerous external tools and command-line pre-processors that are used to create (embedded) domain-specific languages.

  2. Racket 为强有力的保护机制提供构建模块。Racket provides building blocks for strong protection mechanisms.

    如果编程就是用恰当的语言解决问题，那么系统必然由使用若干不同语言、相互连接的组件构成。由于这些连接，值会从一种语言语境流入另一种。语言有责任提供并维持不变量，因此，语言的创建者必须有能力保护这些不变量。这意味着，Racket 必须提供相应机制，使程序员能够保护单个组件，免受其使用方的破坏。If programming is about solving problems in the correct language, systems will necessarily consist of interconnected components in several different languages. Due to the connections, values flow from one linguistic context into another. Since languages are charged with providing and preserving invariants, the creators of languages must have the power to protect the languages’ invariants. By implication, Racket must come with mechanisms that enable programmers to protect individual components from their clients.

    因此，Racket 提供恰当的基础构件，让人们能够在任何层次建立或构造保护机制：从 C，一直到拥有健全高阶类型系统的语言，以及两者之间的任意混合形式。For this reason, Racket comes with the proper building blocks to set up or construct protection mechanisms at any level, all the way from C to languages with sound, higher-order type systems, and any mixture in between.

  3. Racket 将语言之外的机制转变为语言构造。Racket turns extra-linguistic mechanisms into linguistic constructs.

    当程序员不得不依靠语言之外的机制来解决问题时，就说明所选语言未能满足需求。即使解决办法并非总是显而易见，编程语言研究者也应接受这个总体思路，并努力寻找恰当的语言机制。由于 Racket 自身的应用需求，这门语言目前已经把若干通常位于底层操作系统中的资源管理机制内化进来。同样，这种理念也排斥其他 IDE 中的“项目”概念，因为它同样把资源管理、链接及程序创建的其他方面放到了语言之外。When programmers must resort to extra-linguistic mechanisms to solve a problem, the chosen language has failed them. Even if it is not always obvious how to fix such failures, programming language researchers ought to accept the general idea and try to work on finding the proper linguistic mechanisms. Due to Racket’s uses, the language currently internalizes several resource-management mechanisms that are often found in the underlying operating system. Similarly, this philosophy prohibits the idea of “projects,” as found in other IDEs, because this also externalizes resource management, linking, and other aspects of program creation.

要评估这些原则的运用，必须建立一个反馈循环，而其范围不能仅限于语言编译器。对于 Racket，这个循环的评估阶段包括多种软件系统，尤其是 Racket 的 IDE——DrRacket（Findler 等，2002）。Evaluating the use of such principles must take place in a feedback loop that encompasses more than the compiler for the language. In Racket’s case, the feedback loop’s evaluation stage contains a range of software systems, especially DrRacket (Findler et al. 2002), the Racket IDE.

接下来的三节将深入解释这些原则：面向语言的编程、支持全谱系编程的保护机制，以及将服务变成语言构造。第六节较详细地介绍 Racket 的设计反馈循环，以及它如何帮助我们按照这些准则把原则变成现实。最后，结论部分将这些原则放在整体背景中审视，特别指出哪些方面仍只是目标，以及达到这些目标还需要哪些研究。The next three sections explain the principles in depth: language-oriented programming, protection mechanisms for full-spectrum programming, and services-as-constructs. The sixth introduces Racket’s design feedback loop in some detail and how it helps us use the guidelines to turn principles into reality. Finally the conclusions puts the principles in perspective, pointing out in particular where they remain goals and the research needed to reach those goals.

3 Racket 是一种用于编写编程语言的编程语言3 Racket is a Programming-Language Programming Language

Racket 是一种编程语言。实际上，乍看之下，它更像一个传统语言家族：其中包括一种小巧、无类型、以函数式为主且按值求值的语言 racket/base，一个功能齐备的扩展 racket，以及一个带类型的变体 typed/racket。Racket is a programming language. Actually, at first glance it looks like a family of conventional languages, including a small untyped, mostly-functional by-value language (racket/base), a batteries-included extension (racket), and a typed variant (typed/racket).

#lang racket
 
(provide
  ; type Video = [Listof Image]
  ; Natural -> Video
  walk-simplex)
 
; IMPLEMENTATION
(require "small.sim" 2htdp/image)
 
; Natural -> Video
(define (walk-simplex timing)
  ... (maximizer #:x 2) ...)

图 1：一个普通的 Racket 模块Figure 1: A plain Racket module

与所有编程语言一样，普通 Racket 要求程序员用其内置的编程构造来表达问题的解法。但 Racket 也是 Lisp 家族的一员，而 Lisp 一直主张使用最恰当、最适合问题领域的语言陈述解法。正如 Hudak 所说（Hudak，1998）：“领域专用语言是终极抽象。”Like all programming languages, plain Racket forces the programmer to formulate solutions to problems in terms of its built-in programming constructs. But, Racket is also a member of the Lisp family, which has always insisted on stating solutions in the most appropriate language, one suited to the problem domain. As Hudak (Hudak 1998) puts it, “domain-specific languages are the ultimate abstractions.”

按照这一思路，每个程序组件都应使用最适合其所解决问题的、基于 Racket 的编程语言来表达。如果这种语言尚不存在，Racket 程序员就创建它，哪怕它可能只供一个模块使用。为支持这种系统构建方式，Racket 本身就是一种用于编写编程语言的编程语言。Following this reasoning, each program component is articulated in the Racket-based programming language that is best suited for the problem it solves. If the language is not available, the Racket programmer creates it, possibly even for a single module. To support this kind of system building, Racket is a programming-language programming language.

#lang at-exp simplex
 
; provides: synthesized function maximizer:
;    #:x Real -> Real
;    #:y Real -> Real
 
#:variables x y
 
3 * x + 5 * y <= 10
3 * x - 5 * y <= 20

图 1 和图 2 说明了这个原则。图 1 的第一个模块使用 racket 语言，通过所谓的 #lang 行指定；#lang 读作“hash lang”。该模块提供一个函数；provide 声明中的注释，以非形式化方式给出一个类型定义，以及基于该类型定义的函数签名。为实现这个函数，模块使用 (require "small.sim") 导入图 2 模块提供的功能，再定义自己的函数。Figure 1 and Figure 2 illustrate the principle. The first module in figure 1 uses the racket language, which is specified in the so-called #lang—pronounced “hash lang”—line. The module provides a single function; the comments inside the provide specification informally state a type definition and a function signature in terms of this type definition. To implement this function, the module uses (require "small.sim") to import functionality from the module in figure 2 and then defines its own functions.

图中标出了文件名。它不属于代码的一部分。细心的读者会发现，这违反了“把一切保留在语言之内”的原则。The figure specifies the filename. It is not a part of the code. The astute reader will notice that this violates the principle of keeping everything in the language.

图 2 模块的创建者更偏好领域专用语言，因为这个模块的目的是为一个单纯形合成函数，而描述单纯形最自然的方式，就是给出一组线性不等式。图 2 中 #lang 行下面的注释说明，模块导出一个函数 maximizer。具体而言，#:variables 声明及其后的不等式共同决定这个 maximizer 函数。以 (maximizer #:x n) 调用时，函数给出最大的 y 值；反过来，(maximizer #:y m) 则给出最大的 x 值。The creator of the module in figure 2 prefers a domain-specific language, because the module’s purpose is to synthesize a function for a simplex, and the most natural way to specify the latter is to state a collection of linear inequalities. The comments below the #lang line in figure 2 state that the module exports a single function, maximizer. Concretely, the #:variables specification and the following inequalities determine the maximizer function. When called as (maximizer #:x n), the function produces the maximal y value; conversely, (maximizer #:y m) delivers the maximal x value.

为支持这种面向语言的编程，Racket 提供了一个语法扩展系统。它借鉴了 Scheme 宏系统的一些元素（Dybvig 等，1992；Kohlbecker 等，1986；Kohlbecker 与 Wand，1987），同时又从多个方向加以改进。第一，Racket 的语法扩展系统着眼于定义语言（Flatt，2002；Krishnamurthi，2001；Krishnamurthi 等，1999），而不只是向已有语言添加新的语言构造。例如，Racket 的类系统（Flatt 等，2006）、一等组件（Flatt 与 Felleisen，1998），以及循环推导式语言，就是这样的子语言，尽管它们的构造看起来与 Racket 的核心功能并无区别。自然地，一种基于 Racket 的语言，本身就是一个模块；模块的导出组成一门新语言。这些导出必须包含某些特定功能，此外还可以包含任何被认为必要的语法构造和运行时值。模块可以自行定义这些导出，也可以从已有语言中导入，再重新导出。因此，语言模块可以轻松地为已有语言增加或删减功能。In support of this kind of language-oriented programming, Racket provides a syntax extension system that borrows elements from Scheme’s macro system (Dybvig et al. 1992; Kohlbecker et al. 1986; Kohlbecker and Wand 1987) but also improves on it in several different directions. First, the Racket syntax extension system is about defining languages (Flatt 2002; Krishnamurthi 2001; Krishnamurthi et al. 1999), not just extending an existing language with new linguistic constructs. For example, Racket’s class system (Flatt et al. 2006), its first-class components (Flatt and Felleisen 1998), and its language of (loop) comprehensions are just such sub-languages, though their constructs are indistinguishable from Racket’s core features. Naturally, a Racket-based language is just a module whose exports make up a new language. These exports must include certain features and may otherwise come with any syntactic constructs and run-time values deemed necessary. The module may define these exports or may import and re-export them from an existing language. Hence, a language module can easily add features to, or subtract them from, an existing language.

第二，语法扩展系统还允许语言模块重新定义已有构造的含义。以函数应用为例：与 Lisp 一样，Racket 的函数应用就是用一对括号括住函数及其参数：Second, the syntax extension system also allows a language module to redefine the meaning of existing constructs. Take function application, for example. Like Lisp, a Racket function application is just a pair of parentheses around the function and its arguments:

(f a ...)

Racket 的语法系统会把表层语法展开为核心语法：Racket’s syntax system elaborates surface syntax to kernel syntax:

(#%app f a ...)

关键字 #%app 是 Racket 内部标示函数应用语法的标记，而一种语言可以重新定义它的含义。下面是一个简化的重定义：The keyword #%app is Racket’s internal sign post for the function application syntax—and a language can re-define its meaning. Here is a simplistic re-definition:
#lang racket
(provide (rename-out [call #%app]) ...)
 
(define-syntax-rule
  (call f a ...)
  ; rewrites to
  (if (check-in-defines f) (#%app f a ...) (signal-error f a ...)))
这个模块定义了语法缩写 call。使用 call 时，它会展开成一个 if 表达式，检查 f 的某项属性：如果成立，就用导入的函数应用语法（高亮处）创建函数应用；否则就报告错误。导出时，call 被重命名为 #%app。这意味着，当其他模块指定本模块作为自己的语言时，编译器会使用 call 语法展开那些模块中的函数应用，例如：This module defines the syntactic abbreviation call. A use of call expands to an if expression that checks a property of f and, if it holds, uses the imported application syntax (high-light) to create a function application; otherwise it signals an error. On export, call is renamed to #%app, meaning when another module specifies this module as its language, the compiler uses the call syntax to elaborate the module’s function applications, e.g.,
(g b ...)
;  – compiles to–>
(#%app g b ...)
; == equivalent  ==
(call g b ...)
; – compiles to–>
(if (check-in-defines g) (#%app g b ...) (signal-error g b ...))
也就是说，最终代码仍然使用普通 racket 的 #%app 构造来求值函数应用，而这正是常规的按值调用式函数应用。That is, the final code uses plain racket’s #%app construct to evaluate the function application—and that is regular call-by-value function application.

这个例子受到教学语言的启发（Felleisen 等，2001）。具体来说，一阶函数式教学语言用它检查：函数位置上是否是由程序或语言定义的名称。这样，一旦出现其他东西，就能给出适合初学者的错误消息。不过，这个模式的用途远不止于此。例如，FrTime 语言使用同样的机制，创建了按值调用的数据流变体（Cooper 与 Krishnamurthi，2006）。This example is inspired by the teaching languages (Felleisen et al. 2001). In particular, the first-order functional teaching language uses it to check whether the function position is a name defined by the program or the language so that it can produce novice-friendly error messages when something else shows up. However, the pattern is used much more widely. For instance, the FrTime language uses this same mechanism to create a dataflow variant of call-by-value (Cooper and Krishnamurthi 2006).

第三，Racket 的语法扩展系统允许定义语言的模块访问使用该语言的模块的整棵语法树，而不只是其中的单个节点。这使上例中针对 #%app 和 define 的重写规则能够协作。这种通信方式可以自然推广到复杂的上下文相关分析任务，尤其能够用于实现一种相当常规的类型检查器（Tobin-Hochstadt 等，2011）。Third, Racket’s syntax extension system grants a language-defining module access to the entire syntax tree for a guest module, not just individual nodes in the syntax tree. This access allows the collaboration between the rewriting rule for #%app and define in the above example. Indeed, this kind of communication smoothly generalizes to complex context-sensitive analysis tasks and, in particular, allows for the implementation of a rather conventional type checker (Tobin-Hochstadt et al. 2011).

第四，Racket 自带支持以编程方式创建词法分析器和语法分析器的库（Owens 等，2004）。因此，语言实现可以把传统语法转换为常规的 S 表达式语法，再交给通常的语法扩展系统及其重写规则处理。图 2 就是一个例子：领域专用语言的实现者更喜欢使用 ASCII 数学记法。重要的是，把语法分析与语法扩展分开，自然地为语言设计中互不相干的两个部分——记法与含义——建立了接口，从而使语言工程师能够把工作分解成两个独立组件：表层记法的设计，以及含义的设计。Fourth, Racket comes with a library that supports the programmatic creation of lexers and parsers (Owens et al. 2004). It is thus possible for a language implementation to transform conventional syntax into regular S-expression syntax and to subject this result to the conventional syntax extension system and its rewriting rules. See figure 2 where the implementor of a domain-specific language prefers an ASCII-mathematics notation. Importantly, the separation of parsing from the syntax extension naturally creates an interface between unrelated parts of language design—notation and meaning—and thus enables language engineers to factor the work into two independent components: design of surface notation and meaning.

最后，Racket 坚持区分语言处理的各个阶段，尤其严格区分编译时代码和运行时代码。例如，重写规则生成纯粹的语法，不能把其他语言值嵌入语法之中。类似地，由于 Racket 的语言世界实际上是一个语言层叠在语言之上的倒金字塔，每个语言处理模块都可能产生副作用；这些副作用必须与语言处理流水线的其余部分隔离。Finally, Racket insists on separating the various stages of language processing, particularly enforcing a strict separation of compile-time from run-time code. For example, the rewriting rules generate pure syntax and may not embed other language values inside this syntax. Similarly, since the world of Racket languages is actually an inverted pyramid of languages atop languages, each language-processing module may have side-effects—and these side-effects must be insulated from the rest of the language-processing pipeline.

总之，Racket 的工具箱使程序员能够快速创建新语言，从而实现面向语言的程序设计。关键在于对 Lisp 和 Scheme 的方式作出改进：Racket 谨慎地安排语法展开的阶段（Flatt，2002），消除了 Lisp 那种存在问题的 eval-when-where 方式；它支持从已有语言快速派生新语言，也支持引入传统语法。In sum, Racket’s toolbox empowers programmers to create new languages quickly and thus enables language-oriented program design. The key to this achievement is to improve over Lisp and Scheme’s approaches: Racket carefully stages syntax elaboration (Flatt 2002), eliminating Lisp’s problematic eval-when-where approach; it enables the quick derivation of new languages from existing ones; and it enables the introduction of conventional syntax.

4 Racket 覆盖完整的编程语言谱系4 Racket Covers a Full Programming Language Spectrum

抽象要强制维持不变量。语言也是抽象，其创建者必须拥有构建必要约束机制的手段；尤其当使用这些语言编写的组件，最终进入相互连接的多语言环境时，更是如此。既然 Racket 是用于构建编程语言的语言，它也提供构建这些约束机制的基础构件。实际上，Racket 的基础构件可以创建一个语言谱系，而程序员能够安全地组合使用这一谱系中不同语言编写的组件。An abstraction enforces invariants. Languages are abstractions, and their creators must have the means to build the necessary enforcement mechanisms—especially when components in these languages end up in interconnected, multi-lingual contexts. Since Racket is a language for building programming languages, it supplies the building blocks for the construction of enforcement mechanisms, too. Indeed, Racket’s building blocks allow the creation of a spectrum of languages, and Racket programmers may safely compose components written in various elements of this spectrum.

虽然 Racket 不会自动保护这种值的流动，但它提供了构建不变量约束机制所需的工具。从技术上说，它提供了 Miller 的代理机制（Miller，2006），并针对 Racket 语言构建者的需要进行了调整。Racket 的代理分为 chaperone 和 impersonator 两个层次（Strickland 等，2012）。每一层都会监控对底层值的访问，以保证基本不变量。程序员可以创建定制代理，监控对函数、不可变值、可变结构和对象的访问，同时不妨碍对被包装的值和对象执行其他操作。While Racket does not automatically protect such flows of values, it comes with the tools to build invariant-enforcement mechanisms. Programmers can create customized proxies that monitor access to functions, immutable values, mutable structures and objects—without ever getting in the way of other operations on the wrapped values and objects.

Racket 基于代理机制实现了完整的契约系统。契约允许组件表达 Eiffel 风格的一阶断言（Meyer，1992）。引入契约边界后，这些一阶陈述便能自然推广到 Racket 的高阶环境。也就是说，通过契约，组件能够针对闭包（Findler 与 Felleisen，2002）、对象、类（Strickland 等，2013）和模块（Strickland 与 Felleisen，2009）等值，声明承诺与义务。Racket offers a comprehensive contract system implemented with the proxy mechanisms. The contracts allow components to express Eiffel-style first-order assertions (Meyer 1992). The introduction of contract boundaries smoothly generalizes these first-order statements to Racket’s higher-order setting. That is, with contracts a component can advertise promises and obligations on values such as closures (Findler and Felleisen 2002), objects, classes (Strickland et al. 2013), and modules (Strickland and Felleisen 2009).

目前，typed/racket（Tobin-Hochstadt 与 Felleisen，2008）是代理和契约最重要的使用者。从实现机制看，typed/racket 与 at-exp simplex 类似；但从目的看，这两种语言有根本区别：typed/racket 是 racket 的姊妹语言，而不只是 Racket 世界中实现的任意一种语言。如前所述，它旨在帮助程序员为无类型模块配备类型，从而加固这些模块。Currently, typed/racket (Tobin-Hochstadt and Felleisen 2008) is the most important client of proxies and contracts. From a mechanical perspective, typed/racket is like at-exp simplex. From the teleological one, the two languages radically differ from each other; typed/racket is a sibling of racket, not just an arbitrary language implemented in the Racket world. As mentioned, its purpose is to help programmers harden untyped modules by equipping them with types.

类型检查保证模块内部以及它与其他带类型模块之间的一致性；而把导出值的类型转换成运行时契约，则确保一种一般化的类型健全性，即 Tobin-Hochstadt 的归责定理。例如，当带类型模块向无类型模块导出一个处理整数的函数时，后者不能用字符串调用该函数。同样，如果一个接收整数值函数的函数，从带类型世界流入无类型世界，后者就必须承诺，不会把它应用于复数值函数。While type checking guarantees consistency within the module and with respect to other typed modules, translating types of exported values into run-time contracts ensures a general form of type soundness, known as Tobin-Hochstadt’s Blame Theorem. For example, when a typed module exports a function on integers to an untyped module, the latter must not apply the function to a string; similarly, if a function on integer-valued functions flows from the typed to the untyped world, the latter must promise not to apply it to complex functions.

作为一种基于库实现的语言，typed/racket 与 Racket 生态中的其他库处于同等地位。因此，它无法像 Java 或 OCaml 这样的带类型语言那样彻底地捍卫自身不变量。填补剩余漏洞、提供更完整的保证，仍是正在进行的研究。As a library-based language, typed/racket is on the same footing as other libraries in the Racket ecosystem. Thus it cannot defend its invariants as thoroughly as typed languages such as Java or OCaml. Closing the remaining loopholes to enable more complete guarantees is ongoing research.

While this translation is straightforward for the functional core, extending this work to Racket’s class-oriented fragment is the fruit of a multi-year research project (Takikawa et al. 2015; Takikawa et al. 2012). This extension implements both novel contract mechanisms for Racket’s first-class classes as well as critical performance enhancements in the types-to-contracts translator.

借助契约和类型，Racket 包含了完整的编程语言谱系。更重要的是，它允许程序员逐步将“脚本”加固成程序。最初，程序员可以编写传统意义上的“脚本”，也就是 C 库外面的一层薄封装。假如选择 C 并非出于关键性能需求，程序员就可以把代码迁移到 Racket，换取模块的运算安全性与内存安全性。第三步，是为库的导出添加契约，保护使用方与库之间的交互。最后，程序员可以为代码添加显式、可静态检查的类型，从而形成经过验证的文档，改善代码的性能和可维护性，还可能发现细微错误。图 7 用示意图概括了 Racket 语言谱系中的这一加固过程。With contracts and types, Racket includes a full spectrum of programming languages and, importantly, allows programmers to incrementally harden their “scripts” into programs. Assuming the choice of C is not performance-critical, the programmer could move the code to Racket, gaining operational and memory safety for the module in return. 尽管契约和类型在加固过程中都起着核心作用，但从语言角度看，typed/racket 的发展还有更深的意义。为 racket 配备类型系统，是一项富有挑战的任务。使用动态类型语言的程序员，在设计代码时，会在其上叠加自己的推理体系；把这种体系称为类型系统，是合理的。它往往类似朴素集合论，有时又融合了若干不同类型系统的元素。While both contracts and types play a central role in this hardening process, the development of typed/racket is far more interesting from a linguistic perspective. Equipping racket with a type system is a challenging task. Programmers who use dynamically typed languages superimpose their own reasoning system as they design their code. It is fair to call this reasoning system a type system. Often this informal type system resembles naive set theory; at other times it incorporates elements from several different type systems.

typed/racket 的设计挑战，是把所有这些非形式化的类型系统统一到一个框架中，同时不引入不兼容或矛盾。毕竟，程序员加固项目时，并不想为了迎合类型检查器而修改代码。更糟的是，这类修改可能引入错误，或者以不希望的方式改变程序行为。由于我们希望支持渐进且可选择的加固，typed/racket 保持 racket 的语义也至关重要。The design challenge for typed/racket is to bring all of these informal type systems together in one framework—without introducing incompatibilities and contradictions. After all, when programmers harden a project, they do not want to modify their code to accommodate the type checker. Worse, any such modification might introduce a mistake or change the behavior of the program in undesirable ways. Because of the desire to allow incremental and selective hardening, it is also critical for typed/racket to preserve the semantics of racket.

typed/racket 的类型系统设计，是一个复杂但独立的问题。全面讨论这一设计，并不能帮助解释 Racket 的设计原则，因此我们把这个主题留给未来的论文。The design of typed/racket’s type system is a complicated, but separate problem. A full discussion of this design would not illuminate the explanation of Racket’s design principles, which is why we reserve this topic for a future paper.

5 Racket 将语言之外的机制内化5 Racket Internalizes Extra-Linguistic Mechanisms

许多编程问题源自“现实”世界，但程序开发本身也是一个问题领域。因此，支持编程的工具，也理应拥有自己的语言。编译器作者认真对待这一想法：例如，Dybvig 及其团队开发了一种语言，将编译器表述成纳米级的微小转换，并用于教学（Sarkar 等，2005）和商业实践（Keep 与 Dybvig，2013）。然而，涉及程序开发或执行时，IDE 却往往依赖外围操作系统的机制：要求程序员在项目语境中开发程序，把执行委托给操作系统，并使用外部工具检查程序及其执行状态。Racket 强调语言是解决问题的关键，因此提出另一种方案：把这些语言之外的机制，转变为语言构造（Flatt 等，1999）。While many programming problems originate in a “real” world, program development is also a problem domain. As such, tools that support programming deserve a language of their own. Compiler writers take this idea seriously; for example, Dybvig and his group have developed a language for stating compilers as nano-scale transformations and used it for both educational (Sarkar et al. 2005) and commercial purposes (Keep and Dybvig 2013). When it comes to program development or program execution, however, IDEs resort to mechanisms from the surrounding operating system. They force programmers to develop programs in project contexts, delegate program execution to operating systems, and use external tools to inspect programs and their execution states. Racket’s focus on languages as the key to problem solving points to the alternative solution of turning these extra-linguistic mechanisms into linguistic constructs (Flatt et al. 1999).

这条准则并不意味着语言构造应该取代所有工具。它只是说，每当创建一种工具，都应追问：它究竟应该是工具，还是语言的一部分？有些任务最好交给工具，另一些则更适合通过语言扩展实现。找到良好平衡，是个困难的问题。例如，Racket 库或大型系统的创建者，完全可能希望按项目来思考；但初学者在编写并运行“hello world”之前，绝不应该先被要求执行“创建项目”。This guideline does not mean that linguistic constructs ought to replace all tools. It merely means that the creation of every tool must raise the question whether it should be a tool or a part of the language. Some tasks are best left to tools, others are better realized as extensions of the language. Finding a good balance is a difficult problem. For example, the creator of a Racket library or a large system may very well wish to think in terms of projects, but a novice student must never have to say “create project” before writing and running a “hello world” program.

要理解这个想法，可以回到最初的问题：为编程新手构建教学 IDE。显然，对教学和初学者的重视，意味着不能引入“项目”；学生应当不必先具备计算机知识，就能输入程序，并且不离开所用 IDE 就能运行程序。这意味着，IDE 要在自己的控制下运行学生程序。然而，学生会犯错，其中一个常见错误，就是启动一个发散、不终止的程序，不断消耗时间、内存或其他资源，例如文件端口、数据库句柄和网络连接；有时，这些访问还可能经过教师提供的库。类似地，初学者希望查找程序中的错误，教师也就希望演示如何逐步执行程序。最后，当学生向作业服务器提交程序时，该程序必须在受限制的安全环境中运行，不能窥探其他学生的解答、攻击服务器等。To appreciate this idea, consider the original problem of building a pedagogic IDE for novice programmers. Clearly, the emphasis on pedagogy and novices prohibits the use of “projects;” students should be able to type in programs without any knowledge about computers and to run these programs without leaving the IDE they use. By implication, the IDE runs student programs under its control. Students make mistakes, though, and one common mistake is to launch a diverging program, that is, a program that consumes unbounded amounts of time, memory, or other resources (e.g., file ports, database handles, network connections; sometimes the access may be via instructor-provided libraries). Similarly, novices want to find mistakes in programs, meaning their instructors want to show them how to step through a program’s execution. Finally, when a student submits a program to some homework server, this program must run in a security context that prohibits it from inspecting other students’ solutions, attacking the server, and so on.

仔细审视这些要求，很快就会发现若干需要关注的领域。借助其设计反馈循环，Racket 目前已把下列外部机制变成语言构造：建立访问权限层级的检查器 inspector；可从外部关闭的线程 thread；限制服务访问的沙箱 sandbox；管理文件句柄、套接字和数据库连接的资源托管者 custodian；处理 GUI 资源和事件的事件空间 eventspace；以及其他一些机制。本节剩余部分概述其中两项能力——检查器和资源托管者——并说明，把它们放进语言内部，如何实现对检查和资源的细粒度控制。A close look at these requirements immediately suggests several areas of concern. Due to its design feedback loop, Racket includes the following external mechanisms as constructs at the moment: inspectors, which establish a hierarchy of access rights; threads that can be shut down from the outside; sandboxes, which restrict access to services; custodians, which manage file handles, sockets, and database connections; eventspaces, which deal with GUI resources and events; and several more. The remainder of this section sketches two of these capabilities—inspectors and custodians—and how providing them inside the language provides fine-grained control over inspection and resources.

最后，Racket 还内化了其运行语境的其他方面。从一开始，Racket 程序就能以编程方式链接模块（Flatt 与 Felleisen，1998）和类（Flatt 等，1998）。在传统语言中，程序员必须依赖语言之外的工具，才能对这些语言构造进行抽象；只有 ML 风格的语言和某些脚本语言，也让模块与类成为可编程操作的对象。Finally, Racket also internalizes other aspects of its context. Dating back to the beginning, Racket programs can programmatically link modules (Flatt and Felleisen 1998) and classes (Flatt et al. 1998). In conventional languages, programmers must resort to extra-linguistic tools to abstract over such linguistic constructs; only ML-style languages and some scripting languages make modules and classes programmable, too.

6 Racket 的设计反馈循环6 The Racket Design Feedback Loop

语言设计必须发生在反馈循环中。与许多编程语言一样，Racket 的反馈循环包含健全性定理（Takikawa 等，2012）、性能评估（Takikawa 等，2015）和可用性研究（Marceau 等，2011）。然而，如图 11 所示，Racket 的反馈循环还包括若干软件应用。前面几节已经表明，DrRacket 对发展、打磨和检验这些原则发挥了作用；而创建 Redex（Felleisen 等，2010；Matthews 等，2004）、Scribble（Flatt 等，2009）、Slideshow（Findler 与 Flatt，2006）等大型复杂的领域专用语言，也产生了同样重要的影响。The design of a language must take place in the context of a feedback loop. Like the feedback loop for many programming languages, Racket’s feedback loop contains soundness theorems (Takikawa et al. 2012), performance evaluations (Takikawa et al. 2015), and usability studies (Marceau et al. 2011). As figure 11 shows, however, the Racket feedback loop also includes a number of software applications. While the preceding sections already indicate the role that DrRacket played for developing, honing, and checking the principles, the creation of large and complex domain-specific languages such as Redex (Felleisen et al. 2010; Matthews et al. 2004), Scribble (Flatt et al. 2009), Slideshow (Findler and Flatt 2006), and others have had an equally significant impact.

Redex 很可能是 Racket 语法扩展系统最复杂的使用者。它在两个层次使用该系统：一是编译 Redex 用于定义文法、类型和语义的语言，二是把该系统用作编译目标。因此，Redex 不断推动语法扩展系统拓展自身边界。Redex is probably the most sophisticated client of Racket’s syntax extension system. It employs the latter at two levels: to compile the Redex language of grammar, type, and semantics definitions and as a target for the compilation. As such, Redex has stretched and expanded the syntax extension system.

Scribble 是一种用于创建 Racket 文档的领域专用语言。与传统语言的文档系统不同，Scribble 程序可以引用 Racket 库中的绑定，并使用这些绑定进行计算。因此，程序员能够方便地创建交叉引用非常密集的手册、语言指南和书籍，让标识符的每一次出现都自动链接到其文档。实际上，Scribble 文件本身就是 Racket 模块，所以 Scribble 文档拥有其他 Racket 代码的一切优势，包括独立编译；许多文档标记处理器都没有这项功能。将 Scribble 与 Racket 集成，暴露了 Racket 语法扩展系统阶段分离模型中的缺口，进而迫使这个模型扩展（Flatt，2002）。Scribble is a domain-specific language for creating Racket documentation. Unlike the documentation system of conventional languages, a Scribble program can refer to, and compute with, bindings from a Racket library. As a result, programmers can easily create intensively cross-referencing manuals, language guides, and books in such a way that each occurrence of an identifier is automatically linked to its documentation. In fact, a Scribble file is just a Racket module, so Scribble documents come with all the benefits of other Racket code—including separate compilation, a feature absent in numerous document markup processors. Integrating Scribble with Racket exposed a gap in, and thus forced an expansion of, the phase separation model of Racket’s syntax extension system (Flatt 2002).

最后，Slideshow 既是一种用于编写演示文稿的领域专用语言，也是一种用于展示演示文稿的图形工具。对于懂得关注话语体系的语言设计者来说，设计一种以编程方式创建演示文稿的语言，是很自然的一步。演讲者希望有单一控制点，希望参数化复用幻灯片、幻灯片元素及其他概念。这些东西很容易用语言表达，却很难从所见即所得工具中获得。身兼演讲者的程序员，还会自然地希望在演示中求值代码，甚至求值演示文稿本身（Flatt 等，1999）。正因这些因素结合，Slideshow 的构建在设计反馈循环中所起的作用，几乎与 DrRacket IDE 相同。Finally, Slideshow is both a domain-specific language for programming presentations and a graphical tool for displaying them. For a linguist with an awareness of the language of discourse, designing a language for the programmatic creation of presentations is a natural step. Presenters want a single point of control: they want parametrized re-use of slides, slide elements, and other concepts that are most easily expressed with a language but are difficult to obtain in a WYSIWYG tool. A programmer-as-presenter also has the natural desire to evaluate code within a presentation, possibly even the presentation itself (Flatt et al. 1999). Because of this combination, Slideshow’s construction plays almost the same role in the feedback loop of design as the DrRacket IDE.

总的来说，这些应用都在挑战解决问题的程序员身上的语言设计者。每个应用都会带来若干不同类型的问题，最适合用针对问题的语言来表述和解决。在所有这些情形中，语言的目的都是提供一种受到保护、得到强制落实的抽象。同样重要的是，它们都需要细粒度控制那些通常位于语言之外的资源管理机制。这些应用的存在及其设计，既印证了 Racket 的原则，也展示了这些原则。因此，Racket 社区成员在贡献新语言、或向已有语言引入新概念时，经常会参考这些应用。In general, all of these applications challenge the linguist in the problem-solving programmer. Each poses several different kinds of problems, best articulated and solved with problem-specific languages. In all cases, the purpose of the languages is to provide a protected and enforced abstraction. Equally important, they all need fine-grained control over resource-management mechanisms that are usually found outside of the language. Their existence and their designs both confirm the Racket principles and illustrate them, so Racketeers frequently consult these applications when they contribute new languages or new concepts to existing languages in the realm of Racket.

7 Racket 的未来：从不足到研究机会7 Racket, the Future: From Imperfections to Research Opportunities

Racket 的设计原则，已经造就了一种具备以下能力的编程语言：Racket’s design principles have produced a programming language that

  • 能够针对特定问题领域快速创建新语言，从而实现面向语言的编程；enables the rapid creation of new languages for specific problem areas and thus enables language-oriented programming;

  • 支持完整的通用编程语言谱系，涵盖传统上各种不同程度的安全性；supports a full spectrum of general-purpose programming languages with various conventional degrees of safety; and

  • 把系统语境中的机制内化为语言构造，以实现细粒度、可编程的控制。internalizes mechanisms from its system context into linguistic constructs for fine-grained, programmable control.

把原则变成现实，几乎总会产生一个不完整、甚至可能存在缺陷的产品。Racket 也不例外，但我们把这些不足视为未来研究的机会。本文余下部分概述其中一些机会。Turning principles into reality almost always yields an incomplete, and possibly even flawed, product. Racket is no exception, but we consider these imperfections as opportunities for future research. The remainder of this paper sketches some of them.
同样的提醒不仅适用于设计结果，也适用于设计过程；不过，讨论设计过程的优缺点，超出了本文范围。The same caveat applies to the design process, not only its result, but covering the positives and negatives of the design process is beyond the scope of this paper.

Racket 的核心优势是语法扩展系统。它能让有经验的程序员获得极高的生产力，但学习曲线也异常陡峭。它的语法展开算法难以理解，工具箱庞大而复杂，还存在一些脆弱、尚未探索清楚的角落，偶尔连有经验的程序员也会被绊倒。这要求我们简化语法系统，并从工具与文档两方面，为工具箱建立一条平缓的入门路径。Racket’s key advantage is its syntax extension system. It makes experienced programmers extremely productive, but it comes with an extraordinarily steep learning curve. Its syntax elaboration algorithm is hard to understand; its toolbox is large and complex; and it has some brittle, unexplored corners that occasionally trip up even experienced programmers. The situation calls for simplifications of the syntax system and for the creation of a smooth ramp for the toolbox (in terms of both tools and documentation).

此外，语法扩展系统不支持关注点分离，程序也因此受到影响。例如，许多编程语言允许把规格说明与实现分开。在常规 Racket 中，契约扮演规格说明的角色，函数负责实现，程序员可以选择在模块中分离这两方面。但语法系统尚无这种分离。虽然 Culpepper 的博士研究（Culpepper 与 Felleisen，2010）已经朝这个方向取得一些进展，但要把语法规格与语法实现分离，仍需大量工作。实现这两项目标，将显著增强 Racket 对面向语言编程原则的支持。In addition, the syntax extension system does not allow for a separation of concerns, and programs suffer from this. For example, many programming languages allow programmers to separate specifications from implementations. In conventional Racket, contracts play the role of specifications, functions implement them, and programmers may choose to separate the two concerns in a module. No such separation exists for the syntax system. While Culpepper’s dissertation (Culpepper and Felleisen 2010) research has made some progress in this direction, a lot more work on separating syntax specifications from syntax implementations is needed. Realizing both will greatly improve Racket’s support for the principle of language-oriented programming.

现代程序员需要的不只是一种语言，还需要一个生态系统。事实上，许多程序员会把语言与其生态系统等同起来。对 Racket 来说，这意味着，创建新语言时，也应该能够从 DrRacket 派生出相应的 IDE。在某种程度上，DrRacket 已经可以自动支持新语言，例如提供在线语法检查和简单重构操作。但对于语法制导的单步执行器等其他工具，这个过程仍需投入大量工作，而且缺乏指导与自动化支持。Besides a language, modern programmers need an ecosystem. Indeed, many programmers equate languages with their ecosystems. For Racket, this equation means that the creation of a new language ought to include the derivation of an IDE from DrRacket. To some extent, DrRacket can already support new languages automatically, e.g., with on-line syntax checking and simple refactoring actions. For other tools, such as a syntax-directed stepper, this process would need a significant amount of work and comes without guidance or automation.

Spoofax（Lennart 与 Visser，2010）提供了用于生成 IDE 工具的领域专用语言，但也依赖语言之外的机制。Spoofax (Lennart and Visser 2010) comes with domain-specific languages for the generation of IDE tools, but also relies on extra-linguistic mechanisms.

现有约束机制形成了传统编程语言的完整谱系：Typed Racket、带契约的 Racket、Racket，以及 ffi/unsafe Racket。这个谱系虽然富有表达力，但两端仍缺少能力。为全面控制自身语境，Racket 可能需要访问所有平台上的汇编语言，从硬件直到 Web 上的 JavaScript；但如何以可移植方式集成这种能力，目前尚不清楚。为充分发挥类型的力量，Racket 则需要配备依赖类型。Tobin-Hochstadt 及其 Typed Racket 团队正在朝这个方向迈出第一步，重点关注 typed/racket 中的数值约束。Racket 程序使用向量时，对应的带类型版本会检查写入和读出向量的值，但与 ML 或 Haskell 一样，下标索引仍留给运行时系统中的契约检查。把 Xi 与 Pfenning 的数值约束编程方式（Xi 与 Pfenning，1998）集成进 typed/racket，是超越普通类型的自然下一步。The currently available enforcement mechanisms give rise to a full spectrum of conventional programming languages: Typed Racket, Racket with contracts, Racket, and ffi/unsafe Racket. Although this spectrum is expressive, it lacks power at both ends. To achieve full control over its context, Racket probably needs access to assembly languages on all possible platforms (from hardware to the web’s JavaScript). How to integrate this power in a portable manner is unclear. To realize the full power of types, Racket will have to be equipped with dependent types. Tobin-Hochstadt and his Typed Racket group are currently working on first steps in this direction, focusing on numeric constraints in typed/racket. When a Racket program uses vectors, its corresponding typed variant type-checks what goes into these vectors and what comes out, but like ML or Haskell, indexing is left to a (contractual) check in the run-time system. Integrating Xi and Pfenning’s form of programming with numeric constraints (Xi and Pfenning 1998) into typed/racket is a natural step beyond plain types.

更一般地说，Racket 的语言谱系为程序员创造了一个多语言世界，只是它比当前实践中的多语言系统具有多得多的结构。尽管 Matthews 与 Findler 研究了多语言程序的基础（Matthews 与 Findler，2009），他们的理论仍只覆盖这个世界的一小部分。我们对 Racket 的研究，显然要求从多个方向扩展这些结果，包括 Racket 的按值与按名（或惰性）变体之间、普通类型与依赖类型变体之间的健全交互，等等。我们期待，通过研究如何在经过验证的代码与其他代码混合时保护前者，获得有关 Racket 安全机制的新见解。More generally, Racket’s spectrum of languages creates a multi-lingual world for programmers, though with far more structure than currently found in practice. Even though Matthews and Findler (Matthews and Findler 2009) have studied the basics of multi-lingual programs, their theory covers only a small part of this world. Our work on Racket clearly calls for extensions of this result in several directions, including the sound interaction between by-value and by-name (or lazy) variants of Racket, typed and dependently typed variants, and so on. We expect that studying how to protect verified code as it is co-mingled with other kinds of code will yield new insights into Racket’s safety mechanisms.

Racket 还必须拓宽视野，考虑安全问题：既把安全视为一种约束保障，也把它视为第三项原则的应用。沙箱虽然解决了在作业提交服务器中运行学生程序时的一些安全问题，但要妥善解决整个问题，就必须表达安全策略，并在系统中强制执行。Moore 等人近期提出了 Shill，一种在 Racket 之上实现的安全脚本语言（Moore 等，2014）。这项工作暴露了 Racket 的面向语言编程原则与实际实现之间的严重差距，也揭示了其安全保障方式中的缺口。我们再次把这些薄弱之处看作改进 Racket 的机会，并期待在不久的将来研究这些问题。Racket must also broaden its horizon and consider security concerns, both as an enforcement action but also as an application of the third principle. While sandboxes address some of the security concerns of running a student program in a homework submission server, properly addressing this problem calls for articulating security policies and enforcing them in a system. Moore, et al. (Moore et al. 2014) recently presented Shill, a secure scripting language implemented atop Racket. Their work exposed serious gaps between Racket’s principle of language-oriented programming and its implementation as well as in Racket’s approach to enforcing security. Once again, we consider these weaknesses an opportunity to improve Racket and expect to study these problems in the near future.

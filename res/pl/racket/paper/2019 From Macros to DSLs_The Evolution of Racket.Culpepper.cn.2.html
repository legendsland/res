摘要Abstract

Racket 语言倡导面向语言的编程风格。开发者创建许多领域特定语言，用它们编写程序，再通过 Racket 代码将这些程序组合起来。只有当小语言的创建与组合既简单又有效时，这种编程风格才能奏效。Racket 源于 Lisp，这或许会让人以为有宏就够了；然而，它的设计团队发现宏存在重大不足，不得不从许多方面加以改进。本文介绍 Racket 宏系统的演进，包括其中一次未能成功的尝试，并评估其现状。The Racket language promotes a language-oriented style of programming. Developers create many domain-specific languages, write programs in them, and compose these programs via Racket code. This style of programming can work only if creating and composing little languages is simple and effective. While Racket’s Lisp heritage might suggest that macros suffice, its design team discovered significant shortcomings and had to improve them in many ways. This paper presents the evolution of Racket’s macro system, including a false start, and assesses its current state.

1　宏与领域特定语言1 Macros and Domain-Specific Languages

《Racket 宣言》[20,21] 主张采用面向语言编程（language-oriented programming，LOP）[13,45] 的软件开发方式。其思想是认真对待 Hudak 所说的“语言[是]终极抽象”[33]，用领域特定语言（domain-specific languages，DSL）编程，就像使用所选语言内部的正规抽象一样。对于各种抽象，程序员都有相同的期待：创建 DSL，用它们编写程序，将这些程序嵌入底层语言的代码，并让它们相互通信。The Racket manifesto [20,21] argues for a language-oriented programming (LOP) [13,45] approach to software development. The idea is to take Hudak’s slogan of “languages [as] the ultimate abstractions” [33] seriously and to program with domain-specific languages (DSLs) as if they were proper abstractions within the chosen language. As with all kinds of abstractions, programmers wish to create DSLs, write programs in them, embed these programs in code of the underlying language, and have them communicate with each other.

按照 Lisp 的世界观，具有宏的语言尤其适合支持这一愿景。借助宏，程序员可以针对某个领域定制语言。由于用这些定制语言编写的程序位于宿主程序内部，它们很容易与宿主及彼此通信。简言之，创建、使用和组合 DSL 看起来都很容易。According to the Lisp worldview, a language with macros supports this vision particularly well. Using macros, programmers can tailor the language to a domain. Because programs in such tailored languages sit within host programs, they can easily communicate with the host and each other. In short, creating, using, and composing DSLs looks easy.

然而，只有宏还造不出 DSL——这是 Racket 团队在二十多年实现面向语言编程的工作中得到的教训。本文回顾 Racket 为支持面向语言编程而设计的语言机制的发展历程；以团队的经验为基础并不断提炼，提出对 DSL 支持机制的理想要求；同时评估 Racket 目前的能力在多大程度上满足这些要求。这段历史始于 Scheme 的卫生宏，而卫生宏又源于 Lisp（见第 2 节）。在一次未能成功的尝试之后（见第 4 节），Racket 设计者转向过程式卫生宏，并使其能够跨模块工作；他们还严格分离了展开期与运行期（见第 5 节）。最终，他们创建了一个用于编写宏的元 DSL，能够恰当地表达语言扩展的语法约束、检查这些约束，并合成合适的错误信息（见第 6 节）。将一般的 DSL 实现要求（见第 3 节）与 Racket 的能力比较，可以看出，对于某一类 DSL，Racket 的支持在若干方面仍有不足（见第 7 节）。Macros alone do not make DSLs, however, a lesson that the Racket team has learned over 20 years of working on a realization of language-oriented programming. This paper recounts Racket’s history of linguistic mechanisms designed to support language-oriented programming; it formulates desiderata for DSL support based on, and refined by, the Racket team’s experiences; it also assesses how well the desiderata are met by Racket’s current capabilities. The history begins with Scheme’s hygienic macros, which, in turn, derive from Lisp (see sec. 2). After a false start (see sec. 4), the Racket designers switched to procedural, hygienic macros and made them work across modules; they also strictly separated expansion time from run time (see sec. 5). Eventually, they created a meta-DSL for writing macros that could properly express the grammatical constraints of an extension, check them, and synthesize proper error messages (see sec. 6). A comparison between general DSL implementation desiderata (see sec. 3) and Racket’s capabilities shows that the language’s support for a certain class of DSLs still falls short in several ways (see sec. 7).

▶注。本文不讨论面向语言编程的安全问题。正如与本文作者大致相同的一组作者在《Racket 宣言》[20] 中所解释的那样，面向语言编程意味着：程序员使用一种宿主语言，安全地组合许多用不同 DSL 编写的小段代码，并用同一种宿主语言实现这些 DSL 本身。因此，面向语言编程显然需要安全链接 DSL 的工具（例如契约 [22] 或类型 [41]），也需要纳入系统级的保护机制（例如沙箱和资源托管器 [30]）。▶Note. This paper does not address the safety issues of language-oriented programming. As a similar set of authors explained in the Racket Manifesto [20], language-oriented programming means programmers use a host language to safely compose code from many small pieces written in many different DSLs and use the very same host language to implement the DSLs themselves. Hence language-oriented programming clearly needs the tools to link DSLs safely (e.g., via contracts [22] or types [41]) and to incorporate systems-level protection features (e.g., sandboxes and resource custodians [30]).

2　Lisp 与 Scheme 的前史2 The Lisp and Scheme Pre-history

Lisp 从 1963 年起就支持宏 [32]，Scheme 则于 1975 年从 Lisp 继承了宏 [43]。大体而言，Lisp 或 Scheme 的实现先使用读取器，将字符序列转换成一种具体的树形表示——S 表达式；再使用展开器得到抽象语法树（AST）。展开器遍历 S 表达式，寻找并消除宏的使用。宏把一个 S 表达式改写成另一个，展开器再重新遍历改写后的结果。一旦展开器发现某个节点使用核心语言的语法构造子，例如 lambda，它就进入该节点的各个分支，递归地展开。直到整个 S 表达式全部由核心构造子组成时，这一过程才结束。Lisp has supported macros since 1963 [32], and Scheme inherited them from Lisp in 1975 [43]. Roughly speaking, a Lisp or Scheme implementation uses a reader to turn the sequence of characters into a concrete tree representation: S-expressions. It then applies an expander to obtain the abstract syntax tree(s) (AST). The expander traverses the S-expression to find and eliminate uses of macros. A macro rewrites one S-expression into a different one, which the expander traverses afresh. Once the expander discovers a node with a syntax constructor from the core language, say lambda, it descends into its branches and recursively expands those. The process ends when the entire S-expression is made of core constructors.

宏极大地增强了 Lisp 的能力，但将宏表述为 S 表达式上的函数，既容易出错，也不方便。如图 2 所示，函数作者对输入 S 表达式的形状作出了一些假定，而宏展开过程并不保证这些假定成立。即便只需把假定形状的 S 表达式变换成形状正确的输出，也要编写繁琐的例行代码模式；宏作者必须自行管理这些模式，很容易发生遗漏和疏忽。Macros greatly enhance the power of Lisp, but their formulation as functions on S-expressions is both error-prone and inconvenient. 具体来看，let 宏可能遇到以下问题：
1. S 表达式可能是非真列表。按现有写法，变换器不会注意到这一问题，也就是说，编译过程会忽略对该语言扩展隐含语法的违反。
2. S 表达式可能过短；第二部分可能不是列表；即便是列表，其中也可能包含没有 cadr 字段的 S 表达式。这些情况下，宏变换器会抛出异常，编译过程随之中止。
3. S 表达式长度正确，但第二部分中的列表可能包含过多的 S 表达式。宏变换器同样会忽略这个问题。
4. 局部声明的 lhs 部分可能不是标识符；或者第二个子句的 lhs 重复使用了同一个标识符。这时宏仍会生成代码，指望编译过程的后续阶段发现问题。等到问题被发现时：
a. 可能已经无法根据源代码报告错误，程序员也就不知道该到哪里寻找语法错误。
b. 肯定已经无法用语言扩展本身的术语报告错误，程序员可能无法理解错误信息。
5. 宏作者可能忘记在 lhs 左侧写反引用符 ,。在 Lisp 家族的许多语言中，生成的代码在语法上仍然正确，但语义却与预期大不相同。尤其在传统 Lisp 中，这会生成一个函数，由新建的 lambda 绑定 body 中出现的所有 lhs——这显然违反了注释中所表达的作用域意图。
总之，如果 S 表达式不满足这些假定，宏变换就可能失败、忽略代码，或生成直到编译后续阶段才被认定有错、但错误描述又不恰当的代码。程序员即便只犯一个小错，也可能运行起奇怪的代码，并很可能造成难以解释的运行期错误。For concreteness, consider the following problems in the context of let macro: 1. The S-expression could be an improper list. The transformer, as written, does not notice such a problem, meaning the compilation process ignores this violation of the implied grammar of the language extension. 2. The S-expression could be too short. Its second part might not be a list. If it is a list, it may contain an S-expression without a cadr field. In these cases, the macro transformer raises an exception and the compilation process is aborted. 3. The S-expression has the correct length but its second part may contain lists that contain too many S-expressions. Once again, the macro transformer ignores this problem. 4. The S-expression may come with something other than an identifier as the lhs part of a local declaration. Or, it may repeat the same identifier as an lhs part of the second clause. In this case, the macro generates code anyways, relying on the rest of the compilation process to discover the problems. When these problems are discovered, a. it may have become impossible to report the error in terms of source code, meaning a programmer might not understand where to look for the syntax error. b. it has definitely become impossible to report errors in terms of the language extension, meaning a programmer might not comprehend the error message. 5. The author of the macro might forget the unquote , to the left of lhs. In many members of the Lisp family, the resulting code would be syntactically well formed but semantically rather different from the intended one. In particular, conventional Lisp would generate a function that binds all occurrences of lhs in body via this newly created lambda – a clear violation of the intended scoping arrangements expressed in the comments. In short, if the S-expression fails to live up to the stated assumptions, the macro transformation may break, ignore code, or generate code that some later step in the compilation process recognizes as an error but describes in inappropriate terms. If the programmer makes even a small mistake, strange code may run and is likely to cause inexplicable run-time errors.

Kohlbecker 在博士论文中对宏所作的研究大大改善了这一状况 [35,36,37]。他为 Scheme 84 设计的宏系统，给宏作者的工具箱增添了两个要素。第一个是用于表述宏变换的 DSL：将变换写成由模式和模板组成的改写规则。修改后的宏展开器用指定模式匹配 S 表达式；如果匹配成功，就用所得替换实例化模板。这个 DSL 从宏定义中消除了例行编程模式，并在一定程度上消除了上述第 1 至第 3 个问题。图 2 右侧给出了一个例子。Kohlbecker’s dissertation research on macros greatly improves this situation [35,36,37]. His macro system for Scheme 84 adds two elements to the macro writer’s toolbox. The first is a DSL for articulating macro transformations as rewriting rules consisting of a pattern and a template. The revised macro expander matches S-expressions against the specified patterns; if there is a match, the template is instantiated with the resulting substitution. This DSL removes programming patterns from macro definitions and, to some extent, eliminates problems 1 through 3 from above. For an example, see the right-hand side of fig. 2.

▶注。我们把 Lisp 风格的宏称为过程式宏，把 Kohlbecker 的方法称为声明式方法。▶Note. We call Lisp-style macros procedural and Kohlbecker’s approach declarative.

第二个新要素是卫生展开。默认情况下，Kohlbecker 的宏展开器认为，源代码中包含的标识符必须与宏在绑定位置生成的标识符相区分。这样一来，就不再需要显式防范宏的词法作用域与宏使用处上下文的作用域意外干扰——宏代码中又少了一种例行编程模式。至少，这种卫生展开器不会像上述第 5 个问题那样，绑定 body 中的 lhs。The second novel element is hygienic expansion. By default, Kohlbecker’s macro expander assumes that identifiers contained in the source must be distinct from macro-generated identifiers in binding positions. As such, it eliminates the need to explicitly protect against accidental interference between the macro’s lexical scopes and those of its use contexts – that is, yet another programming pattern from macro code. At a minimum, this hygienic expander would not bind lhs in body as indicated in problem 5 above.

后续工作 [2,8,15] 进一步完善了面向模式的宏描述方法和卫生宏展开。到 20 世纪 90 年代末，define-syntax-rule 构造和卫生展开已成为 Scheme 标准的一部分 [1]。从 1988 年开始，与 Scheme 标准化进程并行，Dybvig 等人 [15] 设计并实现了一个宏定义构造 define-syntax-cases（实际代码中需要组合使用 define-syntax 和 syntax-case），将 Lisp 世界中的过程式和声明式要素融为一体。他们还从 S 表达式转向由语法对象构成的树。这些树包含源码位置，使错误处理代码能够尝试指回表层代码（上述问题 4a）。Further work [2,8,15] refined and improved the pattern-oriented approach to specifying macros as well as hygienic macro expansion. The define-syntax-rule construct and hygienic expansion became part of the Scheme standard by the late 1990s [1]. Starting in 1988 and in parallel to the Scheme standardization process, Dybvig et al. [15] designed and implemented a macro definition construct, define-syntax-cases (in actual code it requires a combination of define-syntax and syntax-case) that merged the procedural and declarative elements of the Lisp world. Dybvig et al. also switched from S-expressions to trees of syntax objects. These trees included source locations so that the error handling code could try to point back to the surface code (problem 4a above).

从 20 世纪 80 年代末开始，研究者探索了其他便于编写宏的方法，其中两项与利用宏创建 DSL 有关。Dybvig 等人 [14] 发明了展开器传递宏：宏作者编写自己的展开器，并在不同宏中使用不同展开器。在抽象层面，展开器传递风格预示了检查静态属性的需要。Blume [3] 以及 Kelsey 和 Reese [34] 则加入了能够导出和导入宏的模块。这些模块使宏程序员能够封装一组宏，这是封装 DSL 设计与实现的第一步。Starting in the late 80s, researchers explored other ways to facilitate the work of macro authors, including two relevant to creating DSLs from macros. Dybvig et al. [14] invented expander-passing macros. Macro authors would write their own expanders and use different ones in different macros. At an abstract level, expansion-passing style anticipates the need for checking static attributes. Blume [3] as well as Kelsey and Reese [34] added modules that could export and import macros. Such modules allow macro programmers to encapsulate bundles of macros, a first step towards encapsulating a DSL’s design and implementation.

3　DSL 需要的不只是一堆宏3 DSLs Require More than Bunches of Macros

就扩展现有语言而言，Scheme 风格的宏比 Lisp 的宏进步很大。开发者可以给程序添加简洁且词法上正确的宏，并立即使用它们来编写普通的运行期代码或更多的宏。这种即时性既强大又诱人：程序员无须离开熟悉的编程环境，不必使用外部工具，也不用折腾脚手架配置。Scheme-style macros greatly improve on Lisp’s as far as the extension of an existing language is concerned. A developer can add concise and lexically correct macros to a program and may immediately use them, for writing either ordinary run-time code or additional macros. This immediacy is powerful and enticing because a programmer never has to leave the familiar programming environment, use external tools, or mess with scaffolding setups.

宏的思想在抽象层面也很容易理解。从概念上说，一个宏定义为 Racket 的某条文法产生式——定义或表达式——添加了一种新的选择。声明式方法使简单的 S 表达式变换器易于直截了当地描述；卫生宏展开则保证程序词法作用域的完整性。The idea of macros is also easy to comprehend at the abstract level. Conceptually, a macro definition adds a new alternative to one of Racket’s grammatical productions: definitions or expressions. The declarative approach makes it easy to specify simple S-expression transformers in a straightforward manner; hygienic macro expansion guarantees the integrity of the program’s lexical scope.

问题在于，语言扩展只会让人产生已经拥有某种专门定制语言的错觉。一方面，如果程序员把一组基于宏的语言扩展当作自成一体的 DSL 来用，就必须在编码时保持极强的自律。另一方面，宏系统未能支持使用 DSL 的一些传统优势：在解析或类型检查阶段发现错误；利用约束生成优化代码；或者链接到、乃至以专门定制的运行期函数为目标。The problem is that a language extension provides only a false sense of a purpose-tailored language. On one hand, a programmer who uses a bunch of macro-based language extensions as if it were a self-contained DSL must code with an extreme degree of self-discipline. On the other hand, the macro system fails to support some of the traditional advantages of using DSLs: catching mistakes in the parsing or type-checking stage; exploiting constraints to generate optimized code; or link with/target tailor-made run-time functions.

通常，创建 DSL 需要由多个编译阶段构成的流水线：
1. 一个解析器：基于显式规定的领域特定词汇与文法，并在 DSL 的源码层面报告错误；
2. 一套静态语义：因为从应用程序接口转向 DSL 的目标之一，就是静态地强制某些约束；
3. 一个代码生成与优化阶段：因为引入 DSL 的另一个目标，是利用静态约束或语言约束改善性能；
4. 一个运行期系统：因为宿主语言可能缺少某些功能，或者目标语言可能与宿主语言不同。
Scheme 宏本身并不支持创建这样的流水线，也不支持对它进行适当封装。Conventionally, the creation of DSLs demands a pipeline of compiler passes: 1. a parser, based on explicit specification of a domain-specific vocabulary and a grammar, that reports errors at the DSL’s source level; 2. a static semantics, because one goal of migrating from an application interface to a DSL is to enforce certain constraints statically; 3. a code generation and optimization pass, because another goal of introducing DSLs is to exploit the static or linguistic constraints for improved performance; and, 4. a run-time system, because (1) the host language may lack pieces of functionality or (2) the target language might be distinct from the host language. Scheme macros per se do not support the creation of such pipelines or its proper encapsulation.

Racket 设计者在创建最早的一批教学语言 [18,19] 时，注意到了其中一些问题。为此，他们开展了两项相互独立的工作，希望通过语言扩展机制支持 DSL 开发，同时明确要求保留语言扩展原有的易用性：The Racket designers noticed some of these problems when they created their first teaching languages [18,19]. In response, they launched two orthogonal efforts to support the development of DSLs via language-extension mechanisms with the explicit goal of retaining the ease of use of the latter:

一项工作着眼于 DSL 的封装，以及对一些传统编译阶段的支持。其思路是开发一个允许导出、导入宏和函数的模块系统，同时保留模块的分别编译概念。
另一项工作旨在提供一种机制，方便地表达宏对输入的假定，并在适当的层次上合成错误信息，也就是解决第 2 节中的问题。这些成果也将有助于通过模块实现 DSL。
第 4 节介绍一次雄心勃勃、试图同时解决所有这些问题、但最终被放弃的尝试；第 5、6 节则描述 Racket 最终为 DSL 设计者与实现者提供的工具。One concerned the encapsulation of DSLs and support for some traditional passes. This idea was to develop a module system that allows the export and import of macros and functions while also retaining a notion of separate compilation for modules. The other aimed at a mechanism for easily expressing a macro’s assumptions about its input and synthesizing error messages at the appropriate level, i.e., the problems from sec. 2. The results would also help with implementing DSLs via modules. While sec. 4 reports on an ambitious, and abandoned, attempt to address these problems all at once, secs. 5 and 6 describe the tools that Racket eventually provided to DSL designers and implementors.

4　雄心勃勃的开端4 Ambitious Beginnings

Racket 设计者发现传统 Scheme 宏系统的不足后，决定通过三项创新加以解决。首先，不再沿用传统的 S 表达式语法表示，而改用结构更丰富的表示（见第 4.1 节）。其次，他们意识到，宏需要协同工作才能实施上下文相关的检查。为此，他们在声明式宏之外补充了过程式的微宏（micro），用来处理展开上下文的属性（见第 4.2 节）。最后，他们决定用模块容纳基于宏的 DSL 实现，同时将模块作为使用 DSL 的单位（见第 4.3 节）。When the Racket designers discovered the shortcomings of a traditional Scheme macro system, they decided to address them with three innovations. First, they decided to move beyond the traditional S-expression representation of syntax and instead use a richly structured one (see sec. 4.1). Second, they realized that macros needed to work together to implement context-sensitive checks. To this end, they supplemented declarative macros with procedural micros that could deal with attributes of the expansion context (see sec. 4.2). Finally they decided to use modules as the containers of macro-based DSL implementations as well as the units of DSL use (see sec. 4.3).

4.1　从 S 表达式到语法对象4.1 From S-expressions to Syntax Objects

为了在宏展开过程中跟踪源码位置，Racket 与 Dybvig 的 Chez Scheme 一样，引入了表层代码的语法对象表示，放弃传统的 S 表达式表示。粗略地说，语法对象类似于在每个节点外面包裹一个结构体的 S 表达式。这个结构体至少包含语法中各个词元的源码位置。利用这些信息，宏展开器通常能够准确指出语法错误的源码位置，从而部分解决第 3 节所提到的问题 4a。To track source locations across macro expansion, Racket – like Dybvig’s Chez Scheme – introduced a syntax object representation of the surface code, abandoning the conventional S-expression representation. Roughly speaking, a syntax object resembles an S-expression with a structure wrapped around every node. At a minimum, this structure contains source locations of the various tokens in the syntax. Using this information, a macro expander can often pinpoint the source location of a syntax error, partially solving problem 4a from sec. 3.

4.2　微宏的词汇表4.2 The Vocabularies of Micros

回想一下，宏是作用于语法表示的函数。一旦这种表示使用了结构体，而不再仅仅使用 S 表达式，宏的签名也必须相应调整：Recall that a macro is a function on the syntax representation. Once this representation uses structures instead of just S-expressions, the signature of a macro has to be adapted:

Syntax-Object −→  Syntax-Object

当然，这个签名本身就意味着，宏无法自然地表达1用于传递展开上下文属性的通信渠道。Of course, this very signature says that macros cannot naturally express1 communication channels concerning attributes of the expansion context.

Krishnamurthi 等人的工作 [39] 以微宏补充宏，来解决这个问题。与 define-macro 一样，define-micro 指定一个接收语法表示的函数。此外，它还可以接收任意数量的 Attribute 值，使一组微宏能够显式地相互传递上下文信息：Krishnamurthi et al.’s work [39] supplements macros with micros to solve this issue. Like define-macro, define-micro specifies a function that consumes the representation of a syntax. Additionally, it may absorb any number of Attribute values so that collections of micros can communicate contextual information to each other explicitly:

Syntax-Object −→(Attribute ... −→Output)

1 宏作者可以采用将属性编码为语法对象的协议，实现这种通信。我们认为这种编码并不自然，因此使用“自然地表达”这一说法。1 A macro author could implement this form of communication via a protocol that encodes attributes as syntax objects. We consider an encoding unnatural and therefore use the phrase “naturally express.”

如这个签名所示，微宏与宏的另一个区别是，其结果可以是名为 Output 的任意类型。所有协同工作的微宏必须使用同一种结果类型，但不同微宏集合可以采用不同类型。对于类似宏的微宏，Output 就是 Syntax-Object；而对于嵌入式编译器，Output 则是 AST，即目标语言的抽象语法树类型。目标语言可以是 Racket，也可以完全不同，例如 GPU 汇编代码。Racket 团队当时并未探索这一方向。As this signature shows, a micro also differs from a macro in that the result is some arbitrary type called Output. This type must be the same for all micros that collaborate but may differ from one collection of micros to another. For macro-like micros, Output would equal Syntax-Object. By contrast, for an embedded compiler Output would be AST, meaning the type of abstract syntax trees for the target language. This target language might be Racket, but it could also be something completely different, such as GPU assembly code. The Racket team did not explore this direction at the time.

这说明，应当把 DSL 的微宏看作某个集合的成员。为了使这一概念具体化，Krishnamurthi 等人还引入了词汇表（vocabulary）的概念。宏和微宏的集合决定了 DSL 的“词语”和“句子结构”，因此词汇表在形式上相当于词典与文法规则。微宏自身则把嵌入语言中的“句子”转换成有意义的、也就是可执行的程序。As this explanation points out, micros for DSLs must be thought of as members of a collection. To make this notion concrete, Krishnamurthi et al. also introduce the notion of a vocabulary. Since collections of macros and micros determine the “words” and “sentence structure” of a DSL, a vocabulary represents the formal equivalent of a dictionary and grammar rules. The micros themselves transform “sentences” in an embedded language into meaningful – that is, executable – programs.

在 Krishnamurthi 等人的系统中，词汇表通过 (make-vocabulary) 创建，并配有两种操作：define-micro 向给定词汇表添加一个微宏函数；dispatch 则在特定词汇表的上下文中，将微宏应用于一个表达式。In Krishnamurthi et al.’s setting, a vocabulary is created with (make-vocabulary) and comes with two operations: define-micro, which adds a micro function to a given vocabulary, and dispatch, which applies a micro to an expression in the context of a specific vocabulary.

与 Scheme 的宏定义一样，微宏定义使用模式 DSL 来指定输入。对于 Attribute ... 序列，微宏通过显式的 lambda 来接收。为了创建输出，if 微宏通过 make-AST-if 分配一个 AST 节点；这个节点的各个组成部分，是原始 if 表达式三个子部分分别展开的结果。这些子表达式的展开使用 dispatch：该函数以分阶段的方式接收待展开表达式、上下文词汇表及属性（这里没有属性）。Like Scheme’s macro definitions, micro definitions use a pattern DSL for specifying inputs. 建立 type-check 词汇表之后，开发者既可以单独使用它，也可以将它与 compiler 词汇表组合使用。例如，Racket 的软类型系统 [24] 需要对语言的 letrec 构造作出不同的解释，即使用一个与实际编译器无关的独立 type-check 词汇表。将这两个词汇表组合起来，就可以实现 Typed Racket [44] 的一种变体；在这种情形下，组合过程会丢弃基于 type-check 词汇表的编译阶段所产生的结果。Once the type-check vocabulary is in place, a developer can use it independently or in combination with the compiler vocabulary. For example, Racket’s soft typing system [24] needed a distinct interpretation for the language’s letrec construct, i.e., a distinct type-check vocabulary unrelated to the actual compiler. A variant of Typed Racket [44] could be implemented via the composition of these two vocabularies; in this context, the composition would discard the result of the pass based on the type-check vocabulary.

总体而言，词汇表和微宏给 DSL 创建者带来两点好处。第一，现在可以用显式的微宏集合规定语言语法。每个微宏代表该语言表达式文法中的一条新产生式，输入模式描述其形状。第二，可以自然地表达并实现静态检查。微宏的附加参数表示“继承”属性，灵活的 Output 类型则允许传播“综合”属性。In general, DSL creators get two advantages from vocabularies and micros. First, they can now specify the syntax of their languages via explicit collections of micros. Each micro denotes a new production in the language’s expression language, and the input patterns describe its shape. Second, they can naturally express and implement static checking. The micro’s secondary arguments represent “inherited” attribute, and the flexible Output type allows the propagation of “synthesized” ones.

利用词汇表实现完整的 DSL，变得像搭乐高一样：（1）词汇表类似混入（mixin）[31]；（2）语言类似类；（3）dispatch 基本上就是方法调用。因此，创建一系列相近的 DSL，往往就像从语言积木盒中取出若干部件并将其连接起来。对于团队在 20 世纪 90 年代中期快速创建和修改教学语言的工作，词汇表是至关重要的第一步。Implementing complete DSLs from vocabularies becomes similar to playing with Legos: (1) vocabularies are like mixins [31], (2) languages resemble classes, and (3) dispatch is basically a method invocation. Hence creating a variety of similar DSLs is often a game of linking a number of pieces from a box of language-building blocks. For the team’s rapid production and modification of teaching languages in the mid 1990s, vocabularies were a critical first step.

4.3　语义模块的语言4.3 Languages for Semantic Modules

根据第 3 节，任何语言的实现都结合了编译器与运行期系统。无论通过宏还是微宏实现，这一原则同样适用于 DSL。两者都将源码转换成目标代码，而目标代码会引用运行期值（函数、对象、常量等）。这些运行期值往往通过“合谋”协作，也就是说，它们在目标代码中的使用满足某些逻辑命题——如果所有代码都能自由访问这些值，这些不变量便无法成立。因此，DSL 实现者几乎肯定希望隐藏这些运行期值，甚至隐藏某些辅助的编译期变换。所有这些都表明，宏、微宏和词汇表应当放入模块，而 DSL 实现应由这样的模块组成。According to sec. 3 the implementation of any language combines a compiler with a run-time system. This dictum also applies to DSLs, whether realized with macros or micros. Both translate source code to target code, which refers to run-time values (functions, objects, constants, and so on). Such run-time values often collaborate “via conspiracy,” meaning their uses in target code satisfies logical statements – invariants that would not hold if all code had free access to these values. That is, the implementor of a DSL will almost certainly wish to hide these run-time values and even some of the auxiliary compile-time transformations. All of this suggests that macros, micros and vocabularies should go into a module, and such modules should make up a DSL implementation.

反过来，DSL 实现者考虑部署的是完整语言，而不是单个构造。事实上，传统的语言实现者设想，DSL 程序员编写的是自成一体的程序。相比之下，Lisp 程序员从语言扩展出发，设想 DSL 程序员可能希望跳回底层宿主语言，甚至在表达式层面整合不同的类 DSL 扩展中的构造。问题是，基于宏与微宏的方法能否摆脱普通 DSL 的“整个程序”思维，实现 Lisp 式的做法——为小块代码部署语言。Conversely, the implementors of DSLs do not think of deploying individual constructs but complete languages. Indeed, conventional language implementors imagine that DSL programmers create self-contained programs. By contrast, Lispers think of their language extensions and imagine that DSL programmers may wish to escape into the underlying host language or even integrate constructs from different DSL-like extensions at the expression level. The question is whether a macro-micro based approach can move away from the “whole program” thinking of ordinary DSLs and realize a Lisp-ish approach of deploying languages for small units of code.

Krishnamurthi 的博士论文 [38] 回答了这两个问题，从而提出了第一个完整框架：以面向宏的方法实现面向语言编程。它将宏与 20 世纪 90 年代 Racket 中称为 unit 的一等模块 [29] 相结合，使后者既成为 DSL 实现的容器，也成为 DSL 部署的容器。从技术上说，这些 unit 具有图 4 的形状。它们以 Language 和链接期导入项为参数，并相应地导出值。Krishnamurthi’s dissertation [38] presents answers to these two questions and thus introduces the first full framework for a macro-oriented approach to language-oriented programming. It combines macros with the first-class modules of the 1990s Racket, dubbed units [29], where the latter becomes both the container for DSL implementations as well as the one for DSL deployment. Technically, these units have the shape of fig. 4. They are parameterized over a Language and link-time imports, and they export values in response.

DSL 实现自身也只是一个将宏、微宏及运行期值组合起来的 unit/lang。只有向语言管理器注册后，它才会被认定为有效的 Language。语言管理器将 unit/lang 表达式分别编译为普通 unit。在此过程中，它展开所有宏和微宏的使用，再解析生成代码中的全部名称，而不将任何名称暴露给其他代码。特别是，开发者无须采取任何操作，例如把运行期值的名称加入 Language 的导出声明，或加入使用该 Language 的 unit/lang。编译结果是一组普通的 Racket unit，Racket 编译器再将它们变成运行中的程序。A DSL implementation is also just a unit/lang that combines macros, micros, and run-time values. It is not recognized as a valid Language until it is registered with a language administrator. The latter compiles unit/lang expressions separately to plain units. For this compilation, the language administrator expands all uses of macros and micros and then resolves all the names in the generated code – without exposing any of them to other code. In particular, the developer does not need to take any action, such as adding the names of run-time values to export specifications of Languages or to unit/langs that use a Language. The result of a compilation is a collection of plain Racket units, and the Racket compiler turns this collection into a running program.

原则上，Krishnamurthi 的 unit/lang 系统解决了第 3 节列出的全部四项 DSL 要求。微宏与词汇表的组合能够强制执行超出宏能力范围的语法约束。它们被设计为通过多个阶段表达静态处理，并明确支持不同于 Racket 的目标语言。此外，以 unit/lang 形式实现 DSL，可以将编译器组件与运行期组件一起封装起来。In principle, Krishnamurthi’s unit/lang system addresses all four DSL criteria listed in sec. 3. The micro-vocabulary combination can enforce syntax constraints beyond what macros can do. They are designed to express static processing in several passes and explicitly accommodate target languages distinct from Racket. And, the implementations of DSLs as unit/langs encapsulates the compiler component with a run-time component.

这个系统未能满足的，是把 DSL 实现技术与 Lisp 的增量式语言扩展方法融合起来的愿望。主要问题是，程序员必须让整个 unit 以一种完整语言为参数，无法从 unit/lang 中选择性地导入单个宏和微宏，而这恰恰是 Racket 开发者真正希望模块化宏系统提供的功能。使用普通 unit 数年之后，Racket 团队还意识到，一等 unit 提供的表达能力超出了日常需求；因此，编程实现链接过程所增加的复杂性，在代码库中几乎从未带来相应回报。What this system fails to satisfy is the desire to synthesize DSL implementation techniques with Lisp’s incremental language-extension approach. The main problem is that a programmer has to parameterize an entire unit over a complete language. It is impossible to selectively import individual macros and micros from a unit/lang, which is what Racket developers truly want from a modular macro system. After a few years of using plain units, the Racket team also realized that first-class units provided more expressive power than they usually needed, meaning the extra complexity of programming the linking process rarely ever paid off in the code base.

此外，从社区层面看，unit/lang 系统也迈得太远。当时仍名为 PLT Scheme 的 Racket 坚定地属于 Scheme 阵营，而 Scheme 社区已经开发并开源了一套迅速流行起来的新语法系统 [15]。这套宏系统将 Krishnamurthi 宏的声明式形式与其微宏的过程式形式结合成单一的 define-syntax-cases 形式。它还采用了与 Krishnamurthi 系统相同类型的语法对象表示，支持源码跟踪、卫生展开以及其他跨展开通信。换句话说，新系统似乎具备 Krishnamurthi 系统的全部优点，却没有它的缺点。因此，Racket 团队决定采用并调整这套宏系统，再围绕它创建模块系统。Additionally, the unit/lang system was a step too far on the social side. Racket – then called PLT Scheme – was firmly in the Scheme camp and, at the time, the Scheme community had developed and open-sourced a new syntax system [15] that quickly gained in popularity. This macro system combined the declarative form of Krishnamurthi’s macros with the procedural form of his micros into a single define-syntax-cases form. Furthermore, this new macro system came with the same kind of syntax-object representation as Krishnamurthi’s, allowing source tracking, hygienic expansion, and other cross-expansion communication. In other words, the new system seemed to come with all the positive aspects of Krishnamurthi’s without its downsides. Hence, the Racket team decided to adapt this macro system and create a module system around it.

为了支持分别编译，Racket 在开始运行模块之前，会丢弃展开阶段产生的效果。这体现了设计者的认识：语言扩展就像编译器，不必使用它所编译的语言来实现，也不与它所翻译的程序在同一阶段运行。阶段分离大大简化了对编译过程的推理，避免了 Lisp 和 Chez Scheme 显式 eval-when、compile-when 指令中的许多陷阱 [5,25,42]。To enable separate compilation, Racket discards the effects of the expansion phase before it moves on to running a module. Discarding such effects reflects the Racket designers’ understanding that language-extensions are like compilers, which do not have to be implemented in the same language as the one that they compile and which are not run in the same phase as the program that they translate. Phase separation greatly facilitates reasoning about compilation, avoiding a lot of the pitfalls of Lisp’s and Chez Scheme’s explicit eval-when and compile-when instructions [5,25,42].

6　通过解析宏正确处理语法6 Syntax Done Properly With Parsing Macros

DSL 语法的实现包含两个基本部分：解析语法上合法的句子，以及报告对语法规则的违反。两者同样重要，但四十年来，宏研究与实践社区大多忽略了后者。The implementation of a DSL’s syntax consists of two essential parts: parsing syntactically legitimate sentences, and reporting violations of the syntactic rules. Both aspects are equally important, but for 40 years, the macro community mostly neglected the second one.

第 2 节列出了通过 Lisp 风格宏进行解析的五个问题。Kohlbecker 基于模式和模板的改写 DSL，消除了其中除第 4 个之外的全部问题。要理解这一问题的复杂性，可以考察用经典 BNF 记法表达的 let 表达式的实际文法产生式：Sec. 2 lists five problems with parsing via Lisp-style macros. Kohlbecker’s rewriting DSL – based on patterns and templates – eliminates all of them except for problem 4. To appreciate the complexity of this particular problem, consider the actual grammatical production of let expressions in classical BNF notation:

(let ({[id expression]}∗) expression+)

(let (((+ 1 2) x)) (* x 3))  ;; => ((lambda ((+ 1 2)) (* x 3)) x)

无论哪种情况，面向模式的语言都会生成 => 箭头右侧的 lambda 表达式。因此，最终的语法错误会谈论 lambda 和参数，而这些概念在 let 的文法描述中根本没有出现。读者也许会觉得这条错误信息“显而易见”，不值得在意；但必须记住，这个 let 表达式可能是由另一个宏生成的，而后者又可能来自某个定义宏的宏，如此层层嵌套。In either case, a pattern-oriented language generates the lambda expression to the right of the => arrow. Hence, the resulting syntax errors speak of lambda and parameters, concepts that the grammatical description of let never mentions. While a reader might be tempted to dismiss this particular error message as “obvious,” it is imperative to keep in mind that this let expression might have been generated by the use of some other macro, which in turn might be the result of some macro-defining macro, and so on.

Dybvig 的 define-syntax-cases 对 Kohlbecker 的 DSL 略有改进：它允许给宏的输入模式附加守卫条件（fender），即布尔表达式。有了这些守卫，宏开发者就可以手动编写条件，检查上述附加约束。然而，即便对 let 这样的简单情形，错误检查代码的规模也是改写说明的许多倍。因此，大多数宏作者要么不添加这些代码，要么即便添加了，也未能写出既全面又能生成良好错误信息的检查。Dybvig’s define-syntax-cases slightly improves on Kohlbecker’s DSL. It allows the attachment of of fenders – Boolean expressions – to a macro’s input patterns. With such fenders, a macro developer can manually formulate conditions that check such side conditions. Even in such simple cases as let, however, the error-checking code is many times the size of the rewriting specification. And this is why most macro authors fail to add this code or, if they do, fail to write comprehensive checks that also generates good error messages.

Culpepper 用于定义宏的 DSL 通过两项创新解决了这个问题 [9,10,11,12]。第一项是在模式匹配 DSL 中增添“词语”，用来表达 let 宏所需的那类分类约束。第二项是提供一个用于规定新分类的 DSL。二者结合，使程序员可以轻松地强制执行对表层语法的假定，用规格说明中的术语合成错误信息，并将宏输入分解成相关片段。Culpepper’s DSL for defining macros solves this problem with two innovations [9,10,11,12]. The first is an augmentation of the pattern-matching DSL with “words” for articulating classification constraints such as those of the let macro. The second is a DSL for specifying new classifications. Together, these innovations allow programmers to easily enforce assumptions about the surface syntax, synthesize error messages in terms of the specification, and deconstruct the inputs of a macro into relevant pieces.

根据前面的讨论，let 的规格说明需要两种语法分类：一种规定 let 输入的第二部分是一个序列，另一种规定该序列中的元素是标识符—表达式对。图 10 展示了如何在 Culpepper 的元 DSL 中定义这些分类。分类至少必须包含一个 pattern 子句，描述该形式的上下文无关形状，并为各个组成部分命名。例如，binding 类使用预定义分类 id（标识符）和 expr（表达式），规定一个绑定具有 (id expr) 的形状，并将 id 命名为 lhs、expr 命名为 rhs。使用这样的语法类时，例如在 distinct-bindings 的定义中，可以通过点记法引用输入的这些属性。因此，distinct-bindings 中的 b.lhs ... 表示标识符序列。例子还表明，语法类定义也可以调用 check-duplicate-id 这样的过程式代码处理输入。fail-when 子句允许宏开发者在默认信息不够清楚时，指定合成错误信息的一部分。Following our discussion above, the specification of let needs two syntax classifications: one to say that the second part of let’s input is a sequence and another one to say that the elements of this sequence are identifier-expression pairs. Fig. 10 shows how a programmer can define these classifications in Culpepper’s meta-DSL. A classification must come with at least one pattern clause, which spells out the context-free shape of the form and names its pieces. For example, the binding class uses the pre-defined classifications id (for identifier) and expr (for expression) to say that a binding has the shape (id expr) and that the name of the id is lhs and the name of expr is rhs. Any use of such a syntax class, for example the one in the definition of distinct-bindings, may refer to these attributes of the input via a dot notation. Thus, b.lhs ... in distinct-bindings denotes the sequence of identifiers. As this example also shows, a syntax-class definition may also defer to procedural code, such as check-duplicate-id to process the input. A fail-when clause allows macro developers to specify a part of the synthesized error message (when the default is not sufficiently clear).

借助这两个语法类，就很容易规定 let 的完整形状，见图 11。bs 的 :distinct-bindings 分类，为输入语法的两个部分引入名称：标识符序列（bs.lhs*），以及与各变量一一对应的右侧表达式序列（bs.rhs*）。语法模板利用这些部分，生成与图 2 中宏相同的目标代码。Using these two syntax classes, specifying the complete shape of let is straightforward; see fig. 11. The :distinct-bindings classification of bs introduces names for two pieces of the input syntax: a sequence of identifiers (bs.lhs*) and a sequence of right-hand-side expressions (bs.rhs*), one per variable. The syntax template uses these pieces to generate the same target code as the macros in fig. 2.

比较图 2 与图 11，可以看出 Culpepper 宏编写 DSL 的优点和缺点。优点是，Culpepper 风格宏定义的规模，看起来仍与 Kohlbecker 风格的相同。修改后的定义只是在宏的模式中加入分类，在宏的模板中加入属性选择。但这种表面的规模比较掩盖了一个事实：这些微小改动，使宏能够检查 let 形状的全部约束，并依据规定的表层语法报告语法错误。正如 Culpepper [10，第 469 页] 所解释的，若用过程式宏实现同等程度的假定检查与错误报告，代码规模会增加“数倍”。此外，“[这个元 DSL 的]主要益处……是提高了”宏输入规格说明和代码模板的“清晰度”。A comparison of figs. 2 and 11 illustrates the advantages as well as the disadvantages of Culpepper’s DSL for writing macros. On the positive side, the size of the Culpepper-style macro definition appears to remain the same as the one for the Kohlbecker-style one. The revised definition merely adds classifications to the macro’s pattern and attribute selections to the macro’s template. This shallow size comparison camouflages that these small changes cause the macro to check all constraints on the shape of let and formulate syntax errors in terms of the specified surface syntax. As Culpepper [10, page 469] explains, implementing the same level of assumption checking and error reporting via procedural macros increases the code size by “several factors.” Furthermore the “primary benefit [of this meta-DSL] . . . is increased clarity” of a macro’s input specification and its code template.

缺点是，如今宏程序员需要开发图 10 那样的语法分类，并像图 11 那样在宏定义中恰当地使用它们。开发语法分类显然带来了新的门槛，但使用它们收益显著，而且多数分类最终会成为库中的可复用组件。因此，开发成本能够通过复用收回。至于在宏模板与模式中使用语法分类，经验表明，大多数宏程序员将这些注解视为类似类型的概念，将属性选择视为自然的附带结果。On the negative side, macro programmers are now expected to develop syntax classifications such as those in fig. 10 and use them properly in macro definitions, as in fig. 11. While the development of syntax classifications clearly poses a new obstacle, their use comes with a significant payoff and most end up as reusable elements in libraries. Hence the cost of developing them is recouped through reuse. As for the use of syntax classifications in macro templates and patterns, experience shows that most macro programmers consider the annotations as type-like notions and the attribute selections as a natural by-product.

简言之，在宏定义方面，Culpepper 的元 DSL 完全取代了 define-syntax-cases 元 DSL。如今，绝大多数 Racket 程序员使用 Culpepper 的 DSL 开发宏，并为不断扩大的语法分类集合贡献代码。In short, Culpepper’s meta-DSL completely replaces the define-syntax-cases meta-DSL for macro definitions. By now, the large majority of Racket programmers develop macros in Culpepper’s DSL and contribute to the ever-expanding collection of syntax classifications.

7　DSL 创建者需要的不仅是模块化的解析宏7 DSL Creators Need Still More Than Modular, Parsing Macros

Racket 着眼于语言扩展与 DSL 实现，在推进宏技术方面取得了长足进步。图 12 大体按照第 3 节的标准概览了这些进展。用于定义宏的 syntax-parse DSL 几乎可以表达所有上下文无关与上下文相关约束；宏开发者只需提供少量提示，就能得到用宏所定义形式的术语报告语法错误的代码。module 系统既支持语言扩展所需的细粒度宏导出与导入，也支持 DSL 的完整实现。Racket has made great progress in improving the state of the art of macros with an eye toward both language extension and DSL implementation. Fig. 12 surveys the progress in roughly the terms of sec. 3’s criteria. The syntax-parse DSL for defining macros can express almost every context-free and -sensitive constraint; macro developers get away with a few hints and yet get code that reports syntax errors in terms of the macro-defined variant. The module system supports both the fine-grained export/import of macros for language extensions and the whole-cloth implementation of DSLs.

目前，Racket 初学者完全有能力实现 DSL [4,16]，专家更能轻松做到。初学者可能侧重基于 module 的 DSL，而专家则利用宏创建与宿主语言无缝嵌入的 DSL。例如，针对运行期值的模式匹配 DSL、syntax-parse DSL 本身，以及 Redex [17,40]。然而，在这一领域，宏框架尚未满足第 3 节列出的全部理想要求。At this point, implementing DSLs is well within reach for Racket beginners [4,16] and easy for experts. While beginners may focus on module-based DSLs, experts use macros to create fluidly embedded DSLs. Examples are the DSL of pattern-matching for run-time values, the syntax-parse DSL itself, and Redex [17,40]. In this domain, however, the macro framework falls short of satisfying the full list of desiderata for from sec. 3.

为说明这一差距，下面对 DSL 作一个简要分类，并描述 Racket 的支持情况：
独立 DSL
这是现实中最广为人知的形式。Racket 通过模块语言支持它们，所提供的便利至少不亚于其他 DSL 构建框架。
具有固定接口的嵌入式 DSL
所有编程语言都带有许多这样的子语言。例如，printf 解释格式 DSL——通常写成嵌入的字符串——把若干值呈现到输出设备。在 Racket 中，这类 DSL 则以一组新的表达式形式出现，程序员用它们组合领域特定程序。不过，即便在 Racket 中，这类 DSL 与宿主语言的交互仍然受到限制。
具有表达式级接口、可扩展的嵌入式 DSL
Racket 的模式匹配 DSL 和结构体声明 DSL，是这类 DSL 的典型例子。前者允许程序员表述复杂模式，并在其中嵌入 Racket 表达式；后者可以包含模式，模式又包含表达式，如此嵌套。模式 DSL 可以扩展，例如，结构体定义 DSL 可以自动生成用于匹配结构体实例的模式。自然，结构体声明 DSL 本身也可以细粒度地嵌入 Racket 表达式。To explain this gap, let us concisely classify DSLs and characterize Racket’s support: stand-alone DSLs These are the most recognized form in the real world. Racket supports those via module languages with at least the same conveniences as other DSL construction frameworks. embedded DSLs with a fixed interface All programming languages come with numerous such sub-languages. For example, printf interprets the format DSL – usually written as an embedded string – for rendering some number of values for an output device. In Racket, such DSLs instead come as a new set of expression forms with which programmers compose domain-specific programs. Even in Racket, though, such DSLs allow only restricted interactions with the host. embedded and extensible DSLs with an expression-level interface Racket’s DSLs for pattern matching and structure declarations are illuminating examples of this kind. The former allows programmers to articulate complex patterns, with embedded Racket expressions. The latter may contain patterns, which contain expressions, etc. The pattern DSL is extensible so that, for example, the DSL of structure definitions can automatically generate patterns for matching structure instances. Naturally, this DSL for structure declarations can also embed Racket expressions at a fine granularity.

对于第三类 DSL，Racket 的宏方法仍存在一些问题。与第 3 节的标准比较，可以看出三个明显的问题。With regard to the third kind of DSL, Racket’s macro approach suffers from several problems. A comparison with the criteria in sec. 3 suggests three obvious ones.

第一个问题涉及需要引入新语法类别的 DSL，进而涉及良好的解析与错误报告技术。语法类虽然允许 DSL 创建者枚举新语法类别的元素，但这个枚举是固定的。有经验的 DSL 实现者能够绕过这一限制，就像程序员可以在面向对象语言中创建可扩展的访问者模式，使数据类型能够在不查看内部实现的情况下扩展一样。The first concerns DSLs that demand new syntactic categories and, in turn, good parsing and error reporting techniques. While syntax classes allow DSL creators to enumerate the elements of a new syntactic category, this enumeration is fixed. Experienced DSL implementors can work around this restriction, just like programmers can create extensible visitor patterns in object-oriented languages to allow the blind-box extension of data types.

第二个问题是上下文相关的语言处理。现有宏框架使上下文相关的静态检查、翻译与优化变换难以实现——即使只是针对 Racket 自身的宏也是如此，更不用说定义新语法类别的宏。Chang 及其学生 [6,7] 已经开始在类型检查领域突破这些限制；类型检查是一种格外丰富的上下文相关处理。具体来说，该团队把类型检查这一丰富领域编码成一个元 DSL。本质上，它使 DSL 创建者能够采用文献中的类型细化规则来表述类型检查（见图 13），而不是采用图 3 的过程式方法。然而，他们的创新依赖脆弱的协议让宏协同工作 [28]。因此，若不借助宏编程的设计模式，就很难扩展这个框架，或将其调整到其他领域。The second problem is about context-sensitive language processing. The existing macro framework makes it difficult to implement context-sensitive static checking, translations, and optimizing transformations – even for just Racket’s macros, not to mention those that define new syntactic categories. Chang and his students [6,7] have begun to push the boundaries in the realm of type checking, a particular rich form of context-sensitivity. Specifically, the team has encoded the rich domain of type checking as a meta-DSL. In essence, this meta-DSL enables DSL creators to formulate type checking in the form of type elaboration rules from the literature (see fig. 13), instead of the procedural approach of fig. 3. However, their innovation exploits brittle protocols to make macros work together [28]. As a result, it is difficult to extend their framework or adapt it to other domains without using design patterns for macro programming.

最后，这个 DSL 框架无法妥善容纳以 Racket 之外的语言为编译目标的语言。设想一个用于 Cuda 编程的嵌入式 DSL，它能从与 Racket 的无缝集成中受益。这样的 DSL 可能需要两种解释：在配有图形协处理器的计算机上，它应编译为 GPU 代码；在没有这种处理器的计算机上，它可能需要表示一个普通 Racket 表达式。沿着 Chang 等人的思路实现依赖类型系统，是第二个具体例子。类型语言不具有 Racket 表达式与定义的语义。虽然可以让这类 DSL 经由 Racket 核心语言展开，但这会迫使 DSL 开发者采用大量宏设计模式。Finally, the DSL framework fails to accommodate languages whose compilation target is not Racket. Consider an embedded DSL for Cuda programming that benefits from a fluid integration with Racket. Such a DSL may need two interpretations: on computers with graphical co-processors it should compile to GPU code, while on a computer without such a processor it may need to denote a plain Racket expression. Implementing a dependent-type system in the spirit of Chang et al.’s work supplies a second concrete example. The language of types does not have the semantics of Racket’s expressions and definitions. Although it is possible to expand such DSLs through Racket’s core, it forces DSL developers to employ numerous macro-design patterns.

为这三个问题提出的绕行方案，说明了为什么 Racket 团队并不认为问题已经解决。Racket 的核心理念就是帮助程序员避免重复的句法设计模式。因此，宏层面设计模式的出现，与 Racket 的行事方式背道而驰；Racket 团队将继续寻求改进。The proposed work-arounds for these three problems reveal why the Racket team does not consider the problem solved. Racket is all about helping programmers avoid syntactic design patterns. Hence, the appearance of design patterns at the macro level is antithetical to the Racket way of doing things, and the Racket team will continue to look for improvements.

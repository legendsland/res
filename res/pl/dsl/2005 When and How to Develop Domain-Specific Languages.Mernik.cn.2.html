When and How to Develop Domain-Specific Languages

Marjan Mernik、Jan Heering、Anthony M. Sloane

Marjan Mernik, Jan Heering, Anthony M. Sloane

摘要

Abstract

领域特定语言（DSL）是针对特定应用领域定制的语言。与通用编程语言相比，在其应用领域中，DSL 在表达能力和易用性方面具有显著优势。DSL 的开发很困难，因为它同时需要领域知识和语言开发专长，而兼具二者的人很少。因此，开发 DSL 的决定往往被无限期推迟，甚至根本未被考虑；大多数 DSL 也始终未能超越应用程序库阶段。

Domain-specific languages (DSLs) are languages tailored to a specific application domain. They offer substantial gains in expressiveness and ease of use compared with general-purpose programming languages in their domain of application. DSL development is hard, requiring both domain knowledge and language development expertise. Few people have both. Not surprisingly, the decision to develop a DSL is often postponed indefinitely, if considered at all, and most DSLs never get beyond the application library stage.

尽管已有许多文章讨论具体 DSL 的开发，但关于 DSL 开发方法论的文献非常有限，何时以及如何开发 DSL 仍有许多问题尚未解决。为帮助 DSL 开发者，我们识别了 DSL 开发中决策、分析、设计和实现阶段的模式。这些模式改进并扩展了早期关于 DSL 设计模式的工作。我们还讨论了可能有助于加快 DSL 开发的领域分析工具和语言开发系统。最后，我们提出若干开放问题。

Although many articles have been written on the development of particular DSLs, there is very limited literature on DSL development methodologies and many questions remain regarding when and how to develop a DSL. To aid the DSL developer, we identify patterns in the decision, analysis, design, and implementation phases of DSL development. Our patterns improve and extend earlier work on DSL design patterns. We also discuss domain analysis tools and language development systems that may help to speed up DSL development. Finally, we state a number of open problems.

1 Introduction

1.1 概述

1.1 General

许多计算机语言是领域特定的，而不是通用的。领域特定语言（DSL）也称为面向应用的语言 [118]、专用语言 [148, p. xix]、面向任务的语言 [108]、专门化语言 [15, p. 17] 或应用语言 [99]。所谓第四代语言（4GL）[99] 通常是面向数据库应用的 DSL。“小语言”是规模较小、且不包含通用编程语言（GPL）中许多特性的 DSL [14, p. 715]。

Many computer languages are domain-specific rather than general-purpose. Domain-specific languages (DSLs) are also called application-oriented [118], special-purpose [148, p. xix], task-specific [108], specialized [15, p. 17], or application [99] languages. So-called fourth-generation languages (4GLs) [99] are usually DSLs for database applications. Little languages are small DSLs that do not include many features found in general-purpose programming languages (GPLs) [14, p. 715].

DSL 通过牺牲通用性来换取有限领域中的表达能力。DSL 提供针对特定应用领域定制的记法和构造，因此与 GPL 相比，它在该领域中能够显著提高表达能力和易用性，并相应提高生产率、降低维护成本。DSL 还减少了所需的领域知识和编程专长，从而使更广泛的软件开发者能够进入该应用领域。表 1 列出了一些广泛使用的 DSL 及其应用领域。第三列给出 [79] 中定义的语言级别。语言级别与生产率之间的关系见同样出自 [79] 的表 2。除了这些例子，DSL 的收益还经常在实践中被观察到，并得到 [72, 11, 79, 89, 66] 等定量结果的支持；但无论总体还是具体案例，对这些收益进行定量验证都很困难，也仍是一个重要的开放问题。因此，本文对 DSL 开发的讨论主要是定性的。

DSLs trade generality for expressiveness in a limited domain. By providing notations and constructs tailored toward a particular application domain, they offer substantial gains in expressiveness and ease of use compared with GPLs for the domain in question, with corresponding gains in productivity and reduced maintenance costs. Also, by reducing the amount of domain and programming expertise needed, DSLs open up their application domain to a larger group of software developers compared to GPLs. Some widely used DSLs with their application domains are listed in Table 1. The third column gives the language level of each DSL as given in [79]. Language level is related to productivity as shown in Table 2, also from [79]. Apart from these examples, the benefits of DSLs have often been observed in practice and are supported by quantitative results such as those reported in [72, 11, 79, 89, 66], but their quantitative validation in general as well as in particular cases is hard and an important open problem. Therefore, the treatment of DSL development in this article will be largely qualitative.

DSL 的使用绝非新事物。用于数控机床编程的 DSL APT 开发于 1957–1958 年 [116]。著名的语法规范形式 BNF 可追溯到 1959 年 [6]。用于硬件描述和协议规范等领域的领域特定可视化语言（DSVL）也很重要，但超出了本文综述的范围。

The use of DSLs is by no means new. APT, a DSL for programming numerically controlled machine tools, was developed in 1957–1958 [116]. BNF, the well-known syntax specification formalism, dates back to 1959 [6]. Domain-specific visual languages (DSVLs), such as visual languages for hardware description and protocol specification, are important, but beyond the scope of this survey.

我们不会定义什么构成应用领域、什么不构成应用领域。有些人把 Cobol 视为面向商业应用的 DSL，另一些人则认为这把应用领域的概念推得太远。抛开定义问题，可以自然地把 DSL 看成一个连续尺度：左端是 BNF 这类高度专门化的 DSL，右端是 C++ 这类 GPL（[79] 的语言级别指标就是量化该尺度的一种尝试）。在这个尺度上，Cobol 位于 BNF 与 C++ 之间，但更接近后者。同样，也很难判断 Unix shell 之类的命令语言或 Tcl 之类的脚本语言是否属于 DSL。显然，领域特定性是一个程度问题。

We will not give a definition of what constitutes an application domain and what does not. Some consider Cobol to be a DSL for business applications, but others would argue this is pushing the notion of application domain too far. Leaving matters of definition aside, it is natural to think of DSLs in terms of a gradual scale with very specialized DSLs such as BNF on the left and GPLs such as C++ on the right. (The language level measure of [79] is one attempt to quantify this scale.) On this scale, Cobol would be somewhere between BNF and C++, but much closer to the latter. Similarly, it is hard to tell if command languages like the Unix shell or scripting languages like Tcl are DSLs. Clearly, domain-specificity is a matter of degree.

任何 GPL 与应用程序库结合后都可以充当 DSL。库的应用程序编程接口（API）构成由类名、方法名和函数名组成的领域特定词汇，使用该库的 GPL 程序可通过创建对象和调用方法来使用这些词汇。既然如此，最初为什么还要开发 DSL？很简单，因为 DSL 能以更好的方式提供领域特定性：

In combination with an application library, any GPL can act as a DSL. The library’s Application Programmers Interface (API) constitutes a domain-specific vocabulary of class, method, and function names that becomes available by object creation and method invocation to any GPL program using the library. This being the case, why were DSLs developed in the first place? Simply because they can offer domain-specificity in better ways:

  • 合适或既定的领域特定记法通常超出了 GPL 所提供的有限、用户可定义的运算符记法。DSL 从一开始就提供合适的领域特定记法。它们的重要性不应被低估，因为它们与使用 DSL 所带来的生产率提升直接相关。

    Appropriate or established domain-specific notations are usually beyond the limited user-definable operator notation offered by GPLs. A DSL offers appropriate domain-specific notations from the start. Their importance should not be underestimated as they are directly related to the productivity improvement associated with the use of DSLs.

  • 合适的领域特定构造和抽象不一定能够直接映射为可放入库中的函数或对象。遍历和错误处理是典型例子 [19, 66, 27]。GPL 与应用程序库结合后，只能间接或笨拙地表达这些构造，而 DSL 可以从一开始就纳入领域特定构造。

    Appropriate domain-specific constructs and abstractions cannot always be mapped in a straightforward way to functions or objects that can be put in a library. Traversals and error handling are typical examples [19, 66, 27]. A GPL in combination with an application library can only express these constructs indirectly or in an awkward way. Again, a DSL would incorporate domain-specific constructs from the start.

  • 使用 DSL 可以基于 DSL 构造进行分析、验证、优化、并行化和转换；若使用 GPL，由于相关源代码模式过于复杂或定义不明确，这些工作会困难得多，甚至不可行。

    Use of a DSL offers possibilities for analysis, verification, optimization, parallelization, and transformation in terms of DSL constructs that would be much harder or unfeasible if a GPL had been used, because the GPL source code patterns involved are too complex or not well-defined.

  • 与 GPL 不同，DSL 不一定必须可执行。DSL 文献对此没有统一意见。例如，[150] 强调不可执行 DSL 的重要性，而 [48] 则要求 DSL 必须可执行。第 1.2 节讨论 DSL 的可执行性。

    Unlike GPLs, DSLs need not be executable. There is no agreement on this in the DSL literature. For instance, the importance of non-executable DSLs is emphasized in [150], but DSLs are required to be executable in [48]. We discuss DSL executability in Section 1.2.

尽管存在这些不足，应用程序库仍是 DSL 的强劲竞争者。可以公平地说，大多数 DSL 从未超越应用程序库阶段。这类方案有时称为领域特定嵌入式语言（DSEL）[76]。即便 DSL 开发工具得到改进，在许多情况下应用程序库仍将是最具成本效益的解决方案；COM 和 CORBA [133] 等组件技术的出现，又进一步增加了 DSL 与应用程序库相对优劣的复杂性。

Despite their shortcomings, application libraries are formidable competitors to DSLs. It is probably fair to say that most DSLs never get beyond the application library stage. These are sometimes called domain-specific embedded languages (DSELs) [76]. Even with improved DSL development tools, application libraries will remain the most cost-effective solution in many cases, the more so since the advent of component technologies such as COM and CORBA [133] has further complicated the relative merits of DSLs and application libraries.

例如，Microsoft Excel 的宏语言是面向电子表格应用的 DSL，它在 Excel 基本的交互模式上增加了可编程性。借助 COM，Excel 的实现被重构为应用框架和一组组件。结果是，Excel 的功能可通过它的组件 API 提供给任何支持 COM 的 GPL。因此，DSL 的功能也可通过组件实现，当像 COM 这样的组件框架本身即可实现时，就不必再创建专门的 DSL。

For instance, Microsoft Excel’s macro language is a DSL for spreadsheet applications which adds programmability to Excel’s fundamental interactive mode. Using COM, Excel’s implementation has been restructured into an application framework and a set of components. As a result, Excel functionality is made available to any COM-enabled GPL via its component API. Hence, DSL functionality can also be implemented via components and there is no need to create a special DSL when a component framework like COM will do.

1.2 DSL 的可执行性

1.2 DSL executability

关于 DSL 是否必须可执行并没有统一意见。至少可以区分以下几种情况：

There is no agreement on whether a DSL has to be executable. At least the following cases can be distinguished:

  • 具有明确定义执行语义的 DSL，例如 Excel 宏语言和 HTML。

    DSL with well-defined execution semantics (Excel macro language, HTML).

  • 应用程序生成器 [33, 129] 的输入语言。这类 DSL 往往更具声明性，并不精确规定所生成应用程序的执行语义。应用程序生成器实际上就是编译器。例子包括 APT [88] 和 GAL [139]。

    Input language of an application generator [33, 129]. Such a DSL often has a more declarative character and does not precisely specify the execution semantics of the generated application. The application generator is effectively a compiler. Examples include APT [88] and GAL [139].

  • 并非主要用于执行、但仍可用于应用程序生成的 DSL。语法规范形式 BNF 就是一个例子：它具有纯声明性，同时也可作为解析器生成器的输入语言。

    DSL not primarily meant to be executable, but nevertheless useful for application generation. The syntax specification formalism BNF is an example of a DSL with a purely declarative character that can also act as an input language for a parser generator.

  • 并非用于执行的 DSL，例如领域特定的数据结构表示 [150]。与可执行 DSL 一样，这类不可执行 DSL 也可受益于专用编辑器、格式化工具、一致性检查器、分析器和可视化工具等支持。

    DSL not meant to be executable. Examples are domain-specific data structure representations [150]. Just like their executable relatives, such non-executable DSLs may benefit from tool support such as specialized editors, pretty-printers, consistency checkers, analyzers, and visualizers.

1.3 DSL 作为复用的促进手段

1.3 DSLs as enablers of reuse

从大型软件系统构建的更广阔视角，也能认识到 DSL 的重要性。在这一背景下，DSL 的主要贡献是促进软件制品复用 [17]。可通过 DSL 复用的制品类型包括语言文法、源代码、软件设计和领域抽象。后文会给出许多 DSL 示例；这里先从复用角度列举几个。

The importance of DSLs can also be appreciated from the wider perspective of the construction of large software systems. In this context the primary contribution of DSLs is to enable reuse of software artifacts [17]. Among the types of artifacts that can be reused via DSLs are language grammars, source code, software designs, and domain abstractions. Later sections provide many examples of DSLs; here we mention a few from the perspective of reuse.

Krueger 在其关于复用的权威综述 [92] 中，按抽象、选择、专门化和集成等维度对复用方法进行分类。他特别把应用程序生成器视为一个重要的复用类别。如前所述，应用程序生成器通常以 DSL 作为输入语言，使程序员能够复用 DSL 中体现的语义概念，而无需亲自进行详细的领域分析。例子包括生成并发对象控制软件的 BDL [16] 和生成高速缓存一致性协议实现的 Teapot [30]。Krueger 指出，定义领域覆盖范围和领域概念是应用程序生成器实现者面临的困难挑战。本文将识别领域分析模式。

In his definitive survey of reuse [92], Krueger categorizes reuse approaches along the following dimensions: abstracting, selecting, specializing, and integrating. In particular, he identifies application generators as an important reuse category. As already noted, application generators often use a DSL as their input language, thereby enabling programmers to reuse semantic notions embodied in the DSL without having to perform a detailed domain analysis themselves. Examples include BDL [16] that generates software to control concurrent objects and Teapot [30] that produces implementations of cache coherence protocols. Krueger identifies definition of domain coverage and concepts as a difficult challenge for implementors of application generators. We identify patterns for domain analysis in this article.

DSL 也在 Krueger 识别的其他复用类别中发挥作用。例如，采用 DSL 时通常会复用软件体系结构，因为应用程序生成器或编译器在由 DSL 输入生成代码时会遵循标准设计。GAL [139] 能够复用视频设备驱动程序的标准体系结构。作为应用程序库实现的 DSL 显然能实现源代码复用，基于 Haskell 的 Fran [50] 等 DSL 是其中的代表。DSL 还可用于软件模式的形式化规范，例如 Nowra [126] 规定软件制造过程，而 SSC [28] 处理子系统组合。

DSLs also play a role in other reuse categories identified by Krueger. For example, software architectures are commonly reused when DSLs are employed because the application generator or compiler follows a standard design when producing code from a DSL input. For example, GAL [139] enables reuse of a standard architecture for video device drivers. DSLs implemented as application libraries clearly enable reuse of source code. Prominent examples are Haskell-based DSLs such as Fran [50]. DSLs can also be used for formal specification of software schemas. For example, Nowra [126] specifies software manufacturing processes and SSC [28] deals with subsystem composition.

复用还可能利用已有语言的文法。例如，Hancock [19] 借用 C，而 SWUL [25] 扩展 Java。XML 在 DSL 领域取得成功，很大程度上也基于其文法可被复用于特定领域。当领域专家使用的记法尚未成为计算机语言时，也可复用这种较不形式化的语言文法，并将其实现为 DSL。例如，Hawk [96] 使用已有可视化记法的文本形式。

Reuse may involve exploitation of an existing language grammar. For example, Hancock [19] piggybacks on C while SWUL [25] extends Java. Moreover, the success of XML for DSLs is largely based on reuse of its grammar for specific domains. Less formal language grammars may also be reused when notations used by domain experts, but not yet available in a computer language, are realized in a DSL. For example, Hawk [96] uses a textual form of an existing visual notation.

1.4 Scope of this article

对于本文标题中的“何时以及如何”这个问题，没有简单答案。前面提到的 DSL 优势并非没有代价：

There are no easy answers to the “when and how” question in the title of this article. The previously mentioned benefits of DSLs do not come for free:

  • DSL 开发很困难，同时需要领域专长和语言开发专长，而兼具两者的人很少。

    DSL development is hard, requiring both domain and language development expertise. Few people have both.

  • DSL 开发技术比 GPL 的开发技术更加多样，必须仔细考虑相关因素。

    DSL development techniques are more varied than those for GPLs, requiring careful consideration of the factors involved.

  • 根据用户群体规模，培训材料、语言支持、标准化和维护可能成为严重且耗时的问题。

    Depending on the size of the user community, development of training material, language support, standardization, and maintenance may become serious and time-consuming issues.

使新 DSL 开发决策复杂化的因素并不止这些。最初，人们通常很难看出 DSL 是否有用，也很难判断开发一个新 DSL 是否值得。往往只有在使用 GPL 进行领域特定软件开发并投入大量资源之后，这一点才会逐渐显现。在使用 GPL 进行了大量编程之后，合适 DSL 背后的概念才可能逐渐显现。在这种情况下，DSL 开发可能成为软件再工程或软件演化中的关键一步 [13]。

These are not the only factors complicating the decision to develop a new DSL. Initially, it is often far from evident that a DSL might be useful or that developing a new one might be worthwhile. This may become clear only after a sizable investment in domain-specific software development using a GPL has been made. The concepts underlying a suitable DSL may emerge only after a lot of GPL programming has been done. In such cases, DSL development may be a key step in software reengineering or software evolution [13].

Our patterns improve and extend earlier work on DSL design patterns, in particular [131]. This is discussed in Section 2.6. The DSL development process can be facilitated by using domain analysis tools and language development systems. These are surveyed in Section 3. 1.5 Literature

这里给出一些关于 DSL 文献的一般性指引。更具体的参考文献将在本文适当位置给出，而不是全部集中在本节。直到最近，DSL 在计算机科学研究界受到的关注仍相对较少，相关书籍也不多。这里列举 [99]，它全面介绍了 4GL；[18]，一套两卷本的软件复用论文集，其中包括 DSL 开发和程序生成；[108]，关注 DSL 在最终用户编程中的作用；[117]，一部“小语言”论文集（并非全部都是 DSL）；以及 [9]，讨论脚本语言（同样并非全部都是 DSL）。[43] 讨论了领域分析、程序生成器、生成式编程技术以及意图编程（IP）。[34] 讨论了领域分析以及使用 XML、DOM、XSLT 和相关语言及工具来生成程序。领域特定语言开发还是软件工厂方法 [68] 的重要组成部分。

We give some general pointers to the DSL literature. More specific references are given at appropriate points throughout this article rather than in this section. Until recently, DSLs received relatively little attention in the computer science research community and there are few books on the subject. We mention [99], an exhaustive account of 4GLs, [18], a two-volume collection of articles on software reuse including DSL development and program generation, [108], which focuses on the role of DSLs in end-user programming, [117], a collection of articles on little languages (not all of them DSLs), and [9], which treats scripting languages (again, not all of them DSLs). Domain analysis, program generators, generative programming techniques, and intentional programming (IP) are treated in [43]. 2 DSL 模式

2 DSL Patterns

2.1 模式分类

2.1 Pattern classification

DSL 开发可以区分为以下阶段：决策、分析、设计、实现和部署。不过在实践中，DSL 开发并不是简单的顺序过程。决策过程可能受到初步分析的影响；分析阶段又可能需要回答设计过程中出现的意外问题；而设计也经常受到实现方面考虑的影响。

The following DSL development phases can be distinguished: decision, analysis, design, implementation, and deployment. In practice, DSL development is not a simple sequential process, however. The decision process may be influenced by preliminary analysis, which in turn may have to supply answers to unforeseen questions arising during design, and design is often influenced by implementation considerations.

我们为上述各开发阶段分别关联一类模式，但部署阶段除外，因为它超出了本文范围。决策阶段对应 DSL 开发中的“何时”，其他阶段对应“如何”。决策模式描述潜在开发者可能遇到的常见情形，而过去在这些情形中曾成功开发过 DSL。在这类情况下，采用现有 DSL 或开发新 DSL 都是值得认真考虑的选择。类似地，分析模式、设计模式和实现模式分别是领域分析、DSL 设计和 DSL 实现的常见方法。属于不同 DSL 开发阶段的模式彼此独立。对于某个决策模式，几乎可以选择任意分析模式或设计模式；设计模式和实现模式之间也是如此。同一类别内的模式则未必独立，可能存在一定重叠。

We associate classes of patterns with each of the above development phases except deployment, which is beyond the scope of this article. The decision phase corresponds to the “when”-part of DSL development, the other phases to the “how”-part. Decision patterns are common situations that potential developers may find themselves in for which successful DSLs have been developed in the past. In such situations, use of an existing DSL or development of a new one is a serious option. Similarly, analysis patterns, design patterns, and implementation patterns are common approaches to, respectively, domain analysis, DSL design, and DSL implementation. Patterns corresponding to different DSL development phases are independent. For a particular decision pattern virtually any analysis or design pattern can be chosen, and the same is true for design and implementation patterns. Patterns in the same class, on the other hand, need not be independent, but may have some overlap.

我们将在独立的小节中讨论每个开发阶段及其相关模式。不可避免地，我们可能遗漏了一些模式。

We discuss each development phase and the associated patterns in a separate section. Inevitably, there may be some patterns we have missed.

2.2 决策

2.2 Decision

决定开发一个新的 DSL 通常并不容易。DSL 开发（包括部署）的投入必须通过后续更经济的软件开发和/或维护来收回。如第 1.1 节所述，对其中权衡进行定量分析很困难。在实践中，短期考虑和专长不足很容易导致决策被无限期推迟。显然，采用已有 DSL 比开发新 DSL 成本低得多，对专长要求也低得多。要找出可用 DSL 可能很困难，因为相关信息分散在各处，且常埋藏于不易发现的文档中。无论如何，采用宣传不足的 DSL 也可能被认为风险过高。

Deciding in favor of a new DSL is usually not easy. The investment in DSL development (including deployment) has to pay for itself by more economical software development and/or maintenance later on. As mentioned in Section 1.1, a quantitative treatment of the tradeoffs involved is difficult. In practice, short-term considerations and lack of expertise may easily cause indefinite postponement of the decision. Obviously, adopting an existing DSL is much less expensive and requires much less expertise than developing a new one. Finding out about available DSLs may be hard, since DSL information is scattered widely and often buried in obscure documents. Adopting DSLs that are not well-publicized might be considered too risky, anyway.

2.3 分析

2.3 Analysis

在 DSL 开发的分析阶段，需要识别问题领域并收集领域知识。输入包括各种显式或隐式领域知识来源，例如技术文档、领域专家提供的知识、现有 GPL 代码以及客户调查。领域分析的输出差异很大，但本质上由较为抽象或较为具体形式的领域特定术语和语义组成。领域分析与知识工程之间存在紧密联系，而这种联系才刚刚开始被探索。知识获取、知识表示和本体开发 [45] 都可能对分析阶段有用。

In the analysis phase of DSL development, the problem domain is identified and domain knowledge is gathered. Inputs are various sources of explicit or implicit domain knowledge, such as technical documents, knowledge provided by domain experts, existing GPL code, and customer surveys. The output of domain analysis varies widely, but consists basically of domain-specific terminology and semantics in more or less abstract form. There is a close link between domain analysis and knowledge engineering, which is only beginning to be explored. Knowledge capture, knowledge representation, and ontology development [45] are potentially useful in the analysis phase.

如何根据分析阶段收集的信息开发 DSL？目前没有明确的指导原则，不过 [139, 137] 提出了一些建议。可变性精确指出了指定系统某个实例所需的信息。这些信息必须直接写在 DSL 程序中，或者能够从 DSL 程序推导出来。术语和概念用于指导实际 DSL 构造的开发，使这些构造对应于可变部分。共性则用于定义语言的执行模型（通过一组公共操作）和基本构造。注意，DSL 的执行模型通常比 GPL 的执行模型丰富得多。基于同一次领域分析，可以开发出许多不同的 DSL，但它们都具有特征模型中发现的重要特性。

How can a DSL be developed from the information gathered in the analysis phase? No clear guidelines exist, but some are presented in [139, 137]. Variabilities indicate precisely what information is required to specify an instance of a system. This information must be specified directly in, or be derivable from, a DSL program. Terminology and concepts are used to guide the development of the actual DSL constructs corresponding to the variabilities. Commonalities are used to define the execution model (by a set of common operations) and primitives of the language. Note that the execution model of a DSL is usually much richer than that for a GPL. On the basis of a single domain analysis many different DSLs can be developed, but all share important characteristics found in the feature model.

另一种领域分析方法是 FAST（面向家族的抽象、规约与转换）[37]。FAST 是一种应用产品线架构原则的软件开发过程，因此直接关联于产品线决策模式。它为一个软件产品家族指定共同平台，其基础是产品之间的相似性和差异。FAST 方法包括以下活动：领域资格评估、领域工程、应用工程、项目管理以及家族变更。

Another domain analysis methodology is FAST (Family-Oriented Abstractions, Specification, and Translation) [37]. FAST is a software development process applying product line architecture principles, so it relates directly to the product line decision pattern. A common platform is specified for a family of software products. It is based on the similarities and differences between products. The FAST method consists of the following activities: domain qualification, domain engineering, application engineering, project management, and family change.

在领域工程期间，先分析领域，再将其实现为一组领域特定的可复用组件。FAST 中领域分析的目的是捕捉领域的共同知识，并指导已实现组件的复用。领域分析包括以下步骤：定义决策模型、共性分析、领域设计、应用建模语言设计、创建标准应用工程过程设计，以及开发应用工程设计环境。领域分析的一项重要任务是共性分析，即识别所有家族成员共有的有用抽象。共性是复用的主要来源，因此重点在于寻找共同部分。除共性外，共性分析也会发现可变性。可变性指出家族生命周期内潜在的变更来源。FAST 以结构化列表描述共性和可变性。对于每个可变属性，都要指定变化范围和绑定时间。共性分析随后用于设计应用建模语言（AML），该语言用于根据规约生成家族成员。

During domain engineering, the domain is analyzed and then implemented as a set of domain-specific reusable components. The purpose of domain analysis in FAST is to capture common knowledge about the domain and guide reuse of the implemented components. Domain analysis involves the following steps: decision model definition, commonality analysis, domain design, application modeling language design, create standard application engineering process design, and develop application engineering design environment. An important task of domain analysis is commonality analysis, which identifies useful abstractions that are common to all family members. Commonalities are the main source of reuse, thus the emphasis is on finding common parts. Besides the commonalities, variabilities are also discovered during commonality analysis. Variabilities indicate potential sources of change over the lifetime of the family. Commonalities and variabilities in FAST are specified as a structured list. For every variable property the range of variability as well as binding time are specified. Commonality analysis is later used in designing an application modeling language (AML), which is used to generate a family member from specifications.

设计 DSL 最简单的方法是以现有语言为基础。潜在好处包括实现更容易（见第 2.5 节），以及用户对语言较熟悉；但后一点只有在用户也是该现有语言的程序员时才成立，而实际情况未必如此。我们识别出三种基于现有语言的设计模式。第一，可以在现有语言的一部分上搭载领域特定特性。一种相关方法是限制现有语言，形成针对问题领域的专门化语言。这两种模式的差别，实际上取决于 DSL 与现有语言其余部分之间的边界有多严格。当某种记法已经广为人知时，经常采用这两种方法。例如，许多 DSL 包含算术表达式，通常沿用数学中的中缀运算符写法。

The easiest way to design a DSL is to base it on an existing language. Possible benefits are easier implementation (see Section 2.5) and familiarity for users, but the latter only applies if users are also programmers in the existing language, which need not be the case. We identify three patterns of design based on an existing language. First, we can piggyback domain-specific features on part of an existing language. A related approach restricts the existing language to provide a specialization targeted at the problem domain. The difference between these two patterns is really a matter of how rigid the barrier is between the DSL and the rest of the existing language. Both of these approaches are often used when a notation is already widely known. For example, many DSLs contain arithmetic expressions which are usually written in the infix-operator style of mathematics.

At the other end of the spectrum is a DSL whose design bears no relationship to any existing language. In practice, development of this kind of DSL can be extremely difficult and is hard to characterize. Well-known GPL design criteria such as readability, simplicity, orthogonality, the design principles listed by Brooks [26], and Tennent’s design principles [135] retain some validity for DSLs. However, the DSL designer has to keep in mind both the special character of DSLs as well as the fact that users need not be programmers. Since ideally the DSL adopts established notations of the domain, the designer should suppress a tendency to improve them. As stated in [151], one of the lessons learned from real DSL experiments is:

经验 T2：你几乎从来都不是在设计一种编程语言。大多数 DSL 设计者有语言设计背景。在那个领域值得推崇的正交性和形式简约原则，未必适用于 DSL 设计。尤其在迎合领域既有术语和记法时，必须谨慎，不能对语言过度修饰或过度泛化。

Lesson T2: You are almost never designing a programming language. Most DSL designers come from language design backgrounds. There the admirable principles of orthogonality and economy of form are not necessarily well-applied to DSL design. Especially in catering to the pre-existing jargon and notations of the domain, one must be careful not to embellish or over-generalize the language.

经验 T2 的推论：只设计必需的东西。学会识别自己过度设计的倾向。

Lesson T2 Corollary: Design only what is necessary. Learn to recognize your tendency to over-design.

一旦确定了与现有语言的关系，DSL 设计者就必须在实现前对设计作出规约。我们区分非正式设计和正式设计。非正式设计的规约通常采用某种自然语言形式，可能包括一组用于说明的 DSL 程序。正式设计则由采用某种现有语义定义方法 [127] 编写的规约组成。最广泛使用的形式化记法包括：用于语法规约的正则表达式和文法，以及用于语义规约的属性文法、重写系统和抽象状态机。

Once the relationship to existing languages has been determined, a DSL designer must turn to specifying the design before implementation. We distinguish between informal and formal designs. In an informal design the specification is usually in some form of natural language probably including a set of illustrative DSL programs. A formal design would consist of a specification written using one of the available semantic definition methods [127]. The most widely used formal notations include regular expressions and grammars for syntax specifications, and attribute grammars, rewrite systems and abstract state machines for semantic specification.

显然，对大多数人而言，非正式方法可能最容易。但也不应忽视正式方法。在 DSL 真正实现之前，开发语法和语义的形式化描述就可能暴露问题。此外，语言开发系统和工具可以自动实现正式设计，从而显著减少实现工作量（第 3 节）。

Clearly, an informal approach is likely to be easiest for most people. A formal approach should not be discounted, however. Development of a formal description of both syntax and semantics can bring problems to light before the DSL is actually implemented. Furthermore, formal designs can be implemented automatically by language development systems and tools, thereby significantly reducing implementation effort (Section 3).

如本节开头所述，设计模式可用两个正交维度刻画：语言发明或语言利用（扩展、专门化或搭载），以及非正式描述或正式描述。图 2 标出了表 8 中各 DSL 在设计模式平面中的位置。我们注意到，使用语言发明模式设计 DSL 时，正式描述比非正式描述更常见；使用语言利用模式时则相反。

解释和编译对 DSL 与 GPL 同样适用，尽管 DSL 的特殊性质常使它们适合采用预处理、嵌入等其他更高效的实现方法。这一观点不同于 [131]：该文认为 DSL 开发与 GPL 开发截然不同，因为前者通常只是项目的一小部分，因此 DSL 开发成本必须适中。然而，情况并非总是如此；解释器和编译器／应用生成器在实践中被广泛使用。

Interpretation and compilation are as relevant for DSLs as for GPLs, even though the special character of DSLs often makes them amenable to other, more efficient, implementation methods, such as preprocessing and embedding. This viewpoint is at variance with [131], where it is argued that DSL development is radically different from GPL development, since the former is usually just a small part of a project and hence DSL development costs have to be modest. This is not always the case, however, and interpreters and compilers/application generators are widely used in practice.

宏和子程序是用于 DSL 实现的经典语言扩展机制。子程序发展出了嵌入式实现，而宏由预处理处理。[22] 给出了近期的一篇宏综述。宏展开往往独立于基础语言的语法，不能保证展开结果在语法上正确，而要由后续阶段的解释器或编译器检查。这是预处理器的典型情况。

Macros and subroutines are the classical language extension mechanisms used for DSL implementation. Subroutines have given rise to implementation by embedding, while macros are handled by preprocessing. A recent survey of macros is given in [22]. Macro expansion is often independent of the syntax of the base language and the syntactical correctness of the expanded result is not guaranteed, but is checked at a later stage by the interpreter or compiler. This situation is typical for preprocessors.

C++ 支持一种语言特定的预处理方法：模板元编程 [143, 142]。它利用模板展开，在编译时生成领域特定代码。模板元编程已被大量用于开发 C++ 数学库：这些库借助 C++ 用户可定义的运算符记法和重载，提供熟悉的领域记法，同时取得良好性能。Blitz++ [144] 就是一个例子。

C++ supports a language-specific preprocessing approach: template metaprogramming [143, 142]. It uses template expansion to achieve compile-time generation of domain-specific code. Significant mileage has been made out of template metaprogramming to develop mathematical libraries for C++ which have familiar domain notation using C++ user-definable operator notation and overloading, but also achieve good performance. An example is Blitz++ [144].

在嵌入方法中，通过定义特定的抽象数据类型和运算符来扩展现有 GPL（宿主语言），从而实现 DSL。随后便可用这些新构造描述领域特定问题。因此，新语言具有宿主语言的全部能力，而应用工程师无需学习太多宿主语言知识就能编程。为了尽可能接近领域特定记法，嵌入方法可以使用宿主语言提供的任何用户可定义运算符语法特性。例如，常见做法是开发 C++ 类库，将现有运算符重载为领域特定语义。虽然这种技术相当强大，但把熟悉的运算符重载为不熟悉的语义也存在陷阱。尽管宿主语言可以是任意通用语言，许多研究者已表明，函数式语言往往很合适 [77, 84]。原因在于函数式语言具有惰性求值、高阶函数，以及带多态和重载的强类型等特性。

In the embedding approach, a DSL is implemented by extending an existing GPL (the host language) by defining specific abstract data types and operators. A domain-specific problem then can be described with these new constructs. Therefore, the new language has all the power of the host language, but an application engineer can become a programmer without learning too much of it. To approximate domain-specific notations as closely as possible, the embedding approach can use any features for user-definable operator syntax the host language has to offer. For example, it is common to develop C++ class libraries where the existing operators are overloaded with domain-specific semantics. Although this technique is quite powerful, pitfalls exist in overloading familiar operators to have unfamiliar semantics. Although the host language in the embedding approach can be any general-purpose language, functional languages are often appropriate, as shown by many researchers [77, 84]. This is due to functional language features such as lazy evaluation, higher-order functions, and strong typing with polymorphism and overloading.

扩展现有语言实现也可视为一种嵌入形式。差别通常只是程度问题。在解释器或编译器方法中，通常只为实现增加少数特性，例如新数据类型及其运算符。真正的嵌入则可能扩展出完整的领域特定语言特性。不过，在这两种情况下，扩展实现通常都非常困难。如何安全、模块化地进行扩展，仍是大量研究的主题。由于编译器尤其难以扩展，许多研究针对预处理器和可扩展编译器，使其能够加入领域特定优化规则和／或领域特定代码生成。例子包括 CodeBoost C++ 预处理器 [8] 和 Simplicissimus GCC 编译器插件 [122] 中的用户自定义优化规则、IBM Montana 可扩展 C++ 编程环境 [130]、GHC Haskell 编译器 [111] 中的用户自定义优化规则，以及 Broadway 编译器 [70] 对应用程序库领域特定语义的利用。有些可扩展编译器，例如 OpenC++ [32]，支持元对象协议。这是一种用于指定语言扩展和转换的面向对象接口 [88]。

Extending an existing language implementation can also be seen as a form of embedding. The difference is usually a matter of degree. In an interpreter or compiler approach the implementation would usually only be extended with a few features, such as new data types and operators for them. For a proper embedding, the extensions might encompass full-blown domain-specific language features. In both settings, however, extending implementations is often very difficult. Techniques for doing so in a safe and modular fashion are still the subject of much research. Since compilers are particularly hard to extend, much of this work is aimed at preprocessors and extensible compilers allowing addition of domain-specific optimization rules and/or domain-specific code generation. We mention user-definable optimization rules in the CodeBoost C++ preprocessor [8] and in the Simplicissimus GCC compiler plug-in [122], the IBM Montana extensible C++ programming environment [130], user-definable optimization rules in the GHC Haskell compiler [111], and exploitation of domain-specific semantics of application libraries in the Broadway compiler [70]. Some extensible compilers, such as OpenC++ [32], support a metaobject protocol. This is an object-oriented interface for specifying language extensions and transformations [88].

基于 COTS 的方法围绕现有工具和记法构建 DSL。通常，这种方法根据领域规则，以受限方式应用现有功能。例如，通用工具 Powerpoint 已被用于领域特定的图表编辑 [150]。目前基于 XML 的 DSL 日益流行，是这种方法的另一个例子 [63, 110]。对于基于 XML 的 DSL，文法使用 DTD 或 XML Schema 描述，其中非终结符类似于元素，终结符类似于数据内容。产生式类似于元素定义：元素名是左部，内容模型是右部。开始符号类似于 DTD 中的文档元素。使用 DOM 解析器或 SAX（Simple API for XML）工具，可以直接获得解析能力。由于解析树也可用 XML 编码，因此可使用 XSLT 转换进行代码生成。所以，XML 和 XML 工具可用于实现编程语言编译器 [59]。

The COTS-based approach builds a DSL around existing tools and notations. Typically this approach involves applying existing functionality in a restricted way, according to domain rules. For example, the general-purpose Powerpoint tool has been applied in a domain-specific setting for diagram editing [150]. The current prominence of XML-based DSLs is another instance of this approach [63, 110]. For an XML-based DSL, grammar is described using a DTD or XML scheme, where nonterminals are analogous to elements and terminals to data content. Productions are like element definitions, where the element name is the left-hand side and the content model is the right-hand side. The start symbol is analogous to the document element in a DTD. Using a DOM parser or SAX (Simple API for XML) tool, parsing comes for free. Since the parse tree can be encoded in XML as well, XSLT transformations can be used for code generation. Therefore, XML and XML tools can be used to implement a programming language compiler [59].

许多 DSL 项目以混合方式使用多种方法，从而利用不同方法的优势。例如，嵌入可与可扩展编译器中的用户自定义领域特定优化相结合；在某些场景下，解释器方法与编译器方法也变得难以区分（见下一节）。

Many DSL endeavors apply a number of these approaches in a hybrid fashion. Thus the advantages of different approaches can be exploited. For instance, embedding can be combined with user-defined domain-specific optimization in an extensible compiler, and the interpreter and compiler approach become indistinguishable in some settings (see next section).

In the functional setting, and in particular if Haskell is used, some of these shortcomings can be reduced by using monads to modularize the language implementation [77]. The decision diagram on how to proceed with DSL implementation (Figure 3) shows when a particular implementation approach is more appropriate. If the DSL is designed from scratch with no commonality with existing languages (invention pattern), the recommended approach is to implement it by embedding, unless domain-specific analysis, verification, optimization, parallelization, or transformation (AVOPT) is required, a domain-specific notation must be strictly obeyed, or the user community is expected to be large.

如果 DSL 纳入了现有语言（的一部分），通常也希望复用现有语言实现（的对应部分）。除此之外，还可以根据采用的语言利用子模式选择不同实现模式。搭载或专门化设计可以用解释器、编译器／应用生成器或预处理器实现，但嵌入或使用可扩展编译器／解释器并不合适；不过，某些语言（例如 Smalltalk）可以通过可扩展编译器／解释器实现专门化。对于搭载设计，从实现复用角度看，将 DSL 转换为所搭载语言的预处理器最好，但预处理在其他方面存在严重不足。语言扩展设计可以使用上述所有实现模式。在这种情况下，从实现复用角度看，嵌入和使用可扩展编译器／解释器尤其有吸引力。

If the DSL incorporates (part of) an existing language, one would like to reuse (the corresponding part of) the existing language’s implementation as well. Apart from this, various implementation patterns may apply, depending on the language exploitation subpattern used. A piggyback or specialization design can be implemented using an interpreter, compiler/application generator, or preprocessor, but embedding or use of an extensible compiler/interpreter are not suitable, although specialization can be done using an extensible compiler/interpreter in some languages (Smalltalk, for instance). In the case of piggyback, a preprocessor transforming the DSL to the language it piggybacks on is best from the viewpoint of implementation reuse, but preprocessing has serious shortcomings in other respects. A language extension design can be implemented using all of the above-mentioned implementation patterns. From the viewpoint of implementation reuse, embedding and use of an extensible compiler/interpreter are particularly attractive in this case.

如果有多种实现模式适用，则收益（见本节讨论）与实现工作量之比最高者最优，除非像语言发明的情况一样，需要 AVOPT、必须严格遵守领域特定记法，或者预期用户群体很大。如前所述，编译器／应用生成器所需的实现工作量最大。成本较低的方法按成本由高到低依次为：解释器、预处理、可扩展编译器／解释器、嵌入。另一方面，就 DSL 用户获得的收益而言，编译器／应用生成器和解释器最好。收益较少的方法按收益由高到低依次为：可扩展编译器／解释器、嵌入、预处理。实践中很少进行这种成本收益分析，决策通常只受实现者经验驱动。当然应考虑经验，但它并非唯一相关因素。

If more than one implementation pattern applies, the one having the highest ratio of benefit (see discussion in this section) to implementation effort is optimal, unless, as in the language invention case, AVOPT is required, a domain-specific notation must be strictly obeyed, or the user community is expected to be large. As already mentioned, a compiler/application generator scores worst in terms of implementation effort. Less costly are (in descending order): interpreter, preprocessing, extensible compiler/interpreter, and embedding. On the other hand, a compiler/application generator and interpreter score best as far as benefit to DSL users is concerned. Less benefit is obtained from (in descending order): extensible compiler/interpreter, embedding, and preprocessing. In practice, such a cost-benefit analysis is rarely performed, and the decision is driven only by implementor experience. Of course, the latter should be taken into account, but it is not the only relevant factor.

3.1 设计与实现支持

3.1 Design and implementation support

如前所述，DSL 开发很困难，同时需要领域知识和语言开发专长。使用语言开发系统或工具包可以简化开发过程。表 16 列出了一些实际用于 DSL 开发的系统和工具包。它们的能力和发展阶段差别很大，但都基于同一原则：根据语言描述生成工具 [71]。生成的工具可能只是一个一致性检查器和解释器，也可能是针对该 DSL 的集成开发环境（IDE），包括语法制导编辑器、美化打印器、（增量）一致性检查器、分析工具、解释器或编译器／应用生成器，以及调试器（假定 DSL 可执行）。如第 1.2 节所述，不可执行 DSL 也可受益于语法制导编辑器、美化打印器、一致性检查器和分析器等工具支持。这些工具可以用相同方式生成。

As we have seen, DSL development is hard, requiring both domain knowledge and language development expertise. The development process can be facilitated by using a language development system or toolkit. Some systems and toolkits that have actually been used for DSL development are listed in Table 16. They have widely different capabilities and are in widely different stages of development, but are based on the same general principle: they generate tools from language descriptions [71]. The tools generated may vary from a consistency checker and interpreter to an integrated development environment (IDE) consisting of a syntax-directed editor, a prettyprinter, an (incremental) consistency checker, analysis tools, an interpreter or compiler/application generator, and a debugger for the DSL in question (assuming it is executable). As noted in Section 1.2, non-executable DSLs may also benefit from various kinds of tool support such as syntax-directed editors, prettyprinters, consistency checkers, and analyzers. These can be generated in the same way.

其中一些系统支持特定的 DSL 设计方法，另一些则基本独立于具体方法。例如，Sprint [36] 假定已有 DSL 解释器，然后通过部分求值，自动将 DSL 程序转换为已编译程序，以消除解释开销。另一些系统，例如 ASF+SDF [23]、DMS [12] 和 Stratego [145]，不仅允许用解释方式定义 DSL，也接受转换式或翻译式定义。另一方面，它们未必支持针对特定程序对 DSL 解释器进行部分求值。

Some of these systems support a specific DSL design methodology, while others have a largely methodology-independent character. For instance, Sprint [36] assumes an interpreter for the DSL to be given and then uses partial evaluation to remove the interpretation overhead by automatically transforming a DSL program into a compiled program. Other systems, such as ASF+SDF [23], DMS [12], and Stratego [145], would not only allow an interpretive definition of the DSL, but would also accept a transformational or translational one. On the other hand, they might not support partial evaluation of a DSL interpreter given a specific program.

这些系统的输入是用专用元语言描述的、待开发 DSL 的各个方面。根据 DSL 类型不同，一些重要的语言方面包括语法、美化打印、一致性检查、分析、执行、翻译、转换和调试。用于描述这些方面的元语言，本身恰好就是针对相应方面的 DSL。例如，DSL 语法通常使用接近 BNF 的形式描述，BNF 是语法规约的事实标准（表 1）。语言开发系统生成的对应工具是解析器。

The input to these systems is a description of various aspects of the DSL to be developed in terms of specialized metalanguages. Depending on the type of DSL, some important language aspects are syntax, prettyprinting, consistency checking, analysis, execution, translation, transformation, and debugging. It so happens that the metalanguages used for describing these aspects are themselves DSLs for the particular aspect in question. For instance, DSL syntax is usually described in something close to BNF, the de facto standard for syntax specification (Table 1). The corresponding tool generated by the language development system is a parser.

虽然描述语言各方面的专用元语言因系统而异，但它们往往（并非总是）基于规则。例如，根据系统不同，程序或脚本的一致性可能需要通过带属性的语法规则（BNF 的扩展）、条件重写规则或迁移规则来检查。更多细节可参见 [127]。

Although the various specialized metalanguages used for describing language aspects differ from system to system, they are often (but not always) rule based. For instance, depending on the system, the consistency of programs or scripts may have to be checked in terms of attributed syntax rules (an extension of BNF), conditional rewrite rules, or transition rules. See, for instance, [127] for further details.

4 结论与开放问题

4 Conclusions and Open Problems

DSL 永远不会成为解决所有软件工程问题的方案，但目前由于（潜在）DSL 开发者缺乏可靠知识，其应用受到不合理的限制。为帮助改善这种情况，我们区分了 DSL 开发的五个阶段，并为除部署以外的各阶段识别了模式，汇总于表 20。此外，我们还讨论了可用于促进开发过程、尤其是其后期阶段的语言开发系统和工具包。

DSLs will never be a solution to all software engineering problems, but their application is currently unduly limited by a lack of reliable knowledge available to (potential) DSL developers. To help remedy this situation, we distinguished five phases of DSL development and identified patterns in each phase, except deployment. These are summarized in Table 20. Furthermore, we discussed language development systems and toolkits that can be used to facilitate the development process, especially its later phases.

An important issue is how to link formal domain analysis with DSL design and implementation.

设计与实现　如何使不熟悉 GPL 开发的领域专家更容易设计和实现 DSL？一些方法如下，它们并不互斥：

Design and implementation How can DSL design and implementation be made easier for domain experts not versed in GPL development? Some approaches are (not mutually exclusive):

  • 利用参数化的语言构建块，以增量式、模块化和可扩展的方式构建 DSL。这对 DSL 尤其重要，因为它们的变化比 GPL 更频繁 [20, 150]。这一方向已取得一些进展 [1, 36, 77, 103]。

    Building DSLs in an incremental, modular, and extensible way from parameterized language building blocks. This is of particular importance for DSLs, since they change more frequently than GPLs [20, 150]. Progress in this direction is being made [1, 36, 77, 103].

  • 一个相关问题是如何将现有 GPL 和 DSL 的不同部分组合成新的 DSL。例如，在 Microsoft .NET 框架中，许多 GPL 都编译到公共语言运行时（CLR）[64]。这能否帮助我们将 GPL 中选定的部分纳入新的 DSL？

    A related issue is how to combine different parts of existing GPLs and DSLs into a new DSL. For instance, in the Microsoft .NET framework many GPLs are compiled to the Common Language Runtime (CLR) [64]. Can this be helpful in including selected parts of GPLs into a new DSL?

  • 提供“模式感知”的开发支持。例如，Sprint 系统 [36] 为解释器模式提供部分求值支持（见第 3.1 节）。其他模式也可能受益于专门支持。嵌入支持将在下一段单独讨论。

    Provide “pattern aware” development support. The Sprint system [36], for instance, provides partial evaluation support for the interpreter pattern (see Section 3.1). Other patterns might benefit from specialized support as well. Embedding support is discussed separately in the next paragraph.

  • 通过支持对语法或美化打印等选定语言方面进行示例描述（DBE，description by example），减少学习语言开发系统中某些专用元语言的需要。DBE 的易用性源于这样一个事实：给出预期行为的示例不需要专门的元语言，或者只需要用到其中很小的一部分。例如，从示例语句中推断文法可能是可行的，尤其是因为许多 DSL 规模很小。这当然不是什么新想法 [41, 108]，但仍有待实现。[40] 报告了一些初步结果。

    Reduce the need for learning some of the specialized metalanguages of language development systems by supporting description by example (DBE) of selected language aspects like syntax or prettyprinting. The user-friendliness of DBE is due to the fact that examples of intended behavior do not require a specialized metalanguage, or only a small part of it. Grammar inference from example sentences, for instance, may be viable, especially since many DSLs are small. This is certainly no new idea [41, 108], but it remains to be realized. Some preliminary results are reported in [40].

  • 如何将语言开发系统和工具包生成的 DSL 开发工具与其他软件开发工具集成？采用基于商用现成产品（COTS）的方法时，DOM 和 XML 解析器等 XML 技术作为 CASE 工具的统一数据交换格式具有很大潜力。另见 [7, 34]。

    How can DSL development tools generated by language development systems and toolkits be integrated with other software development tools? Using a COTS-based approach, XML technologies such as DOM and XML-parsers have great potential as a uniform data interchange format for CASE tools. See also [7, 34].

嵌入　GPL 应当在语法和语义两方面，为嵌入 DSL 提供更强大的支持。一些问题如下：

Embedding GPLs should provide more powerful support for embedding DSLs, both syntactically and semantically. Some issues are:

  • 嵌入受到 GPL 所提供的用户可定义语法十分有限这一问题的制约。或许令人意外的是，多年来 GPL 并未呈现出增强用户可定义语法的趋势。事实上，情况恰恰相反：宏和用户自定义运算符变得不那么流行了。Java 完全不支持用户自定义运算符。另一方面，表 16 中的一些语言开发系统，例如 ASF+SDF，以及在一定程度上的 Stratego，支持具有完全通用的用户可定义上下文无关语法的元语言。虽然在表达能力和效率方面，这些元语言不能作为嵌入宿主直接与 GPL 竞争，但它们可以用来表达源到源转换，将嵌入 GPL 的用户自定义 DSL 语法翻译为适当的 API 调用。[25] 对这一方法进行了详细讨论。

    Embedding suffers from the very limited user-definable syntax offered by GPLs. Perhaps surprisingly, there is no trend toward more powerful user-definable syntax in GPLs over the years. In fact, just the opposite has happened. Macros and user-definable operators have become less popular. Java has no user-definable operators at all. On the other hand, some of the language development systems in Table 16, such as ASF+SDF and to some extent Stratego, support metalanguages featuring fully general user-definable context-free syntax. Although these metalanguages cannot compete directly with GPLs as embedding hosts as far as expressiveness and efficiency are concerned, they can be used to express a source-to-source transformation to translate user-defined DSL syntax embedded in a GPL to appropriate API calls. See [25] for an extensive discussion of this approach.

  • 改进嵌入支持不仅涉及语言特性，也涉及语言实现，尤其是允许添加领域特定优化规则和／或领域特定代码生成的预处理器或可扩展编译器。参见第 2.5.1 节所列参考文献以及 [65, 119]。另一种做法是由 GPL 本身将领域特定优化规则作为一种特殊的编译器指令提供。不过，这类编译器扩展会显著增加嵌入过程的复杂性，其成本收益比还需要进一步仔细研究。

    Improved embedding support is not only a matter of language features, but also of language implementation, and in particular of preprocessors or extensible compilers allowing addition of domain-specific optimization rules and/or domain-specific code generation. See the references given in Section 2.5.1 and [65, 119]. Alternatively, the GPL itself might feature domain-specific optimization rules as a special kind of compiler directive. Such compiler extension makes the embedding process significantly more complex, however, and its cost-benefit ratio needs further scrutiny.

什么是 AI 水文What slop is

治疗之前，先得把病症说清楚。“听起来像 AI”是一种感觉，不是定义，而你没法修复一种感觉。所以，我首先尝试从机械层面把 AI 水文界定清楚。我反复遇到的问题，最后可以归为六种习惯，每一种你都很熟悉。第一，同义词轮换。Before the fix, we have to be precise about the disease. Sounds like AI is a feeling, not a definition. And you cannot fix a feeling. So, the first thing I did was to try to pinch slope down mechanically. And everything I kept running into collapses into six habits. You already know every one of them. One, synonym rotation.

同一个段落里，同一个东西换了三种叫法：user、customer、client，也就是用户、顾客、客户。你始终不确定，它们是不是同一个人。第二，层层保留的含糊说法。各种辅助性动词不断叠加，直到什么也没说：“值得注意的是，这可能潜在地有助于改善……”五个动词，却没有一个真正有力的动词。第三，把动词冻成名词。不说 analyze，而说 perform an analysis of；不说 help，而说 provides assistance。语法学家把它叫作“名词化”。The text calls the same thing three names in one paragraph. The user, the customer, and the client. You are never sure they are the same one person. Two, hedging. Literal help of verbs stack until nothing happens. It's important to note that this may potentially help to improve. Five verbs and zero verb. Three, verbs frozen into nouns. A perform an analysis of instead of analyze. And provides assistance instead of help. The grammar people call it nominalization.

你只需要知道，它会让句子更长、更无力。第四，营销形容词：seamless、robust、powerful、cutting-edge，无缝、稳健、强大、前沿。这些词只是宣称质量，并没有展示质量。第五，[鼻息声] 冗长连缀句。四个意思，本该写成四句话，却用长破折号和分号缝成一句。第六，过于闲聊化的短语动词，比如 spin up、reach out、dive into。这个清单重要，是因为每一项都是具体、可以命名的写作动作。All you need to know is that it makes sentences longer and weaker. Four, marketing adjectives. Seamless, robust, powerful, cutting edge. Words that claim quality instead of showing it. Five, [snorts] run on sentences. Four ideas stitched together with m dashes and semicolons when they should be four sentences. Six, overly chatty phrasal verbs. A spin up, reach out, dive into. And here's why this list matters. STE 是什么What STE is

那么，这套航空标准到底是什么？二十世纪七十年代末，我显然还没有出生，所以，下面是根据我的研究讲述的故事。当时，欧洲航空业遇到了一个可能致命的问题：维修手册是用英语写的。So, what is this aircraft standard? Now, I was obviously not around in the late 1970s. So, this is a story the way my research tells it. Back then, European airspace had a deadly problem. Maintenance manuals were written in English.

但全世界阅读这些手册的大多数维修技师，并不是英语母语者。在飞机上，一条指令读错了，就可能换来一场葬礼。因此，行业制定了一种受控英语。它不是风格指南，而是一套规范，1986 年首次发布。当前的第九版发布于 2025 年 1 月，可以从官方网站免费下载，完整文档共 434 页。But, most of the mechanics reading them around the world were not native English speakers. And one misread instruction on an aircraft is a funeral. So, industry built a controlled version of English. Not a style guide, a specification. And first released in 1986. The current version, issue nine, came out in January 2025. And you can download it for free from the official site. The full document is 434 pages.

坦白说，我并没有坐下来逐页读完。我深入翻查，提炼出真正重要的部分：规则、词典，以及它们背后的逻辑。这花了一些时间，但你不必再亲自打开它。标准分两部分。第一部分是操作规则；第二部分是真正让我惊讶的地方：一部收录大约 900 个核准词汇的词典。And I'll be honest with you, I didn't sit down and read every single page. I dug through it and mined out the parts that matter. The rules, the dictionary, and the logic behind them. That took a while, but you will never have to open it. It has two parts. Part one is procedures. Part two is the part that genuinely surprised me. A dictionary of roughly 900 approved words.

每个词都只有一个明确含义，也只承担一种语法功能。在 STE 中，fall 只能表示“受重力作用向下移动”，不能拿来表示“减少”。如果普通英语用五个词表达同一个意思，STE 只留一个，其余不用。你可以用 start，但不能用 begin、commence、initiate 或 originate。看看这带来了什么：同义词轮换，AI 水文的第一种习惯，从设计上就被消灭了。Where every word gets the exact one meaning and one grammatical job. Now, in AST, fall only means to move down by gravity. You are not allowed to use it to mean decrease. And where English gives five words for one idea, AST keeps one and deletes the rest. You get start. You do not get begin, commence, initiate, or originate. Notice what that just did. Synonym rotation. Slope habit number one is dead by design already.

我还发现了一个数字，说明它早已走出原来的行业范围：如今，申请获取这套标准的人中，有 64% 完全不属于航空航天和国防领域。And one more number I found that tells you this thing escaped its cage. Today, 64% of the people are requesting the standard or outside aerospace and defense entirely.

Phrasal verbs killed by rule 9.3. You remove the panel.

而不是 take it off。关键就在这里：这套写法从来不是为 AI 设计的，而是四十年前，为了消除处于压力之下的人类读者所面对的歧义而设计的。AI 水文，不过是摆出漂亮姿态的含糊表达。航空业花了四十年，把解药做出来了，只不过原本治疗的是另一类病人。You do not take it off. And here's the punchline. A style was never designed for AI. It was designed 40 years ago to remove ambiguity for a stressed human reader. AI slop is just ambiguity with good posture. The aerospace industry has spent four decades engineering the cure but for a different patient.

关于人类读者的证据The human evidence

我去查了研究，确实有实证证据。I went to digging for studies and there's a real evidence.

我会原原本本地告诉你，包括那些削弱结论的部分。我找到的最有力研究来自 1996 年，由 Sherback、Dury 和 Volets 完成。他们给 175 名飞机维修技师提供真实的维修工卡，一部分用普通英语，一部分用简化英语。理解正确率从 76% 提升到 86%。对于非英语母语者，也就是这套标准原本服务的人群，则从 69% 提升到 87%。注意这个结果。I will give it to you straight including the parts that weaken it. The strongest study I found is from 1996 by Sherback, Dury, and Volets. They gave 175 aircraft technicians real maintenance work cards. 这些约束，把原本阅读吃力的人拉到了母语者的水平。微软研究院在 2007 年做了一项更大的研究，把 520 个句子翻译成中文、法语、荷兰语和阿拉伯语。在每一种语言中，受控版本都胜过普通版本，而结果纯属偶然的概率低于千分之一。他们的数据中，最有效的一条规则，是删去华丽和过分正式的措辞，这几乎就是“反水文”的字面定义。不过，接下来要诚实一点。The constraint pulled the struggling readers up to the native speaker level. 翻译质量的提升是真实的，但幅度很小：在四分制量表上，大约只提高了 0.1 分。空客的一项研究发现，过度简化甚至会适得其反，让读者读得更慢。更短并不总是更清楚，而且这里的所有研究测量的都是理解，不是记忆。没有人证明 STE 文本更容易记住，只证明更容易理解。所以，把它用于提高清晰度，不要指望奇迹。Those translations gains were real, but small. 测试The test

而刚才讲的所有事情，都有一个缺口。And here's the gap in everything I just told you.

那些证据，研究的都是飞机手册和人类作者。还没有人把这套标准放到 AI 输出上测试。那条爆红的说法，只是基于类比的推测，所以我亲自做了实验。用简单的话说，设置如下：六种开发者确实会做的写作任务，分别是 README、拉取请求描述、API 文档、错误消息、入门指南和弃用通知。每一种都用四种方式生成。第一种是普通基线。All of that evidence is about aircraft manuals and human writers. Nobody had ever tested this standard on AI output. The Wired claim was a guess by analogy, so I ran it. The setup in plain words. Six real writing jobs a developer actually does. A readme, a pull request description, API docs, an error message, a getting started guide, and a depreciation notice. Each one generated four ways. A plain baseline.

另外三种，是大家现在都在用的禁词清单、奥威尔的六条规则，以及我的 STE 方案。然后，我写了一个检查器，也就是一个小脚本，统计那些可以机械检查的东西：每句词数、分号、被动语态、短语动词、营销形容词。分数是每一百词的违规次数，越低越干净。结果并不含糊：基线 4.36；禁词清单 4.21，只改善 3%。百分之三。奥威尔规则 2.48，降低 43%。The band words list everyone is using right now. Orwell's six rules, and my STE score. Then I wrote a linter, a small script that counts the mechanical stuff. Words per sentence, semicolons, passive voice, phrasal verbs, marketing adjectives. The score is violations per hundred words. And lower is cleaner. The results aren't cloudy. The baseline scored 4.36. The band words list 4.21. This is a 3% improvement. Three. Orwell's rules 2.48. Down 43%.

简化技术英语则是 1.12，降低 74%，不到基线水文程度的三分之一。而在这些数据里，还藏着整个实验最有力的一项反证：禁词清单确实消除了长破折号，从六个降到一个，因为这正是你要求它做的那件事。但总体水文程度几乎没动。你禁用了长破折号，得到的只是一段没有长破折号的水文。And simplified technical English 1.12. Down 74%. Less than a third of the baseline slope. And buried in that data is the most damning number of the whole experiment. The banned words list did kill em dashes, six down to one, because that's the one thing you told it to do. And the overall slope barely moved. You banned the em dashes and you got a slope paragraph with no em dash in it.

两个模型连写水文的方式都不同。Claude 的水文比较张扬：长破折号、seamless、冗长的连缀句。GPT 的水文则很安静：句子看起来干净，只是太长、太多被动语态。同一种病，不同症状。能同时经受两个模型检验的结论，更窄，也更真实：给模型一套真正的写作体系，水文就会减少一半或更多。在我试过的每个模型、每次测试里，STE 都是最好，或并列最好。The two models even sloped differently. Claude's slope is flashy, em dashes, seamless long run ons, but GPT slope is quiet, clean looking sentences that are simply too long and too passive. Same disease, but with the different symptoms. The finding that survives both models is narrower and truer. Give the model a real writing system and slope drops by half or more. Every time on every model I tried. ST was the best or tied for best.

逐个禁词，只是正确思路中一种可靠性较差的版本。有个重要限制必须说明：我的检查器并不等于完整标准。STE 的维护者明确表示，没有软件能够认证完全合规。有些规则，需要人来判断句子是否讲得通，他们说得对。但那些需要判断的规则，并不是这里针对水文的机械规则。Banning words one at a time is just a less reliable version of the right idea. One caveat because it matters, my linter is not the full standard. The people who maintains ST say plainly that no software can certify full compliance. Some rules need a human to judge whether a sentence makes sense or not, and they are right actually. But the judgement rules are not the slope rules.

水文中的这些问题属于机械层面，百分之百可以用脚本检查。而这个想法甚至不新。据我查到的资料，大约从 1990 年起，波音就已经提供简化英语检查器，配有一个包含 350 条规则的解析器。机器检查写作，是一个早已解决、平凡而且经过实战检验的类别。我们只是把它对准了一个新问题。Slope is the mechanical stuff, and the mechanical stuff is 100% checkable by a script. And that idea is not even new. From what I found, Boeing has shipped a simple fighter English checker with a 350 rule parser since about 1990. Mission checked writing is a solved, boring, battle tested category. We just pointed it at a new problem.

我是怎样使用它的How I use it

那么，我实际怎样用？不是把 434 页内容直接粘贴给模型。So, how do I actually use this? Not by pasting 434 pages into the model.

这套标准受版权保护，而把 900 个词条都塞进去，只会白白消耗上下文。应该提炼它。我做了一个小型技能文件，包含两种模式。严格模式用于操作流程和错误消息，也就是误读会付出代价的地方，使用完整规则和硬性的长度上限。STE 风格模式则用于普通行文。The standard is copyrighted and 900 dictionary words would burn your context for nothing. You distill it. I built a small scale file with two modes. Strict mode is for procedures and error messages, anywhere a wrong reading has a cost. It gets the full rules and the hard lines caps. AST flavored mode is for normal prose.

它保留句子纪律、段落纪律，以及不用短语动词的习惯，但去掉词典锁定，因为博客文章需要表达空间，不需要被换掉人格。有些聪明人甚至觉得，这样也太多了。一位工程师回复那个帖子：“STE100 有点过头了。我改写了一套很小的 Vale 规则，能拿到九成收益。”他说得没错，这才是真正的要点：标准只是一种选择。It gives a sentence discipline, the paragraph discipline, and the no phrasal verbs habit, but drops a dictionary lockdown because a blog post needs range, not personality transplants. And some smart people think even that is too much. One engineer replied to the thread, "AST 100 is a bit too much. I wrote a tiny well rule set instead. 90% of the benefit." And he's not wrong, and that's the real takeaway. The standard is one option.

一套简短、可检查的规则，本身就是一种产品。实际上，Caveman 这样的产品，几个月前就已经做出来了，也已经有人采用。但一定要记住这个限制：STE 解决的是水文的形式，不是内容。检查器可以把一段空洞的话，变成一段干净、自信、标点规范的空话，却不能让它变得真实。“水文”是两个问题披着同一件外套：写得不好，以及没东西可说。A short checkable rule set is a product. And this product is actually Caveman, which has been already implemented a couple of months ago, and people have adopted using it, too. But burn this caveat in. AST fixes the form of slop, not the substance. A linter can turn a whole of paragraph into a clean, confident, well punctuated whole paragraph. It cannot make it true. Slop is two problems wearing one coat, bad writing and nothing to say.

这只能解决第一个问题。This fixes the first one only.

结论The verdict

所以，结论是什么？一套 1986 年的飞机维修标准，能治好 AI 水文吗？在形式上，可以，而且比我测试过的其他办法都好。这不是巧合：水文的本质是含糊，而 STE 是四十年来直接针对含糊进行的工程实践。但它不会让你的文章变好，只会让它不再那么水。凡是需要独特声音的文字，都别拿它来套。So, the verdict. Does a 1986 aircraft maintenance standard cure a slop? One form, yes, better than anything else I tested, and for a reason that is not an accident. Slop is ambiguity, and STE is 40 years of engineering aimed directly at ambiguity. But it will not make your writing good. Only unsloppy. And keep it away from anything that needs a voice.

把营销文案放进 STE，就像把扭矩扳手规范套在一首诗上。只有在“不引人注意的清晰表达”就是全部任务时，才使用它：文档、拉取请求、错误消息、智能体输出，除此之外不要乱用。坦白说，真正的启示甚至不在这套标准本身，而在 Vox 指出的、也被我的数字支持的那一点：永远逐个禁用 delve 之类的词，解决不了 AI 写作的问题。Running your marketing copy through STE is a torque wrench spec applied to a poem. Use it where invisible clarity is the entire job. Docs, pull requests, error messages, agent output, and nowhere else. And honestly, this standard is not even the lesson. The lesson is the thing Box pointed at and my numbers back up. You will not fix AI writing by banning dull of one word at a time forever.

你要给模型一套可以自我检查的体系，而不是一张它总能绕过去的黑名单。奥威尔的规则是一套体系，STE 是更严格、而且可以测试的一套，十行 Vale 配置则是一套更小的体系。选一个，把它变成输出必须通过的测试，别再打地鼠了。约束写作者，才能解放读者。You fix it by giving the model a system it can check itself against instead of a blacklist it will always root around. Orwell is a system. STE is a stricter one you can test as well. A 10 line well config is a smaller one. Pick one, make it a test the output has to pass, and stop playing whack a mole. Constrain the writer and you free the reader.
